## Facts used in the page, checked 2026-10-20 / 2026-10-21
- Hipcamp App Store AU copy: book "camping, glamping, and caravan experiences on farms, stations, vineyards, bushland acreage, and other private land across Australia"; "more than 7 million nights"; community photos and reviews (https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=1440066037&country=au).
- Hipcamp service fee: help centre (support.hipcamp.com, "What is the Hipcamp service fee?") says the fee is shown on the checkout page before booking; the help centre blocks automated fetches (403), so this rests on a search extract. A secondary news source gives 10 to 15 per cent; not used.
- Hipcamp March 2026 rig type and access label update: OVR, 14 March 2026 (see caravan facts file).

- NSW National Parks app: "over 225 NSW national parks", downloadable maps usable without reception, places to stay (lookup id 1213696400); Book Now (run 1 facts).

- Anycamp: last release 2024-04-08 (lookup id 1343507387).
- Airbnb 4.7 (79,452), Stayz & Bookabach 4.7 (36,374): rural cabins and farm stays (general; not ranked).
