## Official sources
- CMCA RV Friendly Town program definition: "an initiative aimed at assisting recreational vehicle consumers". "An RV Friendly Town is one that has met a set of guidelines to ensure they provide a certain amount of amenities, and a certain level of services". Essential criteria: parking in the town centre near a general shopping area; "short term, low cost overnight parking (24/48 hours) for self-contained recreational vehicles"; "Access to potable water"; "Access to a free dump point". Desirable: long-term parking, medical or emergency access, pharmacy, visitor information centre with parking. https://gov.cmca.au/content/rv-friendly-town-program (checked 2026-10-20)
- CMCA RV Friendly Destination: "assist small towns that are not able to meet the criteria of the CMCA RV Friendly Town program". Essential: short-term, low-cost overnight parking (24/48 hours) for self-contained RVs; "solid, level surface"; "enough room for large vehicles to manoeuvre"; campgrounds "well maintained" with facilities such as a BBQ area or covered seating. Desirable: dump point, potable water, long-term parking. https://gov.cmca.au/content/rv-friendly-destination-program (checked 2026-10-20)
- CMCA overview of the programs and RV Friendly map: https://gov.cmca.au/ ; https://rvfriendly.cmca.au/ (checked 2026-10-20). Note: RV Friendly is about self-contained RVs and overnight parking; the pages do not set a big-rig or caravan length rule (stated wording only).
- NSW Government, Towing a trailer or caravan: owner's manual limits "should not be exceeded"; loaded trailer mass must not exceed the lesser of towbar and coupling rating, vehicle towing capacity, trailer carrying capacity and tyre capacity; brakes by gross trailer mass: 0 to 750 kg none required, 751 to 2000 kg efficient braking on both wheels of at least one axle, 2001 to 4500 kg on all wheels, over 2.0 tonnes an automatic breakaway system; about "5-10 per cent of the total mass of the trailer plus load should be supported by the vehicle through the coupling". Page shows no last-updated date, only "Copyright 2026". https://www.nsw.gov.au/driving-boating-and-transport/driving-nsw/roads-safety-and-rules/vehicle-safety-and-compliance/towing-a-caravan (checked 2026-10-20)
- Queensland Government, Towing vehicles and trailers: owner's manual "usually indicates the maximum weight and other trailer features appropriate for the vehicle"; may tow a trailer whose ATM exceeds the vehicle's rated towing capacity "provided" the loaded trailer does not exceed that rating. Page last updated 30 May 2022 (older, use as a secondary link). https://www.qld.gov.au/transport/vehicle-safety/towing/towing-vehicles-and-trailers (checked 2026-10-20)
- NHVR: no light-vehicle caravan towing page found (NHVR content is heavy-vehicle focused); do not cite NHVR for caravan towing. Transport for NSW caravans page https://www.transport.nsw.gov.au/roadsafety/road-users/drivers/caravans returned 403 to our fetch, so its GCM wording ("Legally, you must not exceed the specified GCM") is from a search snippet only: unverified directly.
