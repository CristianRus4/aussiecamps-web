### Official rules

Note: immi.homeaffairs.gov.au pages were fetched with curl (content embedded in the page); ato.gov.au and fairwork.gov.au blocked direct fetching (403/timeouts), so ATO and Fair Work lines come from search-result extracts of those official pages. Re-confirm the ATO and Fair Work wording by eye before publishing.

1. Specified work for a second and third WHV
- 417 second visa: at least 3 months of specified work, "a minimum period of 88 calendar days, including weekends or equivalent rest days"; third visa: at least 6 months, "a minimum period of 179 calendar days", on or after 1 July 2019. The same wording applies to 462. You must work the same number of normal work days or shifts as a full-time employee would in that period; part-time and piecework can add up; a single calendar day cannot count as more than one day. (https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/work-holiday-417/specified-work, checked 2026-10-20)
- 417 industries and areas: tourism and hospitality in Northern or Remote and Very Remote Australia (from 22 June 2021); plant and animal cultivation, fishing and pearling, tree farming and felling, mining, and construction in regional Australia; bushfire recovery work after 31 July 2019 in declared areas (new postcodes added 5 April 2025); recovery work in flood, cyclone or severe-weather areas after 31 December 2021 (paid or volunteer, applications lodged on or after 5 April 2025 or undecided then); critical COVID-19 healthcare work after 31 January 2020. Work must be paid under Australian law and awards. Eligible postcodes are listed on the same page. Secondary processing (winemaking, brewing, milling, small goods) and general garden maintenance do not count. The department is "temporarily applying flexibility" so work that supports a specified industry (such as cleaning or administration) in an eligible postcode may be accepted. (https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/work-holiday-417/specified-work, checked 2026-10-20)
- 462: same 3 and 6 month rule, narrower areas: plant and animal cultivation and construction in Northern Australia and other specified regional areas; fishing and pearling and tree farming and felling in northern Australia only; same tourism/hospitality, bushfire, disaster and COVID-19 categories. (https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/work-holiday-462/specified-462-work, checked 2026-10-20)
- UK passport holders: no specified work needed for a second or third 417 if they lodge on or after 1 July 2024 using a UK passport. (https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/work-holiday-417/third-working-holiday-417, checked 2026-10-20)
- Other 2026 items on the Home Affairs pages: lower second-462 fee for eligible Papua New Guinea passport holders from 1 July 2026 (https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/work-holiday-462/second-work-holiday-462); processing for most WHM applications "expected to be around three months" (417 second visa page). Fees AUD 840 first visa from 1 July 2026 and AUD 1,000 for second/third are from a secondary source only (https://visasnews.com/en/australia-announces-reforms-to-working-holiday-and-visitor-visas/), unverified officially.
- RECENT ANNOUNCEMENT, NOT IN FORCE (17 September 2026, Home Affairs Minister Tony Burke): second and third WHM visas to be allocated by ballot, capped at 45,000 places for the second year (about 57,000 qualified last year) and 5,000 for the third (about 31,000 last year). Applicants still need the 88 days / six months of regional work first; UK applicants excluded under the UK FTA. No commencement date published; sources disagree on whether regulation or legislation is needed, and the Home Affairs WHM news page did not carry it when checked. Sources: https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-17/labor-immigration-crackdown-students-backpackers-overstay-visa/107164354, https://visasnews.com/en/australia-announces-reforms-to-working-holiday-and-visitor-visas/, https://www.workvisalawyers.com.au/news/australia-s-working-holiday-visa-ballots-will-backpackers-still-do-regional-work/ (checked 2026-10-20). Official wording not found: unverified. Write the article as "announced, not yet in force" and link the Home Affairs page.
- One ABC article claims applications from 24 of 29 countries were paused before the announcement (https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-19/ag-industry-warns-food-insecurity-working-holiday-maker-change/107168562); not confirmed on a Home Affairs page, unverified.

2. Tax file number and WHM tax rate
- TFN: apply online with the ATO's Individual Auto-Registration (IAR) once you are in Australia with a valid work visa (417 and 462 are eligible); no cost, about 20 minutes; you need passport number, country of issue (must match the visa), name and date of birth as printed. Visas carrying condition 8101, 8103 or 8116 (no work) cannot use IAR. (https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/tax-file-number/apply-for-a-tfn/foreign-passport-holders-permanent-migrants-and-temporary-visitors-tfn-application and https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/coming-to-australia-or-going-overseas/coming-to-australia/working-holiday-makers, via search extract, checked 2026-10-20)
- Without a TFN the employer must withhold 45% (https://www.ato.gov.au/tax-rates-and-codes/schedule-15-tax-table-for-working-holiday-makers, search extract).
- WHM tax rates 2025-26: 15% on $0 to $45,000; 30% on $45,001 to $135,000; 37% on $135,001 to $190,000; 45% above $190,000. The 2026-27 Schedule 15 table shows the same bands and rates. The 15% rate applies only if the employer is registered with the ATO as an employer of WHMs; unregistered employers withhold 30% up to $135,000. (https://www.ato.gov.au/tax-rates-and-codes/tax-rates-working-holiday-makers and https://www.ato.gov.au/tax-rates-and-codes/schedule-15-tax-table-for-working-holiday-makers, via search extract, checked 2026-10-20)
- Note: an older $37,000 / $87,000 table is outdated and still turns up in cached results.

3. DASP (Departing Australia superannuation payment)
- WHM DASP rate is 65% of the taxable component (taxed and untaxed elements), versus 35% and 45% for other temporary residents; the tax-free component is 0%. The 65% applies to anyone who held a 417 or 462 visa, to the whole payment, for payments on or after 1 July 2017. You can claim only after leaving Australia and once your visa has expired; the tax is withheld and the payment is not included in your tax return. (https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/super-for-individuals-and-families/super/temporary-residents-and-superannuation/departing-australia-superannuation-payment-dasp, via search extract, checked 2026-10-20)

4. Fair Work
- Visa holders and migrant workers have the same workplace rights and protections as all other employees, including when they breach visa conditions (FWO can still help recover minimum entitlements). (https://www.fairwork.gov.au/find-help-for/visa-holders-migrants and https://www.fairwork.gov.au/tools-and-resources/fact-sheets/rights-and-obligations/visa-holders-and-migrant-workers-workplace-rights-and-entitlements, via search extract, checked 2026-10-20)
- National Minimum Wage $26.44 per hour from 1 July 2026 (same search extract; confirm on the FWO pay page).
- Horticulture Award piecework: minimum hourly wage guarantee since the first pay period on or after 28 April 2022: for each day worked a pieceworker must be paid at least their hourly rate times hours worked that day. Piece rates must also be set so an average competent pieceworker earns at least 15% more per hour than the minimum hourly rate (casual loading included for casuals). FWO example from 1 July 2026: Level 1 adult $25.74 an hour, casual $32.18. (https://www.fairwork.gov.au/newsroom/news/new-minimum-hourly-wage-guarantee-pieceworkers, https://horticulture.fairwork.gov.au/pay-piecework-rates, checked 2026-10-20 via search extract)
- Record My Hours: free, no registration, on the App Store and Google Play; records shifts automatically by location or manually; stores photos of payslips; exports a zip with timesheets; does not replace the employer's record-keeping duties. Data from installs before May 2024 (Android) or November 2024 (iOS) does not migrate to the current version. (https://www.fairwork.gov.au/tools-and-resources/record-my-hours-app, checked 2026-10-20 via search extract)

5. Visa conditions: 6 months with one employer (condition 8547)
- WHMs usually can work for the same employer for 6 months only; the 6 months runs from the first day of work and includes part-time, casual and voluntary work; it resets when a second WHM visa is granted or a bridging visa takes effect. No permission needed for up to 12 months with the same employer if you work in different locations (no one location over 6 months), or in plant and animal cultivation anywhere in Australia, or in certain industries in northern Australia (fishing and pearling, tree farming and felling, construction, mining; per the search extract of the Home Affairs page, which says older material also listed aged care and disability services, so confirm). Otherwise request permission (online form for condition 8547); grounds include awaiting a visa that allows ongoing full-time work, or work critical to the employer with a supporting letter. (https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/work-holiday-417/6-month-work-limitation and https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/what-we-do/whm-program/specified-work-conditions/WHM-condition-8547-permission-request-form, checked 2026-10-20)
- Content review (2026-10-20) read the condition 8547 page: you can work for the same employer for "more than 6 months without asking permission" in these cases (not "up to 12 months"), and the exceptions include critical sectors anywhere in Australia: agriculture, food processing, health, aged and disability care, childcare, and tourism and hospitality. The page now uses that wording.
- No 2026 change to this rule found.

6. myVEVO
- Home Affairs says you can check your WHM visa details and conditions in your grant letter, on VEVO online, or with the free myVEVO app, including the six-month employer limit and exemptions; employers verify work rights through VEVO with the visa holder's permission. (https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/employing-and-sponsoring-someone/migrant-worker-protections/information-for-migrant-workers and https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/already-have-a-visa/check-visa-details-and-conditions/check-conditions-online, via search extract, checked 2026-10-20)
- App Store text: send visa details by email to an employer (https://apps.apple.com/au/app/myvevo/id988252820).
