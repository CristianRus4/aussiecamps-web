## Feature research (sonnet pass, 2026-10-20)
(Partial 1 of several. App Store listing text is the developer's own copy: https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=<ID>&country=au, checked 2026-10-20. toogoodtogo.com blocks automated fetches with a Vercel checkpoint, so TGTG facts come from its App Store listing and an excerpt of its marketplace page.)

### My 7-Eleven (Fuel Price Lock)
- App Store copy: "FUEL PRICE LOCK: You could save up to 25c per litre*. Search the closest 7-Eleven fuel stores to find our best local fuel price on Mobil fuel. Lock it in for 7 days and fill up any 7-Eleven. *T&Cs apply." Also app-exclusive deals, "free reward on your 7th visit", Velocity points via linked My Card (https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=1032594453&country=au, checked 2026-10-20).
- Terms page still live, headed "Last updated: February 2024": up to 150 L per redemption (extra litres at pump price); discount capped at 25c/L; lock expires exactly 7 days after activation; app checks up to five closest 7-Eleven stores within 250 km; redeem at any 7-Eleven selling that fuel (https://www.7eleven.com.au/fuel-price-lock-terms, checked 2026-10-20).
- Needs: 7-Eleven app with location on, a 7-Eleven Account and My Card scanned at the counter before paying (same terms page, checked 2026-10-20). Needs no Australian bank or address stated; account sign-up details unverified.
- Catches: one unredeemed lock at a time; max two locks per 24 hours; other fuel offers void once a lock is activated; the "Best Local Price" is "not live" and "does not compare other retailers' prices" (same terms page, checked 2026-10-20).
- 2026 status: feature still in the current App Store copy (v2.99.0, 2026-10-13) and terms page; no 2026 change announcements found. Mobil fuel only per the app copy.
- Android: unverified (not checked).
- Fair con: it only compares 7-Eleven's own stores, so a cheaper servo from another brand is not shown (terms page above).

### Too Good To Go
- What it does: buy "Surprise Bags" of unsold food from cafes, bakeries, restaurants and grocery stores; "Prices start from as low as $6.99"; collect at a pre-set time; free to download; no IAP (https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=1060683933&country=au, checked 2026-10-20).
- How bags are priced: businesses set the price. Secondary report: each bag must be worth at least three times the price (bakingbusiness.com.au, https://bakingbusiness.com.au/food-app-too-good-to-go-saves-waste-while-surprising-consumers/, checked 2026-10-20). TGTG AU marketplace page excerpt gave a different "50-75%" discount wording; wording unverified, so state "a fraction of retail value" only (https://www.toogoodtogo.com/en-au/surplus-food-marketplace, excerpt, checked 2026-10-20).
- Coverage: launched Melbourne, then Sydney (https://www.toogoodtogo.com/en-au/press/melbourne-launch; https://convenienceworldmagazine.com.au/?p=33545, checked 2026-10-20). Current city list unverified; regional and outback coverage unverified (likely thin).
- Needs: account; payment in app; AU phone or bank requirement unverified.
- Android: unverified here.
- Fair con: contents are a surprise and collection windows are fixed, which suits a traveller in a city with a fixed stay but not a road trip through the bush (windows and surprise from the App Store copy above).

### EatClub
- What it does: "Save up to 50% off the bill at great restaurants near you"; claim a deal, pay with the EatClub digital card, savings applied automatically; "No booking fees or vouchers"; deals "when they have spare tables to fill" (https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=1053752571&country=au, checked 2026-10-20).
- Catches: site says the digital card is a prepaid reloadable Mastercard issued by Vault Payment Solutions Group Pty Ltd; Apple Pay supported; offers include 20% to 50% off, including drinks (https://www.eatclub.com.au, checked 2026-10-20). AU App Store lists optional subscription: Monthly A$11.90, Yearly A$119.00 (existing facts above); what the subscription adds unverified.
- Cities: site lists venues in VIC (about 2,257 shown), plus NSW, SA, QLD, WA, ACT filters, "7,074 venues" overall (https://www.eatclub.com.au, checked 2026-10-20). App copy: "10,000+ restaurants across Australia, the UK and New Zealand".
- Needs: ID/address/phone checks for the card unverified.
- Android: unverified.
- Fair con: it is a dine-in city tool; deals depend on a venue having spare tables, so choices vary by time (App Store copy).

### ShopBack
- What it does: cashback up to 30% online; partners listed include Booking.com, Agoda, Expedia, Wotif, Flight Centre, Coles, Woolworths, gift cards at Ampol, Uber Eats (https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=1086505626&country=au, checked 2026-10-20).
- Payout: withdrawals "free"; typical payout "Within 10 working days"; main method bank transfer to an "Australian-issued AUD bank account", PayPal also listed; "a small Confirmed-Cashback threshold" and a per-day cap shown on the withdrawal screen (https://www.shopback.com.au/guide/how-to/withdraw-shopback-cashback, checked 2026-10-20). Only Confirmed cashback can be withdrawn; pending waits for the retailer to confirm (ShopBack AU help excerpt via search, https://support.shopback.com.au/hc/en-gb/articles/360039161474-Withdrawing-your-Cashback, page itself returned 403, checked 2026-10-20).
- Needs: Australian bank account or PayPal for payout; a visitor without either cannot cash out (same guide). Android: unverified.
- Fair con: cashback arrives weeks after the purchase and often only on online bookings, not on road costs like fuel or camp fees.

### TopCashback AU
- What it does: cashback via retailer commission; "completely free to join and completely free to use"; travel retailers listed incl. Trip.com, Agoda, Expedia, Virgin Australia Flights; paid to bank account or PayPal "with no minimum amount"; cashback usually shows within 7 days but is withdrawable only after the retailer's returns period ends (https://www.topcashback.com.au/, checked 2026-10-20).
- Payout methods: BECS bank transfer or PayPal; one payout type per 24 hours; some merchants restrict payout type (https://topcashback.com.au/help/how-do-i-receive-my-cashback-payout, checked 2026-10-20).
- App: id 1619068287, free, 4.69 from 1,936 AU ratings, v16.0.4, 2026-10-12 (https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=1619068287&country=au, checked 2026-10-20). Store copy: "over 1000 retailers", "Receive payout earnings via bank transfer or Paypal". Android: site says Google Play exists (https://www.topcashback.com.au/, checked 2026-10-20).
- Fair con: payout needs an Australian bank (BECS) or PayPal, and delay until the returns period ends.

### WiseList
- Copy: compares Coles, Woolworths and ALDI as you add each item; Watch List alerts; Rare Specials; Fridge and Pantry; receipt scanning (https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=1454099261&country=au, checked 2026-10-20). Paid "WiseList+" (A$7.99 monthly or A$49.99 yearly per existing facts) adds Split by Store, barcode scan and compare, cart transfer to Coles or Woolworths online cart, invites to shared lists, expiry alerts. App has ads.
- 400,000+ Australians (developer claim). Publishes own monthly price test at wiselist.app/how-we-measure (not yet fetched).
- Fair con: the useful trip features (Scan and Compare, Split by Store) sit behind WiseList+; needs ads tolerance on free tier.

### Wise (multi-currency account and card)
- Copy: hold 40 currencies; debit card for 160 countries; "Australian account details"; "real mid-market exchange rate"; products "depend on where you live" (https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=612261027&country=au, checked 2026-10-20).
- AU pricing page: registering is free; card order A$10; first A$400 per month in ATM withdrawals free, then 2.69% on the amount above; paying by card in the account's currency is free incl. abroad; conversion fee "varies by currency" starting from 0.19%; holding balances free; receiving domestic AUD payments free (https://wise.com/au/pricing/, checked 2026-10-20). The page gives no separate fee table for visitors.
- Eligibility for a tourist with no Australian address: unverified. The Wise help text found only says check "Terms of Use and product availability for your region" (https://wise.com/au/blog/australia-bank-account-non-resident, checked 2026-10-20). Third-party guides say proof of ID and Australian address are required (not an official source). Best framing: a visitor most likely opens an account in their home country, so the AU page is more for people living in Australia.
- Fair con: the debit card has a A$10 order fee and takes time to arrive; cash withdrawals above A$400 a month cost 2.69%.
