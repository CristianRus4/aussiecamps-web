# Alternatives to ExplorOz (Traveller / EOTopo) for outback and remote travel: facts (run 3)

All facts checked 2026-10-21 unless a different date is shown. "AU" = App Store Australia (`https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=<ID>&country=au`, AU page `https://apps.apple.com/au/app/id<ID>`). Unconfirmed items are marked "unverified". Prices A$.

## Framing rule for AussieCamps (ours)
AussieCamps has NO offline map. Map tiles, routing, weather and photos need signal; place details and guides stored on the phone work offline (App Store text: "Live maps, directions, forecasts and external links still need a connection"). For offline navigation in remote country, ExplorOz, Hema, Newtracs and others are the better choice. AussieCamps is a companion for deciding where to stop (rest areas, camps, dump points, water). Pricing wording: "free to download, with in-app purchases". iPhone only.

## ExplorOz today (primary source: exploroz.com)
- Products (site nav, https://www.exploroz.com/): Traveller (app), EOTopo (map), Tracker, Membership, plus website Places, Routes, Track Logs, Forum. "Plan, Track and Explore Australia".
- Traveller app: https://apps.apple.com/au/app/exploroz-traveller/id1116148680, I.T. Beyond Pty Ltd. A$59.99 upfront (one-time, "platform specific", Family Sharing). iPhone and iPad, iOS 14 or later; Android 10 or later bought separately (https://www.exploroz.com/traveller). Play: 4.34 (Play page 2026-10-21; 963 ratings as of 2026-10-20), price 59.99.
- Rating AU: 4.32 from 1,353 ratings (lookup 2026-10-21). Version 10.4.0, 2026-07-16.
- What the base app includes (developer page): "Australia-wide offline overview maps with offline GPS tracking and trip planning"; full content download of "100,000+ Places and 190+ Routes"; online EOTopo and Satellite/Street viewing with a monthly tile allowance (5,000 EOTopo and 1,000 Satellite/Street tiles); free ExplorOz account to sync places, routes and trips.
- Full offline map (in-app purchase, AU page and developer page): ExplorOz Pro Access A$99.99 a year (latest EOTopo editions offline, unlimited online EOTopo, Membership features: Web App, Tracker, GPX export); EOTopo Annual Access A$59.99 a year (maps only); EOTopo 2026 4-Year Access A$79.99 one-time (single edition offline until 2030, no updates). Tile Packs A$14.99 and A$49.99. App Store: "EOTopo maps are required for offline use and purchased in-app". NOTE nuance: the developer page says the base app includes the low-resolution overview map offline, and that full high-detail offline needs one of the three options; the App Store says the maps are required for offline use. Describe both.
- EOTopo: ExplorOz's own vector topographic map of Australia: 10 m contours, roads and 4WD tracks, hiking and MTB trails, national parks and access information, "Remote tracks often missing or poorly represented in mainstream maps" (https://www.exploroz.com/eotopo). "Offline EOTopo maps are not metered" (Traveller page).
- Community: places "publicly editable with change history"; reviews, photos and updates from travellers; featured Routes such as Canning Stock Route (2006 km), Simpson Desert French Line (439 km), Oodnadatta Track (673 km), Great Central Road (1147 km) (https://www.exploroz.com/).
- Navigation and trip planning: "Navigation, trip planning and GPS tracking"; routing parameters (width, height, weight) since 8.0 (checked 2026-10-20). Not supported directly in CarPlay or Android Auto (Traveller page).
- Membership (A$49.95 a year, shop page https://www.exploroz.com/shop/exploroz+membership): Web App for PC or Mac planning, Tracker sync and sharing, GPX export and storage, unlimited online EOTopo, blogs, priority support. Does not include offline maps (https://www.exploroz.com/membership). Tracker: standalone app ExplorOz Tracker (id 1499300785, free, 4.48 from 104 AU ratings) needs a Membership to record and save trips (https://www.exploroz.com/tracker).
- Strong: offline Australia-wide topographic map, tracks, remote routes, GPS tracking, 100,000+ places. Cons: most expensive in the set once full offline maps are added (A$59.99 plus A$59.99 to A$99.99); not a caravan-park-first directory; no caravan-size filter mentioned on the product page.
- Still the better choice when: you need offline topo maps, track navigation and your own routing in remote country.

## Alternatives

### Hema 4WD Maps (and Hema's other products)
- https://apps.apple.com/au/app/4wd-maps-offline-topo-maps/id465639977, Hema Maps Pty Ltd (copyright also Memory-Map Inc). A$99.99. 3.88 from 1,787. LAST UPDATE v2.4.2, 2022-09-07, minimum iOS 11.4. Hema's own shop still lists "4WD Maps iOS App" A$99.99 (https://hemamaps.com/pages/hema-apps-comparison).
- Store text: "Best value Hema App. The one-time cost includes all Hema 4WD maps, without any recurring subscriptions"; "NAVIGATE OFFLINE... Australia-wide map coverage"; plan routes, waypoints, record tracks; preloaded Hema touring and 4WD maps (Simpson Desert, Kimberley, Cape York and more). In-app purchases on the AU page: Victoria, SA, NSW, QLD and Tasmania Topo maps A$99.99 each; Import 3rd Party Maps A$29.99.
- Retired: HX-1 / Hema Explorer Cloud (https://kb.hemamaps.com/hx1-hema-cloud-due-to-retire). A 2024 Hema post mentions a "4X4 Explorer App" with a Premium Map subscription "normally $49.99 annually" (https://hemamaps.com/blogs/hema-news/hema-explorer-cloud-retired-what-s-next); I could not find it in the AU App Store (unverified whether it is current). Hema's navigators and Hema Nav are hardware or platform products, not covered here.
- Best for: Hema map fans who want a one-off paid app. Cons: stale update date, A$99.99 price, state maps sold separately. Say "last updated September 2022" when quoting.

### Newtracs (4WD tracks)
- https://apps.apple.com/au/app/newtracs/id1456718442, Newtracs Trading Pty Ltd. Free; IAP Monthly Premium A$19.99, Annual Premium A$129.99 (AU page). 4.74 from 8,237. v3.1.1, 2026-09-15. iOS 17.0+. Android: Play 4.66 (checked 2026-10-21).
- Store text: 13,000+ assessed 4x4 trails and 8,000+ campsites, parks, towns and places searchable offline; offline vector maps; crowdsourced track ratings; Victorian track closures (daily feed); snow cover layer; topographic detail on Premium.
- Best for: 4WD track conditions and offline track search. Con: focused on 4WD tracks, not a general camp directory.

### Goin' Bush (4WD navigation)
- https://apps.apple.com/au/app/goin-bush/id6745422236, GOINBUSH 4X4 PTY LTD. Free; Off-Grid A$8.99 a month, A$49.99 a year or A$89.99 lifetime (AU page). 4.87 from 287. v2.0.5, 2026-06-12. Store text: "True offline navigation... works completely without signal" on the paid tier; free tier route planning needs internet. Few ratings; mention briefly or skip.

### WikiCamps Australia
- A$9.99 one-off (iOS and Android bought separately). 4.5 from 16,662. v6.3.0, 2026-10-19. Ads. User-contributed, "Over 65K sites listed". Site: "You can explore sites and your trip plans even when you have no phone reception with Offline Mode." Earlier-checked help: up to three trips offline, straight-line offline distances. Source https://www.wikicamps.com.au/ and notes in au-app-facts.md. Not a topographic map app.

### CAMPS (Camps Australia Wide)
- Free to download, 7-day trial then A$14.99 a year. 4.45 from 3,478. Per-state offline maps with site data: "If you have not yet downloaded the maps for that state, the app will revert to the basic offline maps with limited zoom functionality" (https://www.campsaustraliawide.com/help-centre/how-do-i-get-offline-maps-sites/). Team-checked sites, 4WD-only and dry-weather notes, mobile coverage detail on site pages (https://www.campsaustraliawide.com/app/). Not a track-navigation or topographic app.

### AussieCamps (ours)
- https://apps.apple.com/au/app/id6801098680. Free to download, with in-app purchases. iPhone only, iOS 26+. No AU rating yet. v2026.9.6.
- Useful in remote country for: rest areas, dump points, drinking water, toilets and national park campgrounds from state park agencies and road authorities, with details that stay readable offline; trips with ordered stops and leg times (computed via Apple Maps, needs signal when built); road trips such as Gibb River Road, Nullarbor, Queensland Outback Way and Red Centre Way as guides.
- Not a replacement for an offline topographic map or track navigation. Counts (product-facts.json, 2026-09-09): 5,552 rest areas, 1,838 campgrounds, 1,393 dump points, 3,004 drinking water points. Do not use the store's "75,000+ places", "21 road trips", "50+ guides" (conflicts with the app bundle, which has 20 road trips and 44 articles).

### Free general offline map apps
- Google Maps: offline maps "expire and can auto-update; offline driving only within the downloaded area; no traffic offline" (https://support.google.com/maps/answer/6291838, 2026-10-20). Free. 4.64 from 501,802 AU ratings.
- Apple Maps offline maps with turn-by-turn driving directions (https://support.apple.com/en-au/guide/iphone/iphcfb5f5bc6/ios, 2026-10-20); not available in all regions.
- Organic Maps (https://apps.apple.com/au/app/organic-maps-offline-maps/id1567437057): free, OpenStreetMap-based, "100% offline", turn-by-turn, no ads, 4.73 from 568, v2026.09.29. The listing does not mention 4WD track grading or Australian remote-track detail (unverified either way; do not claim it lacks them). 
- Avenza Maps (id 388424049, free, 4.66 from 23,439): offline map store plus import of your own PDF or GeoTIFF maps; its listing names mostly US publishers; Australian map availability unverified.

## Which to pick (fact-based summary)
- Offline topo and tracks: ExplorOz, Hema 4WD Maps, Newtracs. Offline camp data: CAMPS, WikiCamps, CamperMate (App Store: offline maps and info, no expiration). Stop planning on iPhone with place details offline: AussieCamps.
- Combination that fits the facts: an offline map app plus a place directory.
