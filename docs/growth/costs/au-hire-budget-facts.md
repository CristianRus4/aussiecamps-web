# Australia campervan hire and working-holiday budget: facts file

Read on 2026-10-21 (all "read" dates below are this date unless stated). Prices AUD. Only facts seen on the cited page are given; anything not shown is marked NOT PUBLISHED or UNVERIFIED. No figure here is estimated.

Key structural finding: Maui, Britz, Apollo, Mighty (the site hippiecamper.com now redirects to mightycampers.com) and Cheapa Campa are all thl-group brands. They share one Australian FAQ (same wording on maui-rentals.com/faqs and apollocamper.com/faqs), and none of them publishes a daily rate. Prices only appear after entering dates in the booking engine. Do not quote a thl "from" daily rate.

Other fees: Hobart pick-up AU$125 ($130 from 1 Apr 2027); Broome location fee AU$900 ($930); public-holiday pick-up/drop-off AU$125 ($129).

Visa/Mastercard (credit or debit) or Amex credit.

- Packs and extras (Apollo extras page, https://www.apollocamper.com/travel-info/extras, travel 1 Apr 2026-31 Mar 2027; "$" with no currency code on the page): camping chairs $24 each, table $33, fan/heater $21, esky $29, RV Essentials Kit $155 (HiTop/Endeavour/Euro Mini/Tourer/Plus) or $205 (Euro Quest/Camper/Deluxe), Linen Exchange Kit $42, Drop and Go Pack $145, Re-fuelling Pack $340 (2WD), Windscreen and tyre protection $16/day, Extended roadside assistance $9/day, child/booster seat $46 each.
- Cleaning penalties (FAQ): toilet/waste tank not empty $299 incl. GST per tank; pet cleaning $325 incl. GST.
- Airport/after-hours (FAQ): no early or late pick-ups; "Staying nearby and collecting the next morning is advised, with no refund." Returns no later than one hour before closing. Branches are not airport counters; branch-to-airport distances given (Perth 2.2 km to Melbourne 17.2 km). Airport transfer fee: NOT PUBLISHED for Australia.
- GST: "Goods and Services tax (GST) is included in the daily gross rental rate." (FAQ)
- Unsealed roads (FAQ): 2WD "cannot be driven on unsealed roads", except well-maintained access roads under 12 km to recognised campgrounds or major attractions; over 12 km and beaches not permitted. 4WD: remote tracks need branch permission; some tracks and all beaches not permitted. Cover void on breach: "Any breach of the exclusions or restrictions listed in the rental agreement will render all cover void" (Mighty page).
- Cancellation (FAQ): 61+ days no fee; 60-31 days $250; 30-7 days 20% (min $250); 6 days or less 50% (min $250); no-show 100% (min $250).

## PART 2: WORKING HOLIDAY ROAD TRIP BUDGET

### 2a Home Affairs (WHM 417/462)
Home Affairs visa-listing pages (https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/work-holiday-417 and ...-462) returned HTTP 403 to the fetch tool and the headless browser, and the curl copy loads content by script, so the funds figure, visa application charge and eligibility text could NOT be read from those pages. Facts read from Home Affairs pages that did load:
- 6-month limit, condition 8547 (https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/work-holiday-417/6-month-work-limitation, read 2026-10-21): "you can only work for the same employer for a maximum of 6 months while holding your WHM visa." Exemptions (from 1 January 2024): different locations for the same employer (work at any one location not more than 6 months); plant and animal cultivation anywhere in Australia; natural disaster recovery work; five critical sectors anywhere in Australia (agriculture, food processing, and others listed on the page); some industries in Northern Australia only (fishing and pearling, tree farming/felling, construction, mining). "The 6 months start again with the grant of a new WHM visa."
- Work conditions page (https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/programs-subsite/Pages/working-holiday-maker/work-conditions.aspx): condition 8548 limits study to 4 months. A second or third WHM visa needs specified work; UK passport holders applying on or after 1 July 2024 do not.
- Specified work length (3 months for second, 6 months for third visa), age 18-30 (35 for some countries; Cyprus, Finland, Germany, Korea added from 1 July 2026) come from search-result excerpts of Home Affairs pages, not read in full: treat as indicative.
- Funds: Australian Embassy Tokyo FAQ (https://japan.embassy.gov.au/tkyo/info_faq.html, Australian government, via search excerpt) says "usually about AUD5,000" for the initial stay plus the fare onward. Not from Home Affairs; UNVERIFIED for 2026.
- Visa application charge: secondary sources only (workvisalawyers.com.au, visasnews.com etc.) report the 417/462 first-application charge rose from $670 to $840 from 1 July 2026; subsequent applications $1,000. UNVERIFIED against Home Affairs; do not publish without checking the Home Affairs Visa Pricing Estimator.
