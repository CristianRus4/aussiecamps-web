# Camping websites, 2-weekly check: 2026-11-18

The same report is in both repos. Raw data: `docs/growth/data/2026-11-18/` in each repo.

Data windows: Search Console 19 Oct to 15 Nov (28 days) and the last 3 months; GA4 21 Oct to 17 Nov; App Store 16 Oct to 12 Nov (Apple lags 4 to 6 days, so the 12 Nov conversion work and the search surge are barely in it).

## Verdict

- **campingapp.nz: good.** Search clicks are 4 times what they were a month ago, App Store clicks are up 70%, and the place pages and new guides are being indexed. Installs from the site are flat so far, but Apple's data stops at 12 Nov.
- **aussiecamps.app: good on search, still tiny on installs.** 28-day clicks are 19 times the 28 days before, and the 3-month total is already double the 11 Nov baseline. The site still produced only 19 downloads in the 28 days to 12 Nov.

**Why.** The growth is broad, not one page:
- **Translated guides bring most of the new clicks.** AussieCamps 675 clicks from 687 translated pages; KiwiCamping 1,156 from 625. Taiwan, Japan, Hong Kong and Germany follow the home country in clicks on both sites.
- **English guides:** AussieCamps 177 clicks, KiwiCamping 344.
- **Tools:** high impressions and few clicks. The drive-time calculator had 37.4k impressions and 96 clicks on AussieCamps (0.26% CTR), and 15.8k impressions and 17 clicks on KiwiCamping.
- **Place pages are only starting to show.** AussieCamps 35 pages, 9 clicks, 506 impressions; KiwiCamping 106 pages, 36 clicks, 4.6k impressions.

**Installs per page (checklist line 7): not possible yet.** No campaign token and no referrer reaches Apple's 5-user threshold on either app, so Apple reports none of them. The GA4 clicks by token above are the best proxy until volumes grow. The other option is App Store Connect's own Campaigns screen, which shows tokens from 5 downloads.

## Kill criteria and things to watch

- **No page that had clicks has lost impressions.**
- **Watch the AussieCamps dump-point hubs.** They were retired on 12 Nov with a redirect to the state camping pages, which don't cover dump points. They still had 33 clicks and 2.03k impressions in 28 days, at about 60 impressions a day both before and after 12 Nov (data to 15 Nov). The 14-day window ends 26 Nov. If their queries ("dump point", "dump point near me", "dump points victoria") fall by more than 30% without moving to another page, that is the rollback case, and it is Cristian's call.
- **The noindex validations show Failed on both sites,** for 421 and 500 pages. Those pages are noindex on purpose, so this is expected.
