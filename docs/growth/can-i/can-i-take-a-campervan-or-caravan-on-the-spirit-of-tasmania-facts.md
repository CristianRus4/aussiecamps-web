# Facts: can-i-take-a-campervan-or-caravan-on-the-spirit-of-tasmania

Checked 2026-10-21. Page answer: Yes, if you declare exact length and height, keep gas off and tagged, and meet Tasmanian biosecurity rules.

## Rules used (all opened 2026-10-21 unless noted)

- Booking: motorhome/campervan booked as a vehicle with the motorhome or campervan option; caravans under "towing a caravan or trailer"; make, model and registration needed. https://spiritoftasmania.com.au/before-you-sail/booking-your-ticket
- Fares depend on vehicle length; sizes confirmed at check-in, extra fee if wrong; "There is no additional cost attached to vehicle height"; "Failure to advise the correct height may result in your passage being denied at check-in". Same page.
- Length includes towed items and attachments (drawbar, bike racks, gas cylinders); height includes towed items, roof racks and roof loads (kayaks, baggage, bicycles). https://www.spiritoftasmania.com.au/before-you-sail/boarding-with-your-vehicle/measure-your-vehicle/ and https://www.spiritoftasmania.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/vechicle-booking-information_june-2023.pdf
- Fare structure: standard vehicles 0.01 to 6.00 m (6.00 to 7.00 m next band); "Maximum total length 6.0 metres" for standard vehicles; towing caravans, camper trailers or motorhomes with a towed unit: 0.01 to 7.00 m band plus a per-extra-metre rate; registration details for towed units needed. https://www.spiritoftasmania.com.au/sailing-fares/booking-information
- Contact centre 1800 634 906, Monday to Saturday 10am to 6pm; contactus@spiritoftasmania.com.au (measure-your-vehicle page). Oversize process is not described on any opened page; the page directs questions to the contact centre.
- Book 10 to 11 months ahead for fixed holiday dates; booking inventory opens about 11 months ahead; best fares for booking forward and return together (vehicle booking information PDF, dated June 2023 but hosted in the current uploads folder).
- Gas (Category 1 dangerous goods): caravan/motorhome/camper one additional bottle up to 25 kg (50 L water capacity); secure, upright, off, in date; AS2468/2469/2470 stamped, inspection within 10 years; LPG systems in caravans and campervans checked, isolated, HAZARD tagged, stay off until vehicle clears port precinct; "gas systems must never be active while the vehicle is on the vessel"; propane cylinder connected to an appliance not permitted; non-compliant cylinders confiscated (or disposed of if expired), badly secured ones collected by staff and returned at the disembarkation port; jerry cans diesel only, up to 50 L; Category 2 cans up to 18 x 250 g (not used on page). https://spiritoftasmania.com.au/terms-and-conditions/carriage-of-dangerous-goods
- Caravan "off limits" during crossing; gas cylinders must be switched off: https://www.spiritoftasmania.com.au/guide/top-tassie-caravanning-tips
- Passengers not permitted on vehicle decks while ship at sea (pet statement); kennels $22 each way, strongly recommended; dogs hydatid treatment within 14 days, tick check, declaration (booking page); cabins include bedding, linen, towels, soap; budget recliners in private lounge: https://www.spiritoftasmania.com.au/contact-support/preparing-for-sailing/
- Check-in opens 1.5 to 2.5 hours before departure, closes 45 minutes before; times indicative; Spirit Quay Devonport, East Devonport; Geelong terminal North Geelong: https://www.spiritoftasmania.com.au/sailing-information/sailing-schedule/ (the live sailing table did not load).
- New ships: media release dated 15 September 2026: inaugural sailings 31 October 2026, Spirit of Tasmania IV from Geelong and V from Devonport; bookings for expanded accommodation opened 16 September; more than 3 times the caravan capacity; pet-friendly cabins for up to two pets; no vehicle-rule change described. https://www.spiritoftasmania.com.au/media-releases/spirit-of-tasmania-adds-new-accommodation-and-capacity-ahead-of-inaugural-sailings/ . Corroborated by search extracts of Tasmanian Government and ABC items (Aug to Oct 2026); the premier.tas.gov.au page returned 403.
- Biosecurity Tasmania, Spirit of Tasmania page: clean vehicles, caravans, trailers of visible soil and mud; clean and dry camping gear; dispose of restricted items (fresh fruit and vegetables, seafood, some animal products, plant material, soil, seeds); honey restricted due to varroa; leave honey and firewood at home if unsure; security asks biosecurity questions at Geelong; Notice to Stop sticker; re-inspection by inspectors at Devonport; random vehicle checks; "Penalties may apply for non-compliance". https://nre.tas.gov.au/biosecurity-tasmania/travellers-guide-to-tasmanian-biosecurity-what-you-can-and-cant-bring-into-tasmania/travelling-to-tasmania-on-the-spirit-of-tasmania
- Food: fresh fruit and vegetables including potatoes, herbs, garlic, ginger, onion not permitted; cooked, canned, preserved, commercially dried allowed; unfiltered honey not permitted (unless Tasmanian, sealed); officer makes final decision (traveller's guide index). https://nre.tas.gov.au/biosecurity-tasmania/travellers-guide-to-tasmanian-biosecurity-what-you-can-and-cant-bring-into-tasmania/food
- AussieCamps: Tasmania 27 campgrounds and 80 dump points (lib/data/product-facts.json, dataAsOf 2026-09-09); filters and trips per au-app-facts.md.

## CONFLICTS

- Fare page lists "Maximum total length 6.0 metres" for standard vehicles and (in the extract) for campervans/motorhomes, but also prices motorhomes and towed units in a 0.01 to 7.00 m band plus a per-metre rate. The page states the contradiction softly; confirm with TT-Line.
- The fare page's campervan height bands (under 2 m, over 2.1 m) leave 2.0 to 2.1 m undefined; not used on the page.
- Existing guide tasmania-camping-loop says fares are set by vehicle length and height; TT-Line says height does not change the price. Not edited.
- Third-party blogs give a 9 kg gas limit; the official policy gives 25 kg (50 L water capacity) as the caravan allowance and 9 kg for open trailers (the extract also shows "9 kg (25 L)").
