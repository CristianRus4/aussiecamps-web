# Facts: can-i-camp-in-a-national-park-without-booking-in-australia (publishAt 2026-11-09)

All pages opened 2026-10-21. These return HTTP 403 to automated fetches (WebFetch and curl): parks.qld.gov.au and mirrors, nt.gov.au, dth.nt.gov.au, parks.act.gov.au. Rows marked "search extract" rest on the official-page text a web search returned for that URL.

## Confirmed rules used
- Queensland (https://www.qld.gov.au/recreation/activities/camping/bookings, opened via WebFetch): "You must book and pay for a camping permit before you arrive at a camping area"; most bookings are online and the permit is emailed. Tag displayed at the site (QPWS camping bookings page https://parks.qld.gov.au/camping/bookings, search extract). The qld.gov.au permits page also says "Before camping in a national park or other protected area you will need to make a booking and pay for a camping permit."
- NSW camping safety (https://www.nationalparks.nsw.gov.au/safety/camping-safety, opened): "You may need to book in advance"; wild or back country camping is permitted in some parks; "You don't need to make a booking for remote camping (campgrounds that are not listed on this website), but we highly recommend you fill in a trip intention form".
- NSW Tuross River campground (https://www.nationalparks.nsw.gov.au/camping-and-accommodation/campgrounds/tuross-river-campground, opened): "Bookings are not required for this campground."
- NSW Brou Lake campground (https://www.nationalparks.nsw.gov.au/camping-and-accommodation/campgrounds/brou-lake-campground, opened): no camping fees but a $6 booking fee applies; book up to 8 people or 2 sites online. Smithers Beach (opened) says "There are no camping fees but a $6 booking fee applies. Bookings are required." NSW camping page: more than 350 campgrounds (https://www.nationalparks.nsw.gov.au/camping-and-accommodation/camping-in-national-parks, opened).
- Victoria Parks Victoria camping page (https://www.parks.vic.gov.au/where-to-stay/camping/free-camping-in-victoria, opened): need to book popular bookable campsites in advance, especially busy periods; check park pages to see which campsites need booking; camping only at your designated site and only by the party that made the booking.
- Victoria Cape Conran (https://www.parks.vic.gov.au/places-to-see/parks/cape-conran-coastal-park/where-to-stay/camping-in-cape-conran-coastal-park, opened): Binn Beach, Gunnai Beach and Pearl Point non-bookable, first come first served; Banksia Bluff bookable.
- SA book and pay FAQs (https://www.parks.sa.gov.au/book-and-pay/frequently-asked-questions, opened): signage "emphasises that online bookings are required for camping"; vehicle entry fee waived with most campground bookings (not Flinders Chase); a park pass skips the vehicle entry booking but "you'll still need to book campsites in advance" and the pass does not cover camping (Desert Parks Pass includes camping in some parks); enter HIRECAR in the registration field for a hire car and edit later. Park pages (Coffin Bay, Innes, opened) say camping fees must be paid before arrival.
- WA camping fees (https://exploreparks.dbca.wa.gov.au/camping-fees, opened, page updated 24 June 2026): online bookings for some of the most popular campgrounds, card paid at booking; other campgrounds show payment instructions at park entry or campground; staff or campground hosts may take payment (usually cash only); self-registration envelope; Mitchell Falls Campground is non-bookable and uses the envelopes.
- Tasmania Bay of Fires (https://parks.tas.gov.au/explore-our-parks/bay-of-fires-conservation-area/bay-of-fires-camping, opened): free; no bookings; designated campgrounds only; maximum stay four weeks.
- Tasmania Freycinet (https://parks.tas.gov.au/explore-our-parks/freycinet-national-park/richardsons-beach-honeymoon-bay-and-ranger-creek-camping, opened): "A valid parks pass is required for entry to Tasmania's national parks"; PWS has paused new bookings for stays from 3 November 2026 for the Parks Online Booking System; for stays from 3 November 2026 cancellations less than five days from arrival are ineligible for a refund. The PWS park passes page (https://parks.tas.gov.au/Pages/entry-fees.aspx, opened) says a valid parks pass is required for entry to all national parks.
- NT booking system (https://parkbookings.nt.gov.au/Web/Passes/AdvancePasses.aspx, opened): "You must have a pass to reserve a campsite"; NT residents need no Visitor Pass "but are still required to book and pay for camping and multi-day walks"; the pass does not cover Kakadu or Uluru-Kata Tjuta. NT camping page extract (https://nt.gov.au/parks/camping, search extract): designated campsites booked and paid online before arrival, no payment at the park.
- ACT (https://www.parks.act.gov.au/find-a-nature-park/namadgi-national-park/honeysuckle-campground and the Orroral and Mount Clear pages, search extract): bookings essential at Honeysuckle, Orroral and Mount Clear; maximum seven consecutive days at Honeysuckle and Orroral.
