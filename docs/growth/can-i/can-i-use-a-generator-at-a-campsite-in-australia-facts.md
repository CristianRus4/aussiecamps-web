# Facts: can-i-use-a-generator-at-a-campsite-in-australia (publishAt 2026-11-13)

Opened 2026-10-21. Pages marked "search extract" returned HTTP 403 (Cloudflare or similar) to curl, WebFetch and headless Playwright, so the rule rests on an official-page search extract.

## Confirmed rules used
- NSW NPWS "Generators when camping" (https://www.nationalparks.nsw.gov.au/conservation-and-heritage/care-for-parks/considerate-camping/camping-generators, opened): generators permitted with restrictions in some designated campgrounds; campers must check; use can be prohibited for a period (bird roosting, fire bans); only 1 generator per group; located closer to your camp than neighbours; some parks have noise curfews that apply to generators; medical/disability use allowed after contacting the local NPWS office with evidence such as a medical certificate.
- NSW NPWS Generator use policy (https://www.environment.nsw.gov.au/topics/parks-reserves-and-protected-areas/park-policies/generator-use, opened; policy adopted 2016, updated March 2021, page updated 1 July 2024): generally not permitted; signs show where allowed; no sign means consent from park manager needed; must minimise noise, one generator, closer to own camp, obey curfews; fire: clear of fuels, cords rated external and not across tracks, follow manufacturer guidelines, present at all times, never on days of local fire bans; "generator includes vehicle engines that are left running to charge batteries or directly power other equipment"; prohibition for a period must be signed.
- Queensland Government "Generator use" (https://www.qld.gov.au/recreation/activities/camping/generators, opened; last updated 21 April 2026): not permitted in most parks or forests; consider solar panels, inverters, charging while driving; where allowed, low dB generators up to 2.0 kVA, max 65 dB at 7 m, unless otherwise specified; hours on park pages; fire precautions as NSW; never on days of local fire ban; medical exception: small low dB generator up to 2.0 kVA to charge auxiliary batteries for a medical device where generators are not allowed, needs a current medical certificate.
- Explore Parks WA "Powered campsites and generators" (https://exploreparks.dbca.wa.gov.au/node/3770, opened; page updated 18 April 2024): no campground has electrical power to campsites; generators at some campgrounds only at certain times, usually 8am to 9pm, varies; not permitted at others; a small number of larger campgrounds have separate generator and quiet areas; position closer to you than other campers, cords not across visitor areas, keep running time to a minimum, 3 m clear space, never unattended; not on total fire ban days; medical exemption by application via contact page.
- Victorian Government "Camping" (https://www.vic.gov.au/camping, opened; updated 15 August 2025), state forests: generator allowed if safe; spark arrestor on exhaust, 1.5 m cleared space, able to put out fire immediately with water or stored-pressure extinguisher; solar or battery powered generators encouraged.
- Parks Victoria Warby-Ovens National Park camping (https://www.parks.vic.gov.au/places-to-see/parks/warby-ovens-national-park/things-to-do/camping, opened): "A generator may only be used on a day of Total Fire Ban if a 3-metre area around it is cleared of anything flammable"; consider impacts of generators; clear of fuels, refuel only when engine is cold, cords rated and not across tracks.
- Parks Victoria Wilsons Promontory (https://www.parks.vic.gov.au/places-to-see/parks/wilsons-promontory-national-park, opened via WebFetch; no page date): "No generators" under park rules. Jimmy Creek Campground, Grampians (https://www.parks.vic.gov.au/places-to-see/sites/jimmy-creek-campground, opened): Restrictions "No generators".
- NPWS South Australia camping essentials (https://www.parks.sa.gov.au/know-before-you-go/camping-essentials, opened): "Check with your local parks office to find out if you can use a generator."; minimise noise at night. Onkaparinga River NP (https://parks.sa.gov.au/parks/onkaparinga-river-national-park, opened): "do not bring generators (except where permitted), chainsaws or firearms". Kati Thanda-Lake Eyre (https://www.environment.sa.gov.au/goodliving/posts/2025/05/visit-kati-thanda-lake-eyre, opened): "Only use generators during daylight hours."
- Tasmania PWS: Lime Bay (https://parks.tas.gov.au/explore-our-parks/lime-bay-state-reserve/lime-bay-camping, opened): generators permitted but off by 9pm, all other noise including music also stops by 9pm. Peggs Beach and Black River (https://parks.tas.gov.au/explore-our-parks/peggs-beach-conservation-area/peggs-beach-and-black-river-camping, opened): generators 10am to 8pm. Narawntapu camping (https://parks.tas.gov.au/explore-our-parks/narawntapu-national-park/narawntapu-camping, opened): "Generators and dogs are not permitted in the national park". Cockle Creek (https://parks.tas.gov.au/explore-our-parks/southwest-national-park/cockle-creek-camping, opened): dogs, fires, chainsaws and generators not permitted over the bridge, breaches may incur heavy fines.
- Parks Australia Kakadu: Mardugal One campground (https://parksaustralia.gov.au/kakadu/stay/camping/mardugal-one-campground, opened): "Generators permitted". Maguk (https://parksaustralia.gov.au/kakadu/stay/camping/maguk-campground, opened): "No generators permitted".
- Queensland State-controlled Roads Regulation 2017 (https://www.legislation.qld.gov.au/view/whole/html/inforce/current/sl-2017-0169, opened): s10B(e) at a general vehicle rest area must not operate a generator or engine-driven equipment between 7pm and 7am; s10F(1)(c) person camping on a state-controlled road must not operate a generator or engine-driven equipment, maximum penalty 20 penalty units (dollar value not converted).
- Search extracts (nt.gov.au and parks.act.gov.au block automated fetches): NT booking system terms: generators not permitted unless authorised by the Commission or a sign states "Generators Permitted"; Keep River (https://nt.gov.au/parks/find-a-park/keep-river-national-park): generators only at Goorrandalng Campground, 7.30am to 8.30pm; Litchfield and Nitmiluk fact sheets: generators not permitted. ACT Mount Clear campground (https://www.parks.act.gov.au/find-a-nature-park/namadgi-national-park/mount-clear-campground): "Motorised generator use prohibited".

## NOT CONFIRMED / left out
- NT rules rest on search extracts only (see above). Garig Gunak Barlu (Campground 2 only, 65 dB, off by 9pm), Channel Point (off 10pm to 6am), Limmen, Judbarra hours: extract or third-party only, left out of the page.
- SA National Parks Regulations 2001 generator clause (operating a generator in a reserve without the Director's permission except in an area set aside): seen only in a search extract (legislation.sa.gov.au returns 403), left out.
- Fuel storage rules for generators: no official statement found, left out (page says so).
- Queensland park hours examples (Koombooloomba 8am to 7pm, Broadwater 9am to 12pm): from search extracts of park pages, not opened, left out.
- Third-party claims (65 dB NPWS limit, 9 L spray pump in Victoria, 8am to 9pm in WA from retailer blogs) not used except where the official page says the same.
- Tasmania Parks statewide generator rule: none found; the page cites reserve by reserve.
- Quiet hours by state: only NSW says "noise curfews" apply per park; no statewide quiet hours confirmed.

## CONFLICTS
- A Discover Tasmania tourism listing says Lime Bay generators must be off at 10pm; the PWS page says 9pm. Page uses 9pm.
- responsible-camping-australia says to "follow quiet hours" without detail; no conflict. sleeping-in-car-australia says Queensland rest area generators "must stay off between 7pm and 7am": matches s10B(e) for general vehicle rest areas.
