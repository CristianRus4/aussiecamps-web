# Facts: can-i-stay-overnight-at-a-rest-area-in-australia (publishAt 2026-11-14)

Opened 2026-10-21. Pages marked "search extract" returned HTTP 403 to automated fetches.

## Confirmed rules used
- Queensland Government rest areas (https://www.qld.gov.au/transport/safety/holiday-travel/stops/rest, opened; page last updated 13 May 2021): just over 450 rest areas; three types (motorist, heavy vehicle, combined); "General and recreational vehicles can stop at a rest area for up to 20 hours unless signed otherwise, but they are not camping sites or an accommodation option"; heavy vehicle rest areas not for other motorists, fines may apply; blue guide signs with white tree and picnic table mark a rest area; rest area ahead sign.
- Queensland State-controlled Roads Regulation 2017 (https://www.legislation.qld.gov.au/view/whole/html/inforce/current/sl-2017-0169, opened): s10 heavy vehicle rest areas, road user other than heavy vehicle driver must not use unless reasonable excuse, max 20 penalty units; s10A signed period, default 20 hours within the 24 hour period starting when first entered, must comply with sign or marking, max 20 penalty units; s10B at a rest area: no litter outside containers, no fire or fire pit, no human waste except in facilities, at a general vehicle rest area no generator or engine-driven equipment 7pm to 7am, nothing that unreasonably interferes with others; s10C chief executive can direct a person to leave; s10D/10E camping on a state-controlled road other than motorway or toll road only to manage fatigue, 4pm to 8am, no prohibiting sign, outside built-up area, 200 m from kerb/channel/footpath, 100 m from rest area entry/exit, 100 m from construction site/pit/quarry, 100 m from residence, 100 m from high-water mark of Queensland waters, 9 m from nearest traffic lane; s10F no fire, fire pit or generator while camping, max 20 penalty units. (Dollar value of penalty units not stated or converted.)
- Main Roads WA long distance driving (https://www.mainroads.wa.gov.au/travel-information/driving-in-wa/long-distance-driving/, opened): over 1,600 designated roadside stopping and rest areas; "some 24-hour rest areas across the state, however these are not intended for camping, and stopping at these areas should not exceed 24 consecutive hours"; approximately 200 heavy vehicle rest areas; Travel Map rest area filter.
- Main Roads WA policy "Rest Areas on main roads and highways", D16#249528, February 2023 (https://www.mainroads.wa.gov.au/globalassets/technical-commercial/technical-library/road-and-traffic-engineering/roadside-items/rest-area-policy-and-guideline/policy-and-guidelines-rest-areas-on-main-roads-and-highways.pdf, opened; "printed copies uncontrolled"): most rest areas not intended as a camping place or for stays beyond 24 hours; overnight stops may be permitted in some at Regional Director's discretion; maximum stay 24 hours enforced; "signage permitting 24-hour stops shall be erected in a prominent position"; "No overnight stops are allowed if there is no such signage"; MR-SG-18 information sign at 24-hour sites; heavily occupied by overnight stays makes the rest area unavailable to fatigued motorists; major 24-hour rest areas in general not built near towns or in competition with local business.
- Service NSW (https://www.service.nsw.gov.au/referral/find-rest-areas-in-nsw, opened; last published 9 April 2026): rest areas available 24 hours a day; "Rest areas are not for camping, if you need a longer rest, find a campsite, hotel or motel."
- Transport for NSW caravans (https://www.transport.nsw.gov.au/roadsafety/road-users/drivers/caravans, opened): "these rest areas aren't designated caravan sites and you can't stay overnight".
- NSW Department of Planning, Housing and Infrastructure practice note "Caravan parks and camping grounds", issued 1 July 2026 (https://www.planning.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-07/caravan-parks-and-camping-grounds-practice-note.pdf, opened): "Camping in a roadside rest area may be permitted, unless a 'no camping' or 'no overnight stays' sign has been placed there by a council or Transport for NSW (or other relevant authority)."
- Barossa Council SA (https://www.barossa.sa.gov.au/recreation-and-venues/camping, opened): prohibited to use any public reserve, car park, road or rest areas for camping or overnight stays; on-the-spot fine possible.
- Northern Midlands Council Tas (https://northernmidlands.tas.gov.au/community/facilities/rv-friendly-towns, opened): permit required, free for up to 48 hours, 24/7 online; self-contained vehicles only; discharge of waste prohibited, fine under Litter Act 2007; signposted designated areas (not road-authority rest areas).
- NT (search extracts; dipl.nt.gov.au and nt.gov.au return 403): Department of Transport roadside rest areas policy (https://dipl.nt.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0017/240416/RoadsideRestAreas.pdf): intention of rest areas is a short break; "limited overnight stays must be allowed"; road users may not stay at any one rest area longer than 24 hours. NT road rest stops page (https://nt.gov.au/driving/safety/road-rest-stops-in-nt): lists stops on the Stuart and other highways that allow 24-hour overnight camping (list not reproduced on the page).
- AussieCamps counts: lib/data/product-facts.json totals.restAreas 5,552 and campgrounds 1,838 (dataAsOf 2026-09-09).

## NOT CONFIRMED / left out
- Victoria: no statewide rest-area stay limit found. VicRoads' Victorian Rest Area Strategy (search extract only, 403, older document) says rest areas should let drivers sleep and cater for caravans "not for camping purposes"; left out as not current. Surf Coast Shire $300 fine for sleeping in a vehicle: council page 403, local law and page figures conflict, left out of this page.
- South Australia DIT: no official statewide overnight rule found (dit.sa.gov.au 403). A 24-hours-if-signed rule was not confirmed.
- Tasmania State Growth: no official rule on overnight stays at State Growth rest areas found. A 2019 Treasury competitive neutrality paper (search extract) says self-contained RVs may legally use rest stops where overnight parking is not prohibited; not used.
- NT: the list of rest stops permitting overnight camping is from a search extract; stop names left out.
- Fines other than Queensland's maximum of 20 penalty units: none officially stated, so none given.
- Full Range Camping directory wording ("rest areas are not designed to be camping grounds") is third-party; not used.

## CONFLICTS
- NSW: Transport for NSW says "you can't stay overnight", Service NSW says "not for camping", the July 2026 planning practice note says camping "may be permitted unless a sign". The page states all three; the existing guides sleeping-in-car-australia and is-free-camping-legal-australia mention the Service NSW and planning note pair but not the TfNSW caravans page.
- Existing guides' Queensland and WA facts match (20 hours unless signed; 24 consecutive hours, signed only). sleeping-in-car-australia says "Heavy-vehicle rest areas are for truck drivers only, and generators must stay off between 7pm and 7am": the generator rule (s10B(e)) applies to general vehicle rest areas.
