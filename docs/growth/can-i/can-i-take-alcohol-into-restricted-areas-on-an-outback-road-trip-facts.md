# Facts: can-i-take-alcohol-into-restricted-areas-on-an-outback-road-trip

Checked 2026-10-21. Page answer: Sometimes, within limits; none at all in some communities; rules are per community or region.

nt.gov.au, alcoholpolicy.nt.gov.au and dth.nt.gov.au are behind Cloudflare (403 to WebFetch and curl). All NT facts below rest on official-page search extracts, except the northernterritory.com tourism page and the CLC page, which were opened.

## Queensland (all qld.gov.au pages opened 2026-10-21)

- 15 restricted community areas: Aurukun, Cherbourg, Doomadgee, Hope Vale, Kowanyama, Lockhart River, Mapoon, Mornington Island, Napranum, Northern Peninsula Area, Palm Island, Pormpuraaw, Woorabinda, Wujal Wujal, Yarrabah. Restricted area is the entire shire unless stated; carriage limit applies per person on foot or per vehicle, boat or aircraft regardless of passengers; some communities are zero. https://www.qld.gov.au/firstnations/community-alcohol-restrictions and .../remote-discrete-communities (index page last updated 5 August 2022).
- Zero (possession prohibited): Aurukun (page updated 1 July 2026), Lockhart River, Napranum (Peninsula Development Road section excluded), Wujal Wujal.
- Kowanyama: 2 L unfortified wine and 4.5 L (12 x 375 mL) of beer and premixed spirits under 4%; fortified wine not allowed; homebrew prohibited.
- Pormpuraaw: 4.5 L (12 cans) beer and/or premix up to 4%.
- Doomadgee: up to 22.5 L beer under 4% (two cartons of 30 cans); wine and spirits zero.
- Mornington Island: 11.25 L (30 cans) of beer and premix up to 4%.
- Northern Peninsula Area: 2 L unfortified wine plus either 11.25 L (30 x 375 mL) beer of any strength or 9 L (24 cans) premix up to 5.5%. (A search extract suggested the two options conflicted; the page itself lists both as alternatives.)
- Mapoon: two combinations, listed on the page (not all repeated on the page).
- Bona fide traveller: https://www.qld.gov.au/firstnations/community-alcohol-restrictions/travelling-alcohol-restricted-areas (page last updated 5 September 2016). Named roads: Doomadgee (Savannah Way and public facilities incl. Doomadgee Roadhouse), Lockhart River (Frenchmen's Road, Portland Roads Road), Wujal Wujal (Bloomfield Track etc.). Conditions: secured and covered, not removed from vehicle, not consumed in the restricted area, stop only at prescribed public facilities except emergencies. No exemption elsewhere. Police powers: stop and search, take alcohol, take the vehicle.
- Penalties: https://www.qld.gov.au/firstnations/community-alcohol-restrictions/fines-penalties : first offence 375 penalty units ($64,762), second 525 units ($90,667) or 6 months, third 750 units ($129,525) or 18 months; unit $172.70 from 1 July 2026; attempting to bring in alcohol above the limit up to 375 units (NPA and Lockhart River pages); vehicles may be confiscated.
- Hope Vale, Cherbourg, Palm Island, Woorabinda, Yarrabah, Mapoon details: only "also have restrictions" stated.

## Northern Territory

- Interim alcohol protected areas since 16 February 2023 under the Liquor Act 2019; communities formerly under Stronger Futures are dry again; illegal to bring in, possess, consume, sell or supply; automatically repeal on 28 February 2027 (Director of Liquor Licensing notice, dth.nt.gov.au, search extract). Boundaries are certified plans on NTLIS.
- Penalty: NT Government penalty table lists no infringement option, maximum 200 penalty units or 12 months jail (search extract of nt.gov.au/law/alcohol/bans-and-dry-areas/alcohol-penalties-and-offences). Vehicles can be searched and seized (same extract). Dollar value of a unit not confirmed and not used.
- Aboriginal land: "unless allowed by permit, alcohol is banned" (alcoholpolicy.nt.gov.au extract). CLC (opened): "The possession, consumption and sale of alcohol on Aboriginal land is prohibited and severe penalties apply"; "transit visitors may carry alcohol provided they don't drink it on Aboriginal land". https://www.clc.org.au/permits/
- Tourism page (opened): photo ID for takeaway anywhere in NT; Alice Springs no takeaway Monday or Tuesday; Tennant Creek none on Sunday; limited hours in Alice Springs, Tennant Creek, Katherine; "Generally, takeaway alcohol is not permitted in remote communities"; drinking in public not permitted in Alice Springs, Tennant Creek, Katherine and some parts of Darwin; "drinking is not permitted within 2km of any licensed premises" across the NT. https://northernterritory.com/plan/useful-information/purchasing-and-drinking-alcohol
- June 2026 news (AAP extract): NT Government said it would act well before February 2027. Page says only "confirm the current position".

## Western Australia

- Carriage limits (https://www.wa.gov.au/organisation/racing-gaming-and-liquor/liquor-carriage-limits, opened, last updated 22 June 2026): Broome, Derby, Fitzroy Crossing, Halls Creek, Kununurra, Wyndham, Carnarvon (added August 2023) and within 5 km of the boundaries of the Shires of Broome, Carnarvon, Derby-West Kimberley, Halls Creek, Wyndham-East Kimberley; per person beer, cider, premix 2.7 to 7% one carton (11.25 L); wine 7 to 15% 3 bottles (2.25 L); fortified wine and spirits over 15% 1 L; alone or with one other kind; 0 L with two or more other kinds; vehicle limit by adult occupants (five adults, five quantities; 6+ passenger vehicles five times); exemptions for tourists, pastoralists, station owners, remote work site operators, licensee vehicles, police (conditions not given); fine $40,000.
- Section 64 and 175 list (https://dlgsc.wa.gov.au/liquor-restrictions and .../liquor-restrictions-section-64, opened, no last-updated date): Kimberley-wide no container over 1 L at 6% or more (casks to 1.5 L exempt), no beer in glass bottles of 400 mL or more; drinking on public roads or in private vehicles an offence without permit or licence; drinking in parks or public places without owner's or controlling authority's consent an offence; Fitzroy Crossing takeaway above 2.7% only to permanent or casual hotel lodgers; Halls Creek only to hotel lodgers or liquor merchants; Section 175 restricted communities: bringing in and possessing prohibited, $5,000 licensees, $2,000 anyone else, police can seize; Looma, Strelley, Wakathuni, Warralong exempt transiting vehicles (defined as passing through on a public road without stopping or discharging a person or item); Parnngurr and Kunawarritji tourists may possess if not consumed and kept until they leave; Spinifex tourist exemptions incl. 50 m either side of the Anne Beadell Highway; entries have expiry dates.
- Used on the page: Broome and Derby (15 July 2024) described only as limiting days and hours with ID scanning (not used finally); the page body keeps to carriage limits, Kimberley-wide containers, Fitzroy Crossing, Halls Creek and examples of section 175 communities.
