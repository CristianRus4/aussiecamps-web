# Facts: can-i-camp-on-private-land-or-a-farm-in-australia

Checked 2026-10-21. Page answer: Yes, with the owner's permission; hosts may also need council approval, which varies by state and council.

## Rules used

- NSW (opened 2026-10-21, 5-page PDF): Department of Planning, Housing and Infrastructure practice note "Caravan parks and camping grounds", issued 1 July 2026, replaces PS 21-021. Camping ground use needs development consent under Housing SEPP Chapter 3 Part 9 (except NPW Act land) and council approval under s 68 Local Government Act unless excepted; LG Regulation 2021 exemption: not more than two caravans, campervans or tents on any land with the landowner's permission, not occupied more than two days at a time and not more than 60 days in total in any 12 months; operating a camping ground without approval is an offence (s 626 LG Act); council may order the use to cease (s 9.34 EP&A Act); primitive camping grounds need approval, water supply, toilet and refuse disposal, average two sites per hectare; temporary camping grounds need consent and approval, council should be satisfied water, toilets, refuse are adequate. https://www.planning.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-07/caravan-parks-and-camping-grounds.pdf
- NSW farm stay camping (opened): no planning or building approval if tents are 6 m from any building, caravan, annex or campervan, no more than 20 people at one time, no more than 21 days each visit, farm at least 15 ha. The page says these are "certain requirements, including", so other conditions may apply. https://www.planning.nsw.gov.au/policy-and-legislation/under-review-and-new-policy-and-legislation/agritourism/5-things-you-can-do-on-your-farm
- NSW trespass: Inclosed Lands Protection Act 1901 s 4 (entering inclosed lands without consent of owner, occupier or person in charge, or remaining after being asked to leave; 5 penalty units, 10 for prescribed premises) and s 5 (leaving a gate or slip-panel open, 2 penalty units). RESTS ON A SEARCH EXTRACT: legislation.nsw.gov.au returned 403 to WebFetch and a JS shell to curl. https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/view/whole/html/inforce/current/act-1901-033. Dollar amounts deliberately not stated.
- NSW fire: RFS "Total Fire Ban rules": no fire in the open on a Total Fire Ban day; gas cookers excepted with conditions. RESTS ON A SEARCH EXTRACT (rfs.nsw.gov.au returned 403). The existing campfires guide cites the same page.
- WA: Caravan Parks and Camping Grounds Regulations 1997, official version as at 1 September 2024 (PDF opened, text extracted): r 11(1)(a) camp up to 5 nights in any 28 consecutive days on land the person owns or has a legal right to occupy; r 11(1)(aa) more than 5 nights needs written approval of the local government (r 11A, up to 24 consecutive months) or the Minister (r 11B). https://www.legislation.wa.gov.au/legislation/prod/filestore.nsf/FileURL/mrdoc_47625.pdf/$FILE/Caravan%20Parks%20and%20Camping%20Grounds%20Regulations%201997%20-%20%5B02-i0-00%5D.pdf . Version date is 2024; no 2026 amendment found.
- WA media statement 22 August 2024 (opened): 5 days without approval, local governments can approve up to 24 months, more than one caravan on a lot still needs special approval. https://www.wa.gov.au/government/media-statements/Cook-Labor-Government/WA-Government-to-streamline-temporary-accommodation-approvals-20240822
- City of Kwinana (opened): up to 5 nights in any 28 consecutive days needs the property owner's approval; longer needs owner and council; regular short-term camping makes the property a caravan park or camping ground needing planning and building approvals. https://www.kwinana.wa.gov.au/property-and-pets/building-and-renovating-(residential)/temporary-accommodation
- Victoria: Mount Alexander Shire (opened): permit needed to camp, or keep or use a caravan, on land without a dwelling that is not an approved camping ground. The page also mentions a Local Law 13 change allowing caravans and tiny homes without a permit, conditions unclear; not stated on the page. Fees not used. https://www.mountalexander.vic.gov.au/Permit-finder/Camping-on-public-or-private-land
- Victoria: Consumer Affairs Victoria (opened): "You must register your park as a caravan park with the local council." The page does not mention farms. https://www.consumer.vic.gov.au/licensing-and-registration/caravan-and-residential-park-operators/park-registration
- Queensland trespass: Legal Aid Queensland (opened): Summary Offences Act offence to enter or remain in someone's house, yard or business premises without permission; maximum penalty 1 year imprisonment; police decide whether to charge; the page does not address paddocks or rural land. https://legalaid.qld.gov.au/Find-legal-information/Criminal-justice/Offences/Criminal-trespass
- AussieCamps: 1,838 campgrounds, 745 national parks (product-facts.json); no claim about private land, permits or fire status.

Corrected (before -> after):
- Victoria row: "requires a permit to camp on land without a dwelling that is not an approved camping ground" (omitted the council's own change) -> adds that a Local Law 13 change lets people camp in caravans or tiny homes on wheels without a permit, conditions not listed on the page. "A park that takes campers can need caravan park registration" -> "A park must be registered as a caravan park with the council" (CAV wording).
- NSW farm stay: "without planning or building approval, with tents 6 metres from buildings and vans" -> "if requirements including tents 6 metres from buildings and vans are met" (page says "certain requirements, including"); FAQ adds "with other requirements to meet".
- WA row: "written approval from the local government, for up to 24 months" -> "from the local government (up to 24 months) or the Minister" (reg 11(1)(aa)).
- "More than one caravan on the property needs special approval" -> attributed: "The WA Government says special approval is still required for more than one caravan on a single lot" (media statement wording).
- Fire: "the exception for gas cookers has conditions" -> "gas barbecues" (RFS wording is gas barbecue; no cooker exemption confirmed).

Removed: none outright.

Official-source disagreements: none found. Notes: the NSW exemption is from council approval under the Local Government Act only (practice note), and the practice note says nothing about development consent for the two-caravan case; the page does not claim it. The Kwinana page says more than one caravan makes the site a caravan park needing development and building approvals, while the WA Government statement says "special approval"; page uses the WA Government wording.
