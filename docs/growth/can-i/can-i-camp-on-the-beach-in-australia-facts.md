## Confirmed rules used
- Qld K'gari (https://parks.qld.gov.au/parks/kgari-fraser/camping/camping-permits-and-requirements and the beach camping zone pages, search extract): named beach camping zones; you need the zone name when booking; camping permits must be bought before setting up camp; permits cannot be bought from rangers on the island.
- Qld Teewah Beach, Cooloola (https://parks.qld.gov.au/parks/cooloola/camping/teewah-beach-4, search extract): no marked sites; use existing access tracks and camp behind vegetated foredunes; stay in the zone you booked; portable toilets required.
- Qld Captain Billy Landing (https://book.parks.qld.gov.au/camping/captain-billy-landing-camping-area and https://parks.qld.gov.au/parks/apudthama/camping, search extract): camping permit and fees apply, e-permit, tag displayed; 4WD and off-road camper trailers only; generators not permitted; maximum 21 consecutive nights at all camping areas in that park; the camping area closed 18 to 28 September 2026 for Wuthathi cultural activities (park alert 26763), so the page says "has closed at times".
- NSW Diamond Head campground (https://www.nationalparks.nsw.gov.au/camping-and-accommodation/campgrounds/diamond-head-campground, opened): beachside, 75 sites; park entry fees apply and can be paid at booking; online group booking up to 20 people or 5 sites; fires in off-the-ground braziers only; check in at the onsite office.
- NSW Worimi, Ganyamalbaa (https://worimiconservationlands.com/camping/, opened; NPWS page https://www.nationalparks.nsw.gov.au/visit-a-park/parks/worimi-national-park links to it): bookings essential; camping allowed only in the Ganyamalbaa Camping Area; "Camping is not permitted elsewhere in the Worimi Conservation Lands, including staying overnight in vehicles on the beach"; 4WD access; beach vehicle permits are not included with camping fees. The page is a "camping trial" page and its fees ($33 site fee in the text) may be out of date, so no fee is quoted.
- Vic Cape Conran (https://www.parks.vic.gov.au/places-to-see/parks/cape-conran-coastal-park/where-to-stay/camping-in-cape-conran-coastal-park, opened): Binn Beach, Gunnai Beach and Pearl Point are non-bookable, first come first served; campfires only in designated fireplaces, bring your own wood; Banksia Bluff is the bookable campground.
- SA Wauraltee Beach (https://www.environment.sa.gov.au/our-places/beach-driving, opened): overnight camping, including swags and tents, and large camping vehicles (caravans, campervans, motorhomes, tent trailers) not permitted on Wauraltee Beach, including Second Beach; $315 fine plus $102 victims of crime levy may apply; council's Wauraltee Bush Campground has unpowered sites from $20 a night, permit bought on Yorke Peninsula Council's website. (Page says restrictions from "Monday 3 February"; year not stated, so no date is used.)
- SA Coffin Bay (https://www.parks.sa.gov.au/parks/coffin-bay-national-park, opened): six designated campgrounds, most 4WD only; Black Springs has eight designated campsites within 50 metres of the beach; camping fees apply and must be paid before arrival.
- WA Nyinggulu (Ningaloo) Coastal Reserves (https://exploreparks.dbca.wa.gov.au/park/nyinggulu-ningaloo-coastal-reserves, opened): "Camping is permitted in designated campgrounds only"; campgrounds listed as bookable; Tropical Cyclone Narelle damage and closures, check alerts.dbca.wa.gov.au.
- Tas Bay of Fires (https://parks.tas.gov.au/explore-our-parks/bay-of-fires-conservation-area/bay-of-fires-camping, opened): camping only in designated campgrounds; free; no bookings; maximum stay four weeks; campgrounds include Grants Lagoon, Jeanneret, Swimcart, Cosy South, Cosy North, Seatons Cove and Sloop Reef.
- NT booking system (https://parkbookings.nt.gov.au/Web/Passes/AdvancePasses.aspx, opened): "You must have a pass to reserve a campsite"; NT residents do not need a Visitor Pass "but are still required to book and pay for camping".
- NT crocodile advice (https://becrocwise.nt.gov.au/crocodiles-and-me, search extract; nt.gov.au crocodile safety page gives the same): camp at least 50 metres from the water's edge; in the Top End do not swim unless a swimming area open sign is present.

- Freycinet, Innes (Pondalowie Bay) and Murramarang/Myall Lakes beach campgrounds: pages or extracts read, not used on the page (Freycinet booking pause is on the booking page instead).
