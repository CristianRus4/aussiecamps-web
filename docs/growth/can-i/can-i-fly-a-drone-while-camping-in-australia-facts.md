# Facts: can-i-fly-a-drone-while-camping-in-australia (publishAt 2026-11-11)

All checked 2026-10-21. "Opened" = fetched in full. "Search extract" = the official page was blocked to automated fetch (HTTP 403 or timeout; casa.gov.au timed out on every attempt, parks.qld.gov.au, parks.act.gov.au and nt.gov.au return 403) and the rule rests on the official-page text returned by a web search for that page.

## Confirmed rules used
- CASA drone rules (https://www.casa.gov.au/knowyourdrone/drone-rules and https://www.casa.gov.au/drones/drone-rules/flying-populous-areas; search extracts, two searches agreeing): no licence or accreditation to fly for fun; registration of recreational drones over 250 g paused (commercial unchanged); max 120 m above ground level; keep in sight with own eyes, not through screen or goggles; daylight only; one drone at a time; at least 30 m from people and never over them; not in a populous area (anywhere people live or gather for a purpose, with parks, beaches, roads, festivals as examples); over 250 g stay out of 5.5 km of a controlled airport; no flying over or near emergency operations; use a CASA-verified drone safety app.
- NSW NPWS drones in parks policy (https://www.environment.nsw.gov.au/topics/parks-reserves-and-protected-areas/park-policies/drones-in-parks, opened): approval needed to launch, land or operate a drone from within a park for recreation; application form to the park, first check it is not a drone exclusion area; may be refused or conditioned; park alerts override approval; Kosciuszko plan of management prohibits recreational drones across the whole park; 100 m from marine mammals. Lead time not stated on the page.
- Parks Victoria visitor safety tips (https://www.parks.vic.gov.au/get-into-nature/safety-in-nature/visitor-safety-tips-in-parks, opened): members of the public cannot fly drones for recreation on Parks Victoria land; visitors who fly drones may be fined; commercial filming needs a permit.
- Queensland QPWS (https://parks.qld.gov.au/camping/legal-requirements, search extract; policy PDF https://www.detsi.qld.gov.au/_global/policy-register/policy-register-pdf?getdoc=6945&name=op-pk-vm-drones-qpws-areas.pdf, 403): drones prohibited in all camping areas in protected areas, recreation areas and forests; keep drone stored in a container in the vehicle or vessel; offence to operate in or into a drone restriction area; elsewhere drones of 2 kg or less may be operated without authorisation outside drone restriction areas (not used on the page).
- SA NPWS (https://www.parks.sa.gov.au/permits-and-licences/drones-and-aircraft, opened) and DEW (https://www.environment.sa.gov.au/licences-and-permits/filming-photography, opened): offence to fly a drone over national parks, conservation parks, game reserves, recreation parks or regional reserves, and marine park restricted access zones, without a permit; permits considered only for scientific research and commercial filming or photography.
- WA DBCA (https://exploreparks.dbca.wa.gov.au/drones-parks, opened): recreational drone use needs no department approval; follow CASA rules including 30 m; do not disturb wildlife or visitors; avoid emergency operations; contact the district office before each flight; Swan Canning Riverpark needs a separate permit; the page has no camping-area restriction.
- Tasmania PWS (https://parks.tas.gov.au/explore-our-parks/know-before-you-go/drones-in-parks, opened): operation and use of drones by visitors on reserved land including national parks is not permitted; exceptions for commercial and event filming and scientific research with authorisation; wildlife, privacy, helicopter and fire operations cited.
- NT Government (https://nt.gov.au/parks/permits-for-parks/permits-to-use-aircraft-and-drones-in-parks, search extract; 403): rules depend on the park; some parks need an operation of aircraft permit, some prohibit drones; an NT permit does not cover Kakadu or Uluru-Kata Tjuta.
- ACT Parks (https://www.parks.act.gov.au/before-you-go/drones, search extract; 403): flight and operation of drones is prohibited in Nature Conservation Act reserves; Namadgi page says drones not permitted.
- Kakadu (https://kakadu.gov.au/plan/permits/, opened): drones prohibited except under a management plan or park permit; permits not issued for recreational use; NT permits not valid in Kakadu.
- Uluru-Kata Tjuta (https://uluru.gov.au/plan/permits, opened): a no-drone park; approved only in exceptional circumstances, after Central Land Council consultation.

## CONFLICTS
- DBCA's own licences-and-permits page (search extract) says flying over national parks needs DBCA permission, while the DBCA Drones in parks page (opened) says recreational use needs no approval. The page follows the Drones in parks page and says to contact the district office before each flight.
- camping-rules-queensland and camping-rules-victoria (drones banned in Queensland camping areas; Wilsons Promontory bans drones) are consistent with the sources found.

The page follows the in-force pages; escalate to confirm.
