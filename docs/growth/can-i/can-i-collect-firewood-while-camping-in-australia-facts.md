# Facts: can-i-collect-firewood-while-camping-in-australia (publishAt 2026-11-10)

All opened 2026-10-21. Several official sites (ffm.vic.gov.au, forestrycorporation.com.au, parks.qld.gov.au, parks.desi.qld.gov.au, parks.act.gov.au, nt.gov.au) return HTTP 403 to automated fetches (WebFetch and curl). Rows marked "search extract" rest on the official-page text returned by a web search for that page, not on a page I opened in full.

## Confirmed rules used
- Victoria, Parks Victoria "Using firewood" (https://www.parks.vic.gov.au/things-to-do/using-firewood, opened): collection not permitted in national parks, conservation parks, nature and bushland reserves or Pyrenees Regional Park; designated parts of Bendigo, southern addition to Hepburn, Barkstead, Blackwood, Fingerpost and Spargo Creek regional parks allow free personal-use collection until 1 July 2029; State forest collection is limited to designated areas in autumn and spring seasons run by Forest Fire Management Victoria; bring your own firewood unless collection is specifically permitted; firewood sold by roadside stalls and online is not always legally sourced.
- Victoria, Victorian Government "Collecting firewood" (https://www.vic.gov.au/collecting-firewood, opened): collection not allowed in national, state, regional or metropolitan parks; State forest Domestic Firewood Collection Areas only; seasons 1 March to 30 June and 1 September to 30 November, areas can close at short notice; ground wood only; 2 m3 per person per day, 16 m3 per household per financial year; no sale; no chainsaws during a Total Fire Ban.
- NSW NPWS firewood policy (https://www.environment.nsw.gov.au/topics/parks-reserves-and-protected-areas/park-policies/firewood, opened): "Collecting firewood from within a park to use outside of a park is prohibited"; backcountry collection allowed in some places except where a plan of management or sign prohibits it; bringing wood into a park must be tightly controlled because it can spread pests and pathogens. NPWS news (search extract): collecting firewood including dead wood in national parks, state conservation areas and nature reserves is illegal.
- NSW Forestry Corporation (https://www.forestrycorporation.com.au/firewood, search extract; 403 on fetch): authorisation needed to collect from State forests, fallen timber only, some forests off limits.
- Queensland QPWS legal requirements (https://parks.qld.gov.au/camping/legal-requirements, search extract; 403): campfires only in existing QPWS fireplaces, fire rings or fire locations; "Firewood must not be collected."
- Queensland Government forest products (https://www.business.qld.gov.au/industries/farms-fishing-forestry/forests-wood/native-forests/products-sale, opened): firewood is among other forest products sold by short-term permit or one-off sale under the Forestry Act 1959.
- SA NPWS (https://www.parks.sa.gov.au/insider-tips/campfires-in-national-parks, opened): bring your own firewood; collecting firewood in national parks is prohibited; wood free from dirt and weeds.
- WA DBCA (https://www.dbca.wa.gov.au/firewood, opened): collection only in designated State forest areas in Perth Hills and South West; not in national parks, nature reserves or conservation parks; fallen timber only; Perth Hills permit required, valid for one visit; South West no permit; 1 tonne every 60 days from 1 June to 30 September, 1 tonne per occasion from 1 October to 31 May; "buy it where you burn it"; no removal from the Polyphagous shot hole borer Quarantine Area.
- Tasmania PWS (https://parks.tas.gov.au/explore-our-parks/know-before-you-go/campfires-and-fire-bans, opened): "Firewood cutting and collection is not permitted in Tasmania's National Parks and Reserves"; Fuel Stove Only areas ban campfires all year; seasonal bans ended 1 April 2026; Total Fire Ban prohibits all outdoor fires except electric and LPG.
- Sustainable Timber Tasmania (https://sttas.com.au/permits-access/collecting-firewood/, opened): permit required; designated areas; ground wood only; annual maximum 30 m3.
- Biosecurity Tasmania (https://nre.tas.gov.au/biosecurity-tasmania/travellers-guide-to-tasmanian-biosecurity-what-you-can-and-cant-bring-into-tasmania/travelling-to-tasmania-on-the-spirit-of-tasmania, opened): firewood among items checked at Devonport, may be seized, "If in doubt, please leave these items at home", penalties may apply.
- ACT Parks campfires (https://www.parks.act.gov.au/before-you-go/campfires, search extract; 403): bring your own firewood or buy from Namadgi Visitor Centre.
- Fire-ban line: Total Fire Ban stops all fires (Tas PWS page; Forestry Corporation search extract); NSW State forests and Tasmanian parks allow gas under seasonal solid fuel bans (search extracts).

## CONFLICTS
- camping-rules-northern-territory says to gather firewood outside parks "from fallen branches only"; not confirmed officially here.
- camping-rules-new-south-wales says never collect fallen timber in national parks; NPWS policy page allows some backcountry on-site collection where no sign prohibits it. Not a hard conflict, but the guide's "never" is stronger than the policy.
- camping-rules-act says collecting timber in a park is prohibited; ACT page extract only says bring your own firewood.
