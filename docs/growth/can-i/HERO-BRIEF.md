## Hero photo (one per page; 12 pages, list in docs/growth/progress/max-cani.md)
- Source: Wikimedia Commons only. Licence CC0, public domain, CC BY or CC BY-SA (never NC or ND). Original at least 1600x900.
- On topic and in Australia (the right state where the page is about one). No identifiable people (faces, number plates readable), no readable text or logos dominating, no vehicle badges unless softened. Pick something a reader would accept as "this page's subject": e.g. a beach campsite for beach camping, a 4WD on a beach for beach driving, a campfire or stacked firewood for firewood, a camp generator is not photogenic so a quiet campsite or powered caravan site is fine, a drone in the sky or a coastal camp from above for drone, a person-free river/lake with fishing rod or jetty for fishing, a rest area sign on a highway for rest area, red-dirt remote road for Aboriginal land permits, Bass Strait ferry for Spirit of Tasmania (a ferry or Devonport terminal; no logo text dominating), a farm paddock campsite for private land, a remote outback roadhouse for alcohol restrictions.
- LOOK at at least five candidates per page (download 480px thumbnails or the file itself, build a contact sheet with `magick montage` and view that single image, then view the winner at larger size). Search tool you can reuse: `python3 -I /tmp/maxcani/bin/commons.py "<search or Category:Name>" <outdir-name>` prints candidates (licence-filtered, 1600x900+) and downloads 480px thumbs to /tmp/maxcani/heroes/<outdir-name>/NN.jpg; the Commons search is noisy, so try several specific queries (place names help: "Teewah Beach", "Stockton Bight", "Cape Conran", "Bay of Fires"). Also try the Commons API with curl directly if useful. Do not reuse a hero already used by another AussieCamps guide (check docs/image-sources-*.md).
- Output: public/images/articles/<slug>.webp (1600x900, centre-cropped, quality ~82, metadata stripped) and <slug>-800.webp (800x450). Example: `magick in.jpg -resize 1600x900^ -gravity center -extent 1600x900 -strip -quality 82 out.webp`. Check the crop still shows the subject.
- Credit: add one row per page to docs/image-sources-can-i.md (create it; copy the table format of docs/image-sources-planning-app.md exactly: header columns Article slug, Photograph subject, Creator, Source page, License, License URL). Subject text must be factual and accurate to the photo.
- Alt text: put the final alt text (one factual sentence naming what and where, no "image of") in the page's `imageAlt` in lib/can-i/<slug>.ts (replace the placeholder).
- Then run `npm run images:credits` and `npm run images:manifest` once at the end (these rewrite lib/image-credits.json and lib/image-manifest.json; fine).
