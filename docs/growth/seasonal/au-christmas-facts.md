# Official-source facts: camping over Christmas and summer 2026-27 in Australia

Research lane for the guide "Camping over Christmas and summer 2026-27 in Australia". Every item below was read on **2026-10-21** (the date read for every entry unless noted).

## How to read this file

- **[READ]** means the page text was fetched and read directly, and the quote is verbatim from the page.
- **[EXTRACT]** means the official site refused automated access (HTTP 403) or could not be fetched, so the fact comes from a search-result extract that names that official page. Quotes marked [EXTRACT] are phrases as they appeared in the extract and were NOT confirmed against the live page. Treat them as lower confidence and re-check by hand before publishing.
- **not published** means the official page I could read is silent on it. Nothing was guessed.
- No site was circumvented. Sites that blocked are listed in the Blocked section at the end.
- No em dashes are used in these notes. Four quotes originally contained an em dash, shown here as a spaced hyphen.

## 3. Total Fire Bans and the fire danger season

### Who declares what (all from official pages)

- **NSW:** the RFS Commissioner declares Total Fire Bans.
  - URL: https://www.nationalparks.nsw.gov.au/safety/fire-safety/fire-bans [READ]
  - Quote: "During the bush fire danger period, the Commissioner of the NSW Rural Fire Service may declare a Total Fire Ban (TOBAN) for particular fire weather areas within NSW or statewide."
  - Quote: "A decision to declare a TOBAN is generally made at around 5pm each afternoon during the bushfire danger period (October to March) and applies for the following day, starting from midnight and lasting 24 hours."
  - Statutory Bush Fire Danger Period dates: the RFS site (rfs.nsw.gov.au) blocked me with HTTP 403. The NPWS page above says "(October to March)". Local news in September 2026 reported some council areas brought the period forward (for example Wingecarribee and Wollondilly to 14 September 2026), so the start varies by area. Statewide 1 October to 31 March is [EXTRACT] only.
- **VIC:** the CFA Chief Officer declares Total Fire Bans; the Fire Danger Period is declared by municipality.
  - URL: https://www.cfa.vic.gov.au/warnings-restrictions/fire-bans-ratings-and-restrictions/about-total-fire-bans [READ]
  - Quote: "Total Fire Bans are declared by CFA's Chief Officer on days of heightened fire danger to reduce the risk of a fire starting."
  - Quote: "Total Fire Bans can be declared even if the Fire Danger Rating isn’t ‘extreme’."
  - URL: https://www.cfa.vic.gov.au/warnings-restrictions/fire-danger-period/fire-restriction-dates [READ]
  - Quote: "The Fire Danger Period may be declared as early as October in some municipalities, and typically remains in place until the fire danger lessens, which could be as late as May."
  - Quote: "The Fire Danger Period is NOT the same thing as a Total Fire Ban ."
  - On 21 October 2026 the table showed only a few municipalities declared, for example "EAST GIPPSLAND 05/10/2026 01/05/2027", "WELLINGTON 05/10/2026 01/05/2027", "MILDURA 26/10/2026 01/05/2027". Most others read "No restrictions in force yet - check back for updates". This changes weekly, so link to the page rather than printing dates.
- **SA:** the CFS Chief Officer declares the Fire Danger Season by fire ban district.
  - URL: https://cfs.sa.gov.au/warnings-restrictions/restrictions/fire-danger-season-dates-permits/ [READ]
  - Quote: "The CFS Chief Officer has declared the Fire Danger Season dates for 2026/2027 for some fire ban districts in South Australia."
  - Published 2026/27 dates as of today: Eastern Eyre Peninsula, Flinders, North East Pastoral, North West Pastoral and West Coast "15 October 2026" to "15 April 2027"; Lower Eyre Peninsula "15 November 2026" to "15 April 2027"; Lower South East, Murraylands, Riverland and Upper South East "1 November 2026" to "30 April 2027". Adelaide Metropolitan, Kangaroo Island, Mid North, Mount Lofty Ranges and Yorke Peninsula read "TBA".
  - Quote: "Restrictions on fires under the Fire and Emergency Services Act 2005 will apply throughout South Australia from the dates listed, until midnight on the finish date."
- **WA:** DFES declares Total Fire Bans by local government area.
