# Sydney to Melbourne drive: facts (slug sydney-to-melbourne-drive)

Researched 2026-10-21. OSRM = "OSRM routing on OpenStreetMap data, computed 21 October 2026" (car, no stops, no caravan, CBD to CBD). Official road figures were not retrievable (see Sources), so distances below are OSRM plus Wikipedia/Wikivoyage as secondary cross-checks. Say "about" on every figure.

## 1. Route options

1. **Hume Highway / M31 (inland, main route)**
   - OSRM: 896 km, 11 h 16 min (Sydney, Goulburn, Gundagai, Wagga Wagga, Albury, Wangaratta, Melbourne).
   - Cross-checks: Wikipedia Hume Highway says about 840 km Sydney to Melbourne (highway length, not CBD to CBD); Wikipedia Princes Highway says 870 km on the Hume; Wikivoyage says 874 km and "at least a minimum of 10 hours". The gap to OSRM is the CBD start/end (OSRM 896 km). Do not print one exact number; say "about 870 to 900 km".
   - Road type: Wikipedia says fully dual carriageway since 7 August 2013 (Holbrook bypass opened), limited-access, 110 km/h along the full length. Wikivoyage says some rural intersections are at-grade (conflict: Wikipedia says limited-access). Safe wording: "dual carriageway all the way, 110 km/h".
   - M31 runs Prestons NSW to Campbellfield Vic (Wikipedia). Albury-Wodonga bypass opened 6 March 2007 (Wikipedia).
   - Legs by OSRM: Sydney-Goulburn 194 km 2h22; Goulburn-Gundagai 181 km 2h08; Gundagai-Wagga 80 km 1h05; Wagga-Albury 126 km 1h43; Albury-Wangaratta 74 km 0h54; Wangaratta-Melbourne 240 km 3h01.
2. **Princes Highway coast (A1 / M1 parts)**
   - OSRM: 1,042 km, 14 h 35 min (Sydney, Nowra, Batemans Bay, Eden, Lakes Entrance, Sale, Melbourne).
   - Cross-checks: Wikipedia about 1,040 km; Wikivoyage 1,032 km and "possible in two days with one overnight stop", recommends at least four days to enjoy it. All three agree within 10 km.
   - Road type (Wikivoyage): freeway-grade Sydney to Nowra and Traralgon to Melbourne; otherwise mostly two-lane with occasional passing lanes. Wikipedia: four-lane around Nowra, freeway-standard in Gippsland.
   - OSRM legs: Sydney-Nowra 162 km 2h25; Nowra-Batemans Bay 117 km 1h42; Batemans Bay-Eden 199 km 2h51; Eden-Lakes Entrance 242 km 3h09; Lakes Entrance-Sale 107 km 1h33; Sale-Melbourne 215 km 2h52.
   - Eden to Lakes Entrance with towns (OSRM): Eden-Genoa 66 km, Genoa-Cann River 46 km, Cann River-Orbost 83 km, Orbost-Lakes Entrance 58 km (total 253 km via those waypoints; 242 km direct).
3. **Inland via Canberra (Federal Highway) rejoining the Hume**
   - OSRM: 970 km, 12 h 21 min (Sydney, Goulburn, Canberra, Gundagai, Wagga, Albury, Melbourne). About 74 km and 1 h longer than option 1 by OSRM. Wikivoyage says the Canberra detour from the Hume is 80 km extra; the figures are in the same range.
   - Real option for a Canberra stop; the legs are Goulburn-Canberra 91 km 1h12 and Canberra-Gundagai 162 km 2h05 (OSRM).
4. **Monaro Highway variant (Canberra, Cooma, Bombala, Cann River, then Princes Highway)**
   - Wikivoyage: Monaro Highway is 285 km from Canberra to Cann River via Cooma and Bombala, then Princes Highway west through Lakes Entrance. No total or drive time from a source; OSRM not run. Mention in one line only.

## 2. Legs and overnight stops (main route, Hume)
Suggested two-day split (OSRM times, no stops): Day 1 Sydney to Gundagai or Wagga Wagga (about 375 km to Gundagai per Wikivoyage; OSRM 375 km to Gundagai = 194 + 181), Day 2 on to Melbourne (about 520 km from Gundagai: 80+126+74+240 = 520 km OSRM). Three-day split: Sydney-Goulburn-Wagga-Albury-Melbourne.

Release dates not shown in the extract: say "has been upgraded or is being upgraded", never give a closure date.

## 3. Fuel and services
- Hume: Wikivoyage says petrol stations are "plentiful" on both routes; EV chargers are widely available on the Hume but "still spotty" on the Princes Highway. Ampol Foodary Pheasants Nest Southbound (AU18921, Toilets, Showers) is on the corridor at about 85 km (listed as a dump point in data).
- Longest gap I can source on the Princes route: Wikivoyage says Eden to Orbost is 185 km and "the remotest stretch with scattered services". OSRM gives Eden-Orbost about 195 km (66 + 46 + 83). Cann River is the usual halfway stop (Wikivoyage). Which of Genoa, Cann River or Orbost have fuel is NOT CONFIRMED.
- Hume gap: no gap needing a plan; the largest OSRM spacing between the main towns is Goulburn-Gundagai 181 km, but the highway has service centres between (named: Pheasants Nest, Avenel Service Centre). Fuel availability at each is NOT CONFIRMED.

## 4. Road conditions and closures
- Live sources: Live Traffic NSW (livetraffic.com, phone 132 701), VicTraffic and VicEmergency (Victoria; traffic.vic.gov.au and vicemergency.vic.gov.au did not load for me, so confirm the URLs before linking).
- Tolls, Sydney: NSW Government says "If you travel on a toll road without an active toll account or pass, you have 72 hours to pay or create a toll account." Service NSW says you can "open an account or purchase a pass up to 72 hours after you travel on a toll road" and lists "Visiting NSW or up to 30 days NSW toll road use - eMU Casual Pass". Wikipedia: M5 and M7 are tolled and meet the Hume at Prestons; the Hume itself is not described as tolled. Wikivoyage: no toll roads on either route outside the cities.
- Tolls, Melbourne (Linkt Visitor's Pass page): "good to go for up to 30 days on any toll road in Melbourne"; covers "CityLink trips made up to 5 days ago, and EastLink trips made up to 3 days ago"; opening fee $3.50 online or $5.00 by phone, plus a video matching fee per trip (amount not on the page); needs a card and the licence plate. West Gate Tunnel is listed under Victoria but less clearly. Victorian Government guidance (search extract) says buy a pass before or up to 3 days after travel.
- Snow and chains: no alpine pass on the Hume or Princes Highway. Wikivoyage says on the Monaro Highway snow is unlikely and chains are not required. Chain rules for alpine roads are in australian-alps-camping-route and victoria-high-country-camping (link, do not repeat).
- Fire and flood on the Princes Highway: news reports (not official, search extract) record the highway shut for more than a month in the 2019-20 fires between Bruthen/Orbost and the NSW border, and closed at the Victorian border. No current closure was found for 21 October 2026. Tell readers to check VicEmergency and Live Traffic NSW on the day, especially Cann River to Genoa.
- Wildlife and cameras (Wikivoyage): kangaroos common at night; fixed and mobile speed cameras and frequent patrols; both roads busy from Christmas to the end of January and accommodation often full. Link: australia-road-trip-fatigue-wildlife-safety, christmas-summer-camping-australia-2026-27.
- Roadworks: Wikivoyage mentions Audley Weir in Royal National Park floods after heavy rain (not on the route; skip).

## 5. State border rules (NSW to Victoria)
- Fruit fly: search extract of Agriculture Victoria's page "Regulatory changes in the Greater Sunraysia region" (page returned 403): the Greater Sunraysia Pest Free Area was in place from 2006 and regulation ended in July 2024, so there are no longer restrictions on carrying Queensland fruit fly host produce into or out of Sunraysia. The Agriculture Victoria Fruit Fly Strategy PDF says Victoria deregulated Queensland fruit fly host movement into and within the state on 1 July 2013, with the Greater Sunraysia Pest Free Area as the exception. Mediterranean fruit fly restrictions apply statewide (search extract). The Hume and Princes crossings are not in Sunraysia.
- Safe wording for the writer: "There is no fruit fly checkpoint on the Hume or Princes Highway crossings that we could find. Victoria still regulates Mediterranean fruit fly, so check Agriculture Victoria (136 186) before bringing produce." Do not say "no restrictions".
- Other zones along the Hume (Interstate Quarantine site, undated page): NSW Phylloxera Infested Zone covers areas around Albury-Corowa and the Sydney basin ("Do not take soil, grapevine plants, mulch or garden organics out of these areas"); NSW Rice Biosecurity Zone covers most of the Murrumbidgee and Murray River regions ("Do not bring rice plants or unprocessed grain such as paddy rice or brown rice into the zone"). Victoria: signs on major roads mark phylloxera zones. Mention one line only; none affect normal camping food.
- South Australia's Riverland and Tasmania rules do not apply to this route.

## 7. Other facts a driver needs
- Time zone: no change between Sydney and Melbourne. Both on daylight saving at the travel dates (NOT CONFIRMED by a fetched source, see list).
- Canberra detour: ACT is a separate jurisdiction for road rules but the same time zone. Not researched further.
- National park fees: Park entry fee applies at the NSW national park campgrounds listed above (data shows "Park entry fee"); none are on the Hume proper.
