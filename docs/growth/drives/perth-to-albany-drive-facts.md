# Perth to Albany drive: facts (slug perth-to-albany-drive)

Researched 2026-10-21. OSRM = "OSRM routing on OpenStreetMap data, computed 21 October 2026" (car, no stops, no caravan, centre to centre). Whole route is inside Western Australia: no state border, no interstate checkpoint. Say "about" on every distance.

## 1. Route options
1. **Albany Highway (State Route 30) via Armadale, Williams, Kojonup, Cranbrook, Mount Barker (main route)**
   - Main Roads WA rest area guide (March 2015), map 11: Perth to Albany via Albany Highway 408 km, 4 h 43 min. Distance guide: Armadale 30, North Bannister 95, Bannister 108, Crossman 121, Williams 160.5, Arthur River 199, Boscabel (2 km off) 235, Kojonup 256, Cranbrook (4 km off) 319, Tenterden 328, Kendenup (4 km off) 342, Mount Barker 359, Redmond (8 km off) 391, Albany 408.
   - Wikipedia: 405.47 km from The Causeway to Albany (406.71 km in intersection table); mainly State Route 30; outside Perth a sealed two-lane single carriageway with overtaking lanes in some undulating areas; dual carriageway in Perth and Albany; roadhouses at North Bannister, Crossman and Arthur River; Williams, Kojonup and Mount Barker about 100 km apart.
   - OSRM: 426 km, 5 h 25 min via Armadale, Williams, Kojonup, Cranbrook, Mount Barker (Perth centre to Albany centre; the gap to Main Roads is the start and end points). Legs: Perth-Armadale 37 km 35 min; Armadale-Williams 131 km 1 h 34; Williams-Kojonup 95 km 1 h 09; Kojonup-Cranbrook 70 km 51 min; Cranbrook-Mount Barker 41 km 32 min; Mount Barker-Albany 51 km 42 min.
2. **South Western Highway via Pinjarra, Harvey, Donnybrook, Manjimup, Walpole, Denmark**
   - Main Roads WA guide: Perth to Bunbury 172 km (map 8, via Forrest Highway); Bunbury to Albany via South Western Highway 364 km, 4 h 40 min (map 10): Boyanup 21, Donnybrook 36, Balingup 66, Bridgetown 92, Manjimup 128, Walpole 248, Nornalup 257, Denmark 313, Albany 364. Perth to Albany this way about 536 km by adding the two.
   - OSRM (Pinjarra, Harvey, Donnybrook, Manjimup, Walpole, Denmark): 553 km, 7 h 34 min. Legs: Perth-Pinjarra 83; Pinjarra-Harvey 66; Harvey-Donnybrook 70; Donnybrook-Manjimup 93; Manjimup-Walpole 120 (1 h 37); Walpole-Denmark 66; Denmark-Albany 55 km 42 min.
   - Wikipedia: South Western Highway is 406 km, Armadale to Walpole, then continues as South Coast Highway; Bunbury to Walpole section sparsely populated and heavily forested, especially from Manjimup.
3. **Albany Highway to Mount Barker, Muirs Highway to Manjimup (a loop between the two)**
   - Wikipedia: Muir Highway 161 km, State Route 102, Manjimup to Mount Barker, single-carriageway bitumen, Rocky Gully only village, mainly freight route for plantation timber trucks and long interstate vehicles, through karri and jarrah forest, sparsely populated.
   - OSRM: Mount Barker-Manjimup 163 km, 2 h 04 min; Manjimup-Perth 294 km 3 h 41 min. A loop out by Albany Highway and back by Muirs, Manjimup and Donnybrook is about 508 km from Albany to Perth (6 h 28 min).

## 2. Legs and overnight stops (Albany Highway)
- Perth to Williams about 160 km (Main Roads 160.5 km). Williams to Kojonup 95 km (Main Roads 161 to 256). Kojonup to Mount Barker about 103 km (256 to 359). Mount Barker to Albany about 49 km (359 to 408).
- Day trip possible in one day (about 4.75 to 5.5 h). Natural overnight stops: Kojonup, Mount Barker (also Porongurup, Albany).
- **Kojonup**: Shire visitor site (kodjaplace.com.au): RV stay on the corner of Albany Highway and Gordon Street next to the Giant Wool Wagon; "The cost to stay is $5.00 per night"; "the maximum continuous stay is 3 nights" (same page also says RVs may use the area for 72 hours only); permit from the Kojonup Visitors Centre; "You must be self-sufficient"; generators not allowed; dump point and potable water nearby at the Railway Station Reserve.
- **Mount Barker**: Shire of Plantagenet visitor page lists Mount Barker Caravan Park (31720 Albany Highway), Porongurup Range Tourist Park (7 Box Hill Road), Kendenup Nature Based Camp Ground (Beverley Road, run by Kendenup Community Grounds Committee). "A dump point and potable water is available at the Mount Barker Visitor Centre on Albany Highway." Porongurup "approximately 20kms east of Mount Barker".
- **Albany**: BIG4 Emu Beach Holiday Park (operator page, big4emubeach.com.au/caravan-and-camping/: caravan and camping area, slabs for vans up to 8 m, a limited number of larger sites over 9 m). Fees not recorded.
- Williams: no park named (only Shire licensing pages found). Cranbrook: Shire of Cranbrook super rest bay article undated: NOT CONFIRMED.
- Rest areas (Main Roads 2015 map, names only, facilities not machine-readable): Glen Eagle Forest (km 54), Williams Lion (161), Martup Pool (223), Crapella (236), Tunney (294), Orup Creek (346/347). Main Roads: 24 hour rest areas "are not intended for camping and stopping is NOT to exceed more than 24 consecutive hours".
- Corridor data: AussieCamps data lists NO campground, dump point or rest area within 12 km of the Albany Highway route. On the South Western Highway route: Shannon Campground and Shannon Horse Camp (AU03391, AU03409, km 366, toilets, drinking water, showers, no dogs, camping fee), Banksia Camp and Black Point Campground (AU03315, AU03410, 8.9 km off, near Walpole), Belvidere Campground (AU03319, 12 km off).

## 3. Fuel and services
- Roadhouses on Albany Highway (Wikipedia): North Bannister, Crossman, Arthur River. Fuel availability and opening hours at each: NOT CONFIRMED.
- Longest gap between towns on the main route: Williams to Kojonup 95 km (Main Roads distance guide), with Arthur River roadhouse between. Armadale to Williams about 130 km with North Bannister and Crossman roadhouses between.
- South Western Highway: Manjimup to Walpole 120 km (OSRM), no town between in OSRM legs; Muirs Highway Rocky Gully only village (Wikipedia).
- Main Roads: "Pack extra food, water and fuel in case of emergency or breakdown, especially when rest stops are limited".

## 4. Road conditions
- Main Roads WA Travel Map (travelmap.mainroads.wa.gov.au), 138 138 (guide: general road enquiries, rest area maps, animals on the road). Travel Map has a rest area filter (Main Roads long-distance driving page).
- Main Roads guide: "Many of the major rural highways are unfenced"; "Be alert for animals on the road, particularly dusk and dawn"; "Slow down and sound your horn if you encounter an animal". Long-distance page: reduce speed near animals "particularly at dawn and dusk"; be ready to stop suddenly and avoid swerving. Road trains: "Only overtake when you have a long straight stretch of road ahead which is completely clear."
- UHF channel 40 for road user communication (2015 guide).
- Albany Ring Road: opened 19 May 2024 (buildingfortomorrow.wa.gov.au); diverts heavy vehicles; Menang Drive and Chester Pass Road flyover under construction, completion anticipated 2026 (page conflicts on start date).
- Specific current Albany Highway roadworks: NOT CONFIRMED (only undated news and a 2020 notice found). Tell reader to check Travel Map.
- Wikipedia: Perth end has many traffic lights; about 15 km in the south-east suburbs of 4 to 6 lanes.

## 5. Parks and side trips (DBCA Explore Parks WA)
- Entry (Stirling Range, Porongurup, Torndirrup all same): standard vehicle (12 or fewer occupants) $17.00, concession $10.00; large vehicle $8.00 per person, concession $3.50; motorcycle $10.00. "A valid park pass covers the fees." No dogs or other pets in any of the three.
- Stirling Range: Bluff Knoll "4.5 hours south of Perth and 1.5 hours from Albany"; 6.8 km return, Grade 4, 1,098 m, steep path with many steps; "rapid, unpredictable changes in the weather"; no walk time given; not to enter the bush or footpaths on extreme fire danger days. Moingup Spring Campground "Bookable" via ParkStay (AussieCamps AU03434: toilets, flush toilets, drinking water, no dogs, camping fee). Alerts: alerts.dbca.wa.gov.au. OSRM: Cranbrook to Moingup Spring 77 km 1 h 01; Moingup Spring to Albany 97 km 1 h 19.
- Porongurup: Castle Rock Trail 4.7 km return from Castle Rock picnic area, "Climb 670m to the top of Castle Rock on the Granite Skywalk"; Bolganup Heritage Trail 600 m loop; OSRM Mount Barker to Castle Rock 28 km 30 min.
- Torndirrup: The Gap and Natural Bridge "have reopened"; coast has "notorious record for accidents and deaths"; "Rock fishing is extremely dangerous on this coastline and is not recommended". OSRM Albany to Torndirrup (park centre) 17 km.
- H5 bird flu notice on all three pages: report sick or dead wildlife to 1800 675 888.

## 6. Fruit and plants (inside WA)
- DPIRD Queensland fruit fly South Perth outbreak: Quarantine Area Notice (consolidated, amended 22 September 2026): quarantine area is "within a 15-kilometre radius of the intersection of South Terrace and Sandgate Street, South Perth" until 11:59pm on 31 March 2027. Corrective action zone is the small area inside; export assurance zone is the rest. Clause 10(b): persons must not move fruit grown in the export assurance zone or host plants out of the quarantine area within WA except as the notice allows; clause 12 allows treated fruit (processing, cooking, freezing 24 hours) etc.; clause 14: fruit or host plants entering or transiting the quarantine area are moved in accordance with the notice "if moved under secure conditions in accordance with measures approved by the Department's Chief Plant Biosecurity Officer". Secure conditions (approved measures): unvented packages; vented packages with 1.6 mm mesh; packages enclosed under plastic wrap, tarpaulins, hessian or mesh; fully enclosed vehicles free of gaps over 1.6 mm. Failure to comply "could result in a fine".
- DPIRD page wording: Orange Zone is 15 km radius around detections (page says 15 km); host fruit "must not be moved outside of the Orange Zone unless they have been treated". Red Zone 1.5 km. DPIRD page last updated 21 April 2026 but lists September 2026 notice documents. Contact PaDIS (08) 9368 3080.
- DPIRD FAQ (Sept 2026): "No. You must not take host fruit and vegetables outside the Red Zone unless treated"; recommends home-grown and store-bought host fruit be refrigerated until eaten.
- DPIRD Medfly page: Medfly endemic to WA; "found throughout WA from Esperance through to Carnarvon"; absent from Ord River Irrigation Area; main infestation Perth to Bunbury, Bridgetown, Donnybrook, Manjimup. No traveller movement rule for Medfly found: NOT CONFIRMED.
