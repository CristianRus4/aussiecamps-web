## 1. Route options
- A. Bruce Highway (M1 to Curra, then A1) the whole way. Wikipedia: about 1,679 km, entirely sealed; dual carriageway from Brisbane to Curra (Gympie bypass opened 15 October 2024), mostly two-lane north of Curra. Speeds (Wikipedia): Caboolture to Curra 110 km/h; Maryborough to Rockhampton generally 100 km/h, 50 or 60 through towns such as Childers, Gin Gin, Miriam Vale, Mount Larcom.
  - OSRM direct 1,693 km, 21h57. OSRM via towns 1,736 km, 23h04 (Gympie, Bundaberg, Gladstone, Rockhampton, Mackay, Proserpine, Bowen, Townsville, Ingham, Innisfail).
  - Legs (OSRM, via towns): Brisbane to Gympie 166 km 2h09; to Bundaberg 196 km 2h33; to Gladstone 186 km 2h31; to Rockhampton 108 km 1h30; to Mackay 335 km 4h16; to Proserpine 125 km 1h39; to Bowen 67 km 0h54; to Townsville 204 km 2h44; to Ingham 113 km 1h31; to Innisfail 146 km 1h58; to Cairns 88 km 1h15.
  - Daily legs: Brisbane to Bundaberg 362 km 4h42; Bundaberg to Rockhampton 294 km 4h01; Rockhampton to Mackay 335 km 4h16; Mackay to Townsville 396 km 5h17; Townsville to Cairns 347 km 4h45.
- B. Inland variant: Bruce to Rockhampton, Capricorn Highway to Emerald, Gregory Highway to Clermont, Gregory Developmental Road to Charters Towers, Flinders Highway to Townsville, then the Bruce to Cairns. OSRM total 1,938 km, 26h04 (Brisbane to Rockhampton 629 km 8h05; Emerald 271 km 3h27; Clermont 164 km 2h30; Charters Towers 391 km 5h33; Townsville 136 km 1h42; Cairns 347 km 4h45). Rockhampton to Townsville is about 962 km this way against about 731 km on the Bruce (OSRM legs). So it adds about 245 km (1,938 minus 1,693).
  - Wikipedia: Capricorn Highway 575 km Rockhampton to Barcaldine (A4), Rockhampton to Gracemere duplicated mid-2021; Gregory Highway/Gregory Developmental Road about 930 km in total, northern 756 km is the Developmental Road, Clermont to Springsure 174 km is the Highway; "used by many road trains"; the sealing of the Clermont to Charters Towers section is not stated (the 2015 note is about the northern end). Do not say the road is fully sealed.
- No other real inland route found for Brisbane to Cairns; the inland variant is the only one described.

## 4. Road conditions
- QLDTraffic (qldtraffic.qld.gov.au, 200): traffic alerts for hazards, crashes, congestion, flooding, special events, roadworks; cameras including flood cameras; rest areas including heavy vehicle rest areas; layers include dump points, truck restrictions, BOM rain radar. Phone 13 19 40 (search extract of a Queensland Government road closure document; the QLDTraffic page itself does not list it).
- Get Ready Queensland (200): "Queensland's cyclone season officially runs from 1 November to 30 April, however cyclones can occur at other times." BOM extract: Queensland's severe weather season runs October to April. A Queensland road closure document (search extract): during the Wet, "from November to April", areas can flood or have land slips.
- Wikipedia (Bruce Highway): "prone to frequent flooding in a number of places"; Goorganga Plains south of Proserpine "can be closed for days"; Yeppen floodplain elevated crossing completed 2015 to 2016; Haughton River at Giru completed from July 2022. TMR (annual report 2020 project page, search extract): between Ingham and Innisfail flooding closes the Bruce Highway at one or more locations along a 16 km section "every 2 years" in the wet season, often more than once; Gairloch floodway usually closes first. No current closure was found for 21 October 2026; do not claim any road is open or closed today.
- Fatigue: TMR/Queensland police extracts: take a break every two hours at a rest area or Driver Reviver site; more than 450 rest areas; TMR is changing Driver Reviver management (some sites close but stay as rest areas), so do not name current sites.

## 5. Border and biosecurity
- Fire ants: Queensland zones updated effective 15 April 2026 across parts of Logan, Gold Coast, Moreton Bay, Lockyer Valley, Scenic Rim and Sunshine Coast council areas (finia.org.au extract; fireants.org.au). Carriers: baled hay or straw, manure, mulch, potted plants, quarry and mining materials, soil, turf; "Fire ants can hitch a ride in equipment or materials that have been in contact with soil or stored outdoors" (finia.org.au, 10 May 2026). Biosecurity Regulation 2016: procedures for moving or storing carriers from within the zones; otherwise a biosecurity instrument permit. Zones reviewed monthly. Search extracts do not mention caravans or camping gear. Phone numbers conflict (13 22 68 vs 13 25 23): leave out; point to fireants.org.au.
- Interstate Quarantine (Queensland): "The whole of the state is a biosecurity zone for bananas, grape plants, mangoes and sugarcane. Do not take any plants, soil or items that have been in contact with these plants into Queensland." Melon Thrips North Queensland Restricted Area covers "areas inland and south of Cooktown, including Mossman, Cairns, Innisfail and Townville, down to Bowen" (from the cairns-to-darwin facts file; the page returned 200).
