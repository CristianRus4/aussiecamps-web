# Alice Springs to Uluru drive: facts (slug alice-springs-to-uluru-drive)

Researched 2026-10-21. OSRM = "OSRM routing on OpenStreetMap data, computed 21 October 2026" (car, no stops, centre to centre). Whole route is inside the Northern Territory: no state border. Say "about" on every distance.

## 1. Route options
1. **Stuart Highway south to Erldunda, then Lasseter Highway west to Yulara (main route)**
   - OSRM: 452 km, 5 h 11 min. Legs: Alice Springs-Erldunda 203 km 2 h 09; Erldunda-Curtin Springs 164 km 2 h 00; Curtin Springs-Yulara 85 km 1 h 02. Yulara-Kata Tjuta (centre point) 52 km 44 min (OSRM, centre point only).
   - Discover Central Australia fuel page (regional tourism site): Erldunda 200 km from Alice Springs and 246 km from Yulara; Curtin Springs 361 km from Alice Springs and 85 km from Yulara. Sum Alice to Yulara via Erldunda about 446 km. The same page says Curtin Springs is "100 km west of Erldunda": inconsistent with its own 361 and 200 figures (161 km); do not use 100.
   - Wikipedia Lasseter Highway: 244 km, "fully sealed", Erldunda (Stuart Highway A87) to Tjukaruru Road at the national park; Yulara, Curtin Springs, Mount Ebenezer settlements.
2. **Red Centre Way via Kings Canyon (comparison)**: OSRM Alice Springs-Kings Canyon Resort-Yulara 633 km, 7 h 15 min (Alice-Kings Canyon 330 km 3 h 41; Kings Canyon-Yulara 304 km 3 h 34). OSRM's road names: Stuart Highway, Ernest Giles Road, Luritja Road. Surface of Ernest Giles Road: NOT CONFIRMED. Discover Central Australia permits page: Mereenie Loop "the unsealed section of the Red Centre Way", between Kings Canyon Resort and the Larapinta Drive/Namatjira Drive intersection; Mereenie Loop permit $5.00, valid 3 days from purchase, in person at Alice Springs Visitor Information Centre (corner Todd Mall and Parsons Street) and other outlets at varying prices; "A permit is required to visit any Aboriginal land in the Northern Territory." Tourism Central Australia Uluru camping page: Kings Canyon Resort "about three and a half hours away via Luritja Road". Link red-centre-way-camping; do not repeat it.

## 3. Fuel
- No fuel stop listed between Alice Springs and Erldunda (Discover Central Australia fuel page covers Lasseter Highway stops only): 200 km. Erldunda to Curtin Springs about 161 km (361 minus 200). Curtin Springs to Yulara 85 km.
- Page advice: plan ahead if towing a caravan or driving a motorhome or travelling outside business hours; enough fuel for the next stop with a margin; check hours; MyFuel NT for prices.
- Tourism NT roads page: "Keep your tank full, since fuel may not be available in remote areas. Remote stations have limited hours and banking, and prices vary... Take extra fuel if you're going to remote areas."

## 4. Road conditions and rules (Tourism NT roads page, northernterritory.com/plan/useful-information/safety-information/roads)
- "The speed limit on open roads is 110km/h unless sign posted otherwise." "On the Stuart, Barkly, Victoria and Arnhem Highways the maximum speed limit is 130km/h." Lasseter Highway not in that list.
- Road Report NT: www.roadreport.nt.gov.au or 1800 246 199.
- "During wet season/tropical summer (November to April) some roads may be closed due to flooding"; do not travel on closed roads (heavy fines may apply).
- Road trains "can have three trailers and be over 50m long and 2.5m wide"; "Allow plenty of room before you overtake as they may sway from side to side."
- "Never swerve to avoid animals when driving as this may cause a crash." "Animals are most active at sunrise, sunset or at night."
- Driving tired: 15-minute break every two hours.
- Unsealed roads: 2WD can be driven on most gravel roads, 4WD best for dirt roads; in wet weather gravel may need 4WD.

## 5. Uluru-Kata Tjuta National Park (Parks Australia; uluru.gov.au/plan/passes and Visitor Guide PDF)
- Pass: adult (18+) 3-day $38; annual $50; NT annual vehicle $109 (vehicle registered in the NT, not a tour or hire vehicle); under 18 free. Buy at book.parksaustralia.gov.au/passes/uluru/; if you can't buy online you can get a pass at the entry station; QR code scanned in the left-hand lane. Covers this park only, not Kakadu.
- Visitor guide hours: December, January, February 5:00 am to 9:00 pm; March 5:30 am-8:30 pm; April 5:30 am-8:00 pm; May 6:00 am-7:30 pm; June, July 6:30 am-7:30 pm; August 6:00 am-7:30 pm; September 5:30 am-7:30 pm; October 5:00 am-8:00 pm; November 5:00 am-8:30 pm. "The park closes overnight, and there is no camping permitted in the park. Camping is available at the resort." Cultural Centre 7:00 am-6:00 pm. Ranger-guided Mala walk free: October to April 8:00 am, 1.5 to 2 hours, Mala carpark. "NO DRONE" signs on the guide's maps. Contact (08) 8956 1128.
- Heat: "Temperatures in the park regularly reach 30°C or more, and can exceed 40°C during summer." Tips: walk only in cooler parts of the day, avoid 2:30 pm to 6:30 pm; "In summer we strongly recommend not walking after 11:00 am"; at least one litre of water per person per hour; do not walk on tracks closed due to heat limits.
- Guide PDF is undated (ref UKTNPVG112020_ES in file); pass prices match the passes page.
