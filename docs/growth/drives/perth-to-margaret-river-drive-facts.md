# Perth to Margaret River drive: facts (slug perth-to-margaret-river-drive)

Researched 2026-10-21. OSRM = "OSRM routing on OpenStreetMap data, computed 21 October 2026" (car, no stops, no caravan, centre to centre). Whole route inside Western Australia: no state border. Say "about" on every distance. Do not repeat south-west-wa-camping-loop (camps, karri forest, Cape to Cape, sharks); Albany side is in perth-to-albany-drive.

## 1. Route options
1. **Kwinana Freeway, Forrest Highway, Wilman Wadandi Highway or Bunbury, Bussell Highway (main route)**
   - Main Roads WA rest area guide (March 2015), map 8 Perth to Augusta via Forrest Highway and Bussell Highway: 315 km, 3 h 44 min (Augusta). Distance guide: Mandurah 64, Bunbury 172, Capel turn 199, Busselton 224, Carbunup River 242, Cowaramup 261, Margaret River 273, Witchcliffe 281, Karridale 301, Augusta 315. So Perth to Margaret River 273 km by that guide (1 km = 0.6 mi etc not needed).
   - OSRM: 287 km, 3 h 55 min (Perth, Mandurah, Bunbury, Busselton, Margaret River). Legs: Perth-Mandurah 72 km 57 min; Mandurah-Bunbury 111 km 1 h 26; Bunbury-Busselton 54 km 46 min; Busselton-Margaret River 50 km 44 min.
   - Wikipedia: Kwinana Freeway 72 km, controlled access, 110 km/h south of Safety Bay Road (80 north of South Terrace, 100 north of Safety Bay Road); Forrest Highway 95.67 km, two lanes each way with median, 110 km/h, opened 20 September 2009, twin roadhouses opened late 2017 about 5 km south of Greenlands Road, John Tognela Rest Area near the southern end; Bussell Highway about 140 km Bunbury to Augusta, State Route 10, dual carriageway Bunbury to Capel and in Busselton, single carriageway Capel to Busselton? (Wikipedia says single from Capel to Busselton and Vasse to Augusta with overtaking lanes: out of date, see duplication below).
   - Main Roads: Wilman Wadandi Highway (Bunbury Outer Ring Road): "27-kilometre four-lane, high standard road" linking Forrest Highway to Bussell Highway, complete, opened December 2024, saves "11 to 18 minutes"; works Paris Road to Bussell Highway until late August 2026, 80 km/h (page updated 8 May 2026).
   - Main Roads Bussell Highway Duplication page: "now complete with all four lanes open to traffic on 17 April 2025"; some finishing work expected to continue (page says mid-2025). Building for Tomorrow: 17 km, complete April 2025, 46 km Bunbury-Busselton unimpeded section gets Regional Road Safety Program works.
2. **South Western Highway via Pinjarra, Harvey, Donnybrook, then to Busselton**
   - OSRM: Perth-Pinjarra 83; Pinjarra-Harvey 66; Harvey-Donnybrook 70; Donnybrook-Busselton 77 = 297 km, 4 h 05 min to Busselton, against 237 km by the main route. About 60 km longer. Wikipedia: South Western Highway 406 km Armadale to Walpole.
   - Vasse Highway (Wikipedia): 151 km, Bussell Highway about 3 km east of Busselton to South Western Highway near Diamond Tree, 15 km south of Manjimup, via Nannup and Pemberton, State Routes 104 and 10, jarrah and karri forest. It is the forest road from Busselton to Nannup and Pemberton, not a shortcut from Perth: NOT a faster alternative.
3. **Caves Road finish (Busselton, Dunsborough, Yallingup, Margaret River)**
   - Wikipedia: Caves Road 111 km, Abbey to Augusta, alternative to Bussell Highway, "a narrow winding road in most parts", forms most of Tourist Drive 250 (Cape Naturaliste to Cape Leeuwin).
   - OSRM Busselton-Dunsborough 25 km 26 min; Dunsborough-Yallingup 9 km 10 min; Yallingup-Caves Road at Redgate 43 km 41 min; Redgate-Margaret River 11 km 12 min = about 88 km, 1 h 30 min vs 50 km 43 min on Bussell Highway. Margaret River-Cape Leeuwin 52 km 46 min.
   - DBCA: Mammoth Cave "11km south of Margaret River along Caves Road"; Jewel Cave "about 45km south of Margaret River via Bussel Highway and Caves Road".

## 2. Legs
- Perth to Bunbury 172 km (Main Roads); Bunbury to Busselton about 52 km (Main Roads 172 to 224); Busselton to Margaret River 49 km (224 to 273). One day easily; no overnight needed.

## 5. Leeuwin-Naturaliste National Park (DBCA Explore Parks WA)
- "No park entry fee. Entry to Leeuwin-Naturaliste National Park is free." "No dogs (or other pets) allowed in this park". Cave pages (Mammoth, Lake, Jewel, Ngilgi): "No park entry fee / Admission fees apply"; opening hours and fees via the page link (operator). Jewel and Ngilgi: bookings can be made via the link. Lake Cave one-hour guided tour; Jewel one-hour guided tour; Mammoth self-guided audio tour.
- Park page lists bookable campgrounds: Conto, Point Road, Boranup (7 campsites, tents and small campervans only), Jarrahdene; Hamelin Bay Holiday Park "privately operated". Campground pages: "Bookings essential"; Boranup, Point Road and Jarrahdene bookable up to 180 days ahead, one campsite only; no-flush pit toilets; drinking water not provided; no dogs; generator use not permitted (Boranup, Point Road, Jarrahdene); Jarrahdene has a chemical toilet dump point; campfires only in fire rings in April to November usually (Boranup, Point Road, Jarrahdene); Conto: campfires, solid fuel barbecues, cookers and heaters not permitted in most areas mid-November to mid-April; "December to April can be extremely hot and the risk of bushfire can be very high" (Boranup page). Camping fee per person, no vehicle entry fee, no booking fee, paid by Mastercard or VISA.
- Cape to Cape Track 130 km per DBCA (loop page says 123): not used.
- H5 bird flu notice: report to 1800 675 888.

## 7. Dump points
- Shire of Capel: Buchanan Road, off Capel Drive; lock with access code (page gives code, not printed here); "nothing but effluent"; no fee or hours listed; potable water same code; (08) 9727 0222.
- City of Busselton: Churchill Park, corner of Brown Street and Adelaide Street; lock and code; same code for water; City tourism page: free public dump point behind Churchill Park on Adelaide Street next to Busselton Jetty Tourist Park; most caravan parks in the City provide dump points for visitors.
- Shire of Augusta Margaret River: Margaret River dump point at the western end of Gloucester Park next to the Recreation Centre, "operational all year and free of charge"; Cowaramup on Hall Road between the Cowaramup District Hall and the outdoor basketball courts, "operational all year and free of charge".
- Data coverage: Boyanup-Picton Road, Dardanup (AU19119); Snells Park, Harvey (AU12154, free, washout tap).
