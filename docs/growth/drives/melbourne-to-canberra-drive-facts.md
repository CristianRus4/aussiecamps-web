# Melbourne to Canberra drive: facts (slug melbourne-to-canberra-drive)

Researched 2026-10-21. OSRM = "OSRM routing on OpenStreetMap data, computed 21 October 2026" (car, no stops, no caravan). Source numbers [n] are in section 9.

## 1. Route options

**A. Hume Freeway/Highway via Euroa, Benalla, Wangaratta, Albury, Holbrook, Gundagai to Yass, then Barton Highway to Canberra**
- OSRM: 680 km, 8h35 (waypoints in Euroa, Wangaratta, Albury, Holbrook, Gundagai, Yass). Using OSRM's direct Melbourne to Albury leg (315 km, 3h53) plus Albury to Canberra (353 km, 4h23) gives 668 km, 8h16. [1]
- Hume Highway: 110 km/h on its full length; dual carriageway for its full length since the Holbrook bypass opened 7 August 2013 (Wikipedia). [2]
- Barton Highway: 52 km from the Hume Highway at Yass to Lyneham ACT (Wikipedia). About 42 km is in NSW, and only about 6 km of that is dual carriageway (Wikipedia). It is single carriageway over most of the NSW section. Route number A25. [3]
- Towns: Craigieburn, Seymour, Euroa, Benalla, Wangaratta, Albury-Wodonga, Holbrook, Tarcutta, Gundagai, Coolac, Yass, Murrumbateman. [2][3]

**B. Alpine route: Murray Valley Highway via Albury and Corryong, over the Snowy Mountains, then Cooma and the Monaro Highway**
- Melbourne to Corryong: 315 km (3h53) to Albury plus 128 km (1h49), OSRM. Corryong to Khancoban 29 km (0h24), OSRM. [1]
- Alpine Way Khancoban to Jindabyne: about 107 km (Wikipedia gives the road as 121.3 km with Khancoban at 106.9 km from the Jindabyne end). Jindabyne to Cooma 61 km (0h52), Cooma to Canberra 113 km (1h36), OSRM. [1][4]
- Derived total: about 753 km (sum of OSRM legs and the Wikipedia Alpine Way length). Not a routed figure.
- OSRM's own Corryong to Cooma (186 km, 3h05) runs through Cabramurra and Kiandra (Khancoban to Cabramurra road). That road is closed in winter (see section 4), so do not quote that OSRM total as a drivable route year-round. [1][5]
- Alpine Way: "steep, narrow and winding" Khancoban to Thredbo, rockfalls after rain, not recommended for large caravans (NPWS, search extract). [6]

**C. Princes Highway to Cann River, then Monaro Highway via Bombala, Nimmitabel, Cooma**
- OSRM: 747 km, 10h35 (Bairnsdale 281 km, Orbost 90, Cann River 83, Bombala 92, Cooma 87, Canberra 113). [1]
- Monaro Highway: 285.1 km from Cann River to Morshead Drive, Canberra; Cooma to Canberra 111.6 km (Wikipedia). [7]
- Variant over Brown Mountain via Bega to Cooma: OSRM 846 km, 11h59. [1]

## 2. Legs and overnight stops (route A)
- Day 1: Melbourne to Albury, 315 km, 3h53 (OSRM direct leg). [1]
- Day 2: Albury to Canberra, 353 km, 4h23 (63 to Holbrook, 115 to Gundagai, 107 to Yass, 68 to Canberra). [1]
- Most drivers can do it in one day: 680 km, 8h35 without stops. Say 8h35 is driving time only.

**Albury** (Albury-Wodonga)

**Gundagai**

**Canberra end**
- ACT Parks campgrounds in the data: Cotter Campground AU20838 (toilets, drinking water, showers); Northern Border Campground AU20841; Mount Clear Campground AU20840; Honeysuckle Campground AU20839.

Groups need a permit. Namadgi Visitor Centre (02) 6237 5307. Fee amounts NOT CONFIRMED (the ACT Parks page returned 403; a directory figure was unverified). [11]

## 4. Road conditions and seasonal closures
- Live sources: VicTraffic (URL not opened); Live Traffic NSW, https://www.livetraffic.com (200); ACT roads: not researched. NPWS tells drivers to use Live Traffic for conditions (132 701). [6]
- Snow chains (NSW Government and NPWS): "all 2WD vehicles" must carry chains "between the June long weekend and the October long weekend" on Kosciuszko Road from the park boundary at Thredbo River to Perisher Valley, Alpine Way between Thredbo and Tom Groggin, and Island Bend/Guthega Road. Recommended, not mandatory: Alpine Way between Jindabyne and Thredbo, and the Snowy Mountains Highway. 4WD/AWD: "do not require chains under National Parks regulations" but carrying is recommended. Vehicles with snow tyres must still carry chains. [5][14]
- The Wikipedia Alpine Way page links the October date to Labour Day. NSW Labour Day 2026 is Monday 5 October (derived from the NSW daylight saving start on Sunday 4 October); the rule has ended for 2026 and returns at the June long weekend 2027. [4][18]
- NPWS: "The road from Khancoban to Cabramurra and many of Kosciuszko National Park's minor roads are closed in winter." "Roads can close in extreme weather because of rain, wind and sub-zero temperatures." No closure dates given. [5]
- Kings Highway (Canberra to Batemans Bay): no source found; not on any route here. NOT CONFIRMED.
- Tolls, Melbourne: Linkt Visitor's Pass covers CityLink, EastLink and the West Gate Tunnel; valid up to 30 days; plate and card; opening fee $3.50 online ($5.00 phone) plus a video matching fee by road; covers CityLink trips up to 5 days ago and EastLink trips up to 3 days ago; after more than 5 days an account can cover CityLink trips up to 9 days back. [15] Wikipedia's Hume Highway page describes no tolls on the Hume itself (it names only the Sydney M5 and M7). Whether your Melbourne start needs CityLink depends on the route to the Hume: NOT CONFIRMED.
- Speed: Hume Highway 110 km/h full length. [2]
- Roadworks: Wikipedia notes grade-separated interchanges being built at Lanyon Drive and Isabella Drive on the Monaro Highway near Hume (ACT); no dates. NOT CONFIRMED. [7]

## 5. State border rules
- Interstate Quarantine (undated page, copyright 2026): New South Wales "No fruit fly restrictions are listed" (its NSW zones cover banana bunchy top virus, phylloxera, citrus red mite, rice and potatoes). Victoria: no fruit fly zones listed (restrictions cover phylloxera, potato Plant Protection Districts and the Toolangi district). The ACT is grouped with NSW and no separate ACT zones or fruit fly restrictions are listed. [16]
- Say only that: do not claim "the ACT has no biosecurity rules". No ACT government source on carrying fruit was found. NOT CONFIRMED.
- Agriculture Victoria (search extract): the Greater Sunraysia Pest Free Area regulation lapsed in July 2024; Mediterranean fruit fly restrictions still apply statewide. [17]
- The same site says "at some stage when travelling in Australia you are likely to be inspected or asked to surrender produce in an amnesty bin". [16]
- Red imported fire ants: Queensland and northern NSW only; not on these routes.

## 7. Other facts a driver needs
- Time zones: Victoria, NSW and the ACT all observe daylight saving; it began Sunday 4 October 2026 and ends Sunday 4 April 2027 at 3am AEDT. No time change on any route. [18]
- National park entry fees apply at Kosciuszko National Park campgrounds listed above ("Park entry fee" in the data); amount not checked. NPWS fee page not opened. NOT CONFIRMED.
- ACT camping: Namadgi campgrounds are booked online with ACT Parks; see camping-rules-act.
