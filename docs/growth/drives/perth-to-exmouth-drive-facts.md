# Perth to Exmouth drive: facts (slug perth-to-exmouth-drive)

Researched 2026-10-21. OSRM = "OSRM routing on OpenStreetMap data, computed 21 October 2026" (car, no stops, no caravan, centre to centre). The whole route is inside Western Australia, so there is no state border and no interstate quarantine check. Say "about" on every distance. Several local sources are dated 2026 and reflect Severe Tropical Cyclone Narelle damage (27 March 2026): see section 4.

## 1. Route options

1. **Brand Highway to Geraldton, then North West Coastal Highway (main route; the one asked for)**
   - OSRM: 1,256 km, 14 h 54 min via Perth, Jurien Bay, Geraldton, Overlander Roadhouse junction, Carnarvon, Exmouth. Via Cervantes, Minilya and Coral Bay waypoints OSRM gives 1,283 km, 15 h 23 min (the extra km is the Coral Bay waypoint detour).
   - Brief figure "about 1,270 km" sits inside the OSRM range of 1,256 to 1,283 km. No road authority Perth to Exmouth total was found. Print "about 1,260 to 1,280 km".
   - Road type: Wikipedia (secondary) says the North West Coastal Highway is a sealed two-lane single carriageway, 1,299.06 km long (Geraldton to Port Hedland end), most of it 110 km/h, built-up areas 50, 60 or 70 km/h. No road authority page for the Brand Highway was opened.
   - OSRM legs: Perth-Jurien Bay 218 km 2h42; Jurien-Geraldton 199 km 2h21; Geraldton-Overlander junction 279 km 3h18; Overlander-Carnarvon 197 km 2h16; Carnarvon-Exmouth 364 km 4h14.
2. **Same route with the Kalbarri detour (Geraldton, Northampton, Kalbarri, back to the North West Coastal Highway)**
   - OSRM: Geraldton-Kalbarri 156 km 1h57, Kalbarri-Overlander junction 245 km 2h50 (401 km total, against 279 km direct, so about 120 km extra). This is OSRM only; no tourism body figure was opened.
   - Wikipedia: Binnu roadhouse is 11 km south of the Kalbarri Road turn-off. Treat as secondary.
3. **Inland via Great Northern Highway (Perth, Bindoon, Dalwallinu, Eneabba, Geraldton)**
   - OSRM: Perth to Geraldton 548 km, 7 h 46 min through Bindoon, Dalwallinu, Eneabba. Main Roads WA's 2015 rest area guide has a separate map for "Perth to Geraldton (via Great Northern Highway and Brand Highway)" (map 02), so it is a real route; no more detail researched. Mention in one line.
4. **Indian Ocean Drive (coastal, Perth to Cervantes/Jurien Bay)**
   - Main Roads WA's guide has a map "Perth to Geraldton (via Indian Ocean Drive and Brand Highway)" (map 01). No distance or time sourced: leave the figures out.

Carnarvon to Exmouth: the Minilya-Exmouth Road leaves the highway about 7 km north of Minilya Roadhouse (Wikipedia). A search extract of a travel guide says the Minilya-Exmouth Road runs another 78 km before the turn left to Coral Bay on Coral Bay Road (not official). OSRM: Carnarvon-Minilya 140 km 1h39; Minilya-Exmouth 224 km 2h36 (Minilya-Coral Bay 98 km 1h09; Coral Bay-Exmouth 152 km 1h47). Wikipedia says Minilya is 135 km beyond Carnarvon.

## 2. Legs and overnight stops
Suggested split (OSRM times, no stops): Day 1 Perth to Geraldton (about 417 km, 5h03 via Jurien: 218+199), Day 2 Geraldton to Carnarvon (about 475 km, Main Roads WA), Day 3 Carnarvon to Exmouth (about 364 km, 4h14). A longer trip: add Jurien Bay or Cervantes on day 1, and Coral Bay on day 3.
- Main Roads WA: Geraldton to Carnarvon is 475 km (its 2015 distance guide). Northampton is 52 km from Geraldton, Galena rest area 113 km, Nerren Nerren 187 km, Edagee 398 km, Carnarvon 475 km.
- **Perth to Cervantes / Jurien Bay**: Cervantes (the Pinnacles side) is about 197 km from Perth by OSRM; Jurien Bay about 218 km. Dump point in data: Cadiz Street, Cervantes WA, AU16659 (Free; drinking water, toilets, accessible toilet; no washout tap listed). No caravan park confirmed on an operator page, so name none.
- **Geraldton**: city, last big centre before Carnarvon. No park researched on an operator page: leave out names. Dump point in data: Hampton Gardens, Northampton WA, AU17304 (Free; drinking water, toilets), about 52 km north.
- **Overlander / Billabong turn-off for Shark Bay**: Wikipedia says the Overlander Roadhouse and the Billabong roadhouse are at the same location, 50 km south of the Shark Bay turn-off. Do not give a km from Geraldton other than OSRM (279 km to the Overlander junction).
- **Carnarvon**: the Shire of Carnarvon visitor message is "YOU ARE WELCOME IN CARNARVON, BUT PLEASE DON'T BRING YOUR FRUIT" (see section 5). No park named: not confirmed on an operator page.
- **Minilya Roadhouse**: Main Roads WA marks Minilya as a roadhouse on its Geraldton to Port Hedland map; 609 km from Geraldton on that map.
- **Coral Bay**: Search extract of Australia's Coral Coast update (page stamped 30/06/26): Coral Bay Peoples Park and Ningaloo Coral Bay (Bayview) listed open. A camplify blog (secondary) says Coral Bay has "just two caravan parks" that book out: say "check availability".
- **Exmouth**: see section 4 for what is open after Cyclone Narelle. Australia's Coral Coast page lists RAC Exmouth Cape Holiday Park partially open, Exmouth Escape Resort, Ningaloo Lodge Exmouth, Sal Salis Ningaloo Reef, Camp Easy Ningaloo open (undated list, page stamp 30/06/26). Shire emergency overflow camping facility opened 29 April 2026 (see below).
- **Campgrounds in AussieCamps data near the end of the route (Ningaloo coast, not roadside)**: Sandy Point (Nyinggulu) AU12036; Stans Camp - overflow camping AU12170; The Ridge AU11990; 14 Mile AU11945 (all about 1,104 km along, 7 to 10 km off route, Warroora coast); Bruboodjoo AU11969 (about 1,160 km, 7.9 km off route). Data lists no facilities. DBCA says 14 Mile sites 12 to 28 are closed and the whole campground closes from 1 January 2027 for cyclone recovery works.

**Rest areas in AussieCamps data (stops for a break, not beds)**: Nilgen, Gingin AU08408; Galena South AU08474 and Galena North AU08085, Northampton; Nerren Nerren, Shark Bay AU08221; Edagee, Carnarvon AU09060; Yalabia, Carnarvon AU07624. Each lists Toilets, Shelter, Picnic tables, Accessible toilet, Dump point; all Free. Main Roads WA rest areas marked on its map also include Minilya, Lyndon, Burkett Road (north of Minilya, not on the Exmouth road).
- Rules: Main Roads WA says "There are some 24-hour rest areas across the state, however these are not intended for camping" and "stopping at these areas should not exceed 24 consecutive hours." The AussieCamps guide says WA allows up to 24 consecutive hours only where signed and no overnight stop without a sign. Link: can-i-stay-overnight-at-a-rest-area-in-australia, camping-rules-western-australia.
- Heavy vehicle rest areas are for trucks (Main Roads WA: about 200 of its rest areas are heavy vehicle rest areas, as quoted in the AussieCamps can-i guide).

## 3. Fuel and services
- Main Roads WA long distance driving page: "Extra fuel if travelling long distances" and "Enough food and water to last for 2 to 3 days past your intended travel time". That page gives no roadhouse list or fuel-gap distances.
- Main Roads WA rest area guide (March 2015, so dated; says "data can be altered"): "Pack extra food, water and fuel in case of emergency or breakdown, especially when rest stops are limited". Its Geraldton to Port Hedland map marks roadhouses at Northampton (town), Billabong, Overlander, Wooramel, Carnarvon, Minilya, Nanutarra and others.
- Longest stretch without a town on this route: Geraldton to Carnarvon, 475 km (Main Roads WA). Wikipedia says that apart from Northampton, 50 km out, "the only settlements over this 475-kilometre stretch are four roadhouses" (it names Binnu, Billabong, Overlander, Wooramel). Fuel availability and hours at each roadhouse are NOT CONFIRMED: tell readers to fill up at Geraldton and check at Billabong and Overlander.
- Carnarvon to Exmouth: Minilya Roadhouse is the only roadhouse before the Exmouth turn-off (Minilya about 140 km from Carnarvon by OSRM, 135 km per Wikipedia). Next fuel by data: Coral Bay (98 km further by OSRM) and Exmouth. Fuel at Coral Bay is NOT CONFIRMED on an operator page. Do not give a "longest fuel gap" km: no operator or road authority gave one.
- Main Roads WA lists the road user radio channel (UHF channel 40) in its 2015 guide for the Geraldton to Port Hedland route.

## 4. Road conditions, seasonal risks and the 2026 cyclone recovery
- Live source: Main Roads WA Travel Map (https://travelmap.mainroads.wa.gov.au/, returned 200) and phone 138 138. Main Roads long distance driving page: "Use the Travel Map to plan your journey and to check for any road closures which may affect your travels."
- Wet season: Main Roads WA says "The wet season in the north of WA generally runs from November to April". Cyclone season 1 November to 30 April is from a search extract of a 2018 BoM outlook (bom.gov.au returned 403 to script): say "BoM's tropical cyclone season runs 1 November to 30 April". The Gascoyne coast is within a region BoM's North West manager calls the most cyclone-prone in the country (SES WA item, search extract: "Broome to Exmouth").
- Cyclone Narelle (Shire of Exmouth and Australia's Coral Coast page): hit 27 March 2026. Main Roads "announced on Wednesday 8 April 2026 that they have lifted road restrictions into Exmouth." Australia's Coral Coast page (stamped 30/06/26): North West Coastal Highway reopened from the south into Carnarvon on 31 March; all Coral Coast roads open as at 10/04/26; Minilya-Exmouth Road open "with caution". Say the date, never "now open".
- Exmouth, Shire page (no date shown): "The Shire of Exmouth is re-open to visitors." "accommodation options remain limited as recovery and rebuilding continues." "We urge visitors to ensure they have confirmed with their accommodation provider PRIOR to arrival." Horizon Power scheduled outages across the town. Shire phone (08) 9949 3000. Shire Emergency Overflow Camping Facility opened (details on the Shire camping page; Coral Coast page says 29 April 2026, for self-contained campers).
- Animals: Main Roads WA 2015 guide: "Many of the major rural highways are unfenced... Be alert for animals on the road, particularly dusk and dawn." Road trains: Main Roads WA: "Avoid following too closely behind", "Only overtake when you have a long straight stretch of road ahead which is completely clear", "Do not cut in front of them". ## 5. Quarantine and biosecurity (all inside WA)
- No border checkpoint applies to a driver from Perth. The WA road checkpoints (Eucla, Kununurra) are on the state border; see broome-to-darwin-drive-facts.md.
- Carnarvon: the Shire of Carnarvon page says "Medfly is present in all parts of Western Australia except for the Ord River Irrigation Area near Kununurra". It asks visitors from elsewhere in WA not to bring fruit or vegetables: "Either eat it or dispose of your fruit before you arrive in Carnarvon, or better yet, buy as you go". It says to bag fruit and use "the yellow rubbish bins located in the parking bays outside of Carnarvon". The page mentions no road checkpoint and no formal restriction: it is advisory. Write it as the Shire's request.
- A DPIRD page for the Carnarvon Mediterranean fruit fly eradication pilot says the pilot finished 30 June 2019 (marked possibly out of date). No current formal Gascoyne movement order for travellers was found: NOT CONFIRMED.
- Perth Queensland fruit fly notices were temporary; none confirmed current: NOT CONFIRMED. Tell readers to check DPIRD (dpird.wa.gov.au) before leaving Perth.

## 7. Cape Range National Park, Ningaloo coast stays and other facts
- Time zone: WA is on Australian Western Standard Time all year. No time change on this route. Daylight saving not used in WA: general knowledge, not from a fetched source.
- Cape Range National Park entry (Explore Parks WA): standard vehicle (12 or fewer occupants) $17.00 per vehicle, concession $10.00; motorcycle $10.00; "A valid park pass covers the park entry fees." "No dogs (or other pets) allowed in this park." (page undated; fees may change). Milyering Discovery Centre: 08 9947 8070, open 9:00 to 3:45, 7 days (DBCA guide, September 2026). Closures: alerts.dbca.wa.gov.au.
- Bookable Cape Range campgrounds (Explore Parks WA): Osprey Bay, Yardie Creek, Kurrajong, Neds, North Mandu, Bungarra, Mesa, One K, Boat Harbour, Tulki Beach. North Kurrajong is for outdoor education and commercial tour groups only. ParkStay search extracts: Kurrajong 35 reverse-in sites, no campfires, no dogs, no generators; Yardie Creek 10 sites, pit toilets only, adjacent to the Osprey Sanctuary Zone. Yardie Creek crossing to Boat Harbour and One K needs a low tide (search extract, ParkStay listings).
- DBCA "Guide to booking a campsite in the Ningaloo Coast World Heritage Area" (information current at September 2026): book at parkstay.dbca.wa.gov.au. Bookings open at 10:00am AWST on the first Tuesday of the month. Table in the guide: stays in April 2027 open 6 October 2026; May 2027 open 3 November 2026; June 2027 open 1 December 2026; July 2027 open 5 January 2027; August 2027 open 2 February 2027; September (to 24 September) open 2 March 2027; October 2027 open 6 April 2027. Max stay: up to 14 nights if any night is in a WA school holiday, up to 28 nights otherwise. "You must not book a campsite that you will not be camping on yourself." WA school holidays named: 10 to 26 April 2027, 3 to 18 July 2027, 25 September to 10 October 2027. Guide says all Ningaloo Coast campgrounds were affected by Severe Tropical Cyclone Narelle in 2026: check ParkStay updates.
- Nyinggulara National Park (Janes Bay, North Lefroy, Point Billie, South Lefroy, Winderabandi): ParkStay says all campsites closed until further notice. 14 Mile Campground: sites 12 to 28 closed; whole campground closed from 1 January 2027 for cyclone recovery works. Boat Harbour and One K: inaccessible, estimated reopening 1 August (Coral Coast page, 21/04/26 DBCA note; check current).
- Ningaloo station stays (search extracts): Warroora Station (homestead camp separate from DBCA coastal sites; Coral Coast page lists it open, undated); Bullara Station Stay on Burkett Road about 65 km from Coral Bay on the Exmouth side (anycamp listing; Coral Coast page lists open, undated). Prices and availability: NOT CONFIRMED, tell readers to ring.
- Related guides: perth-to-broome-road-trip (covers the coast north of Carnarvon; keep Exmouth focus here), wet-season-camping-top-end-northern-wa, road-trip-fuel-planning-australia, interstate-biosecurity-road-trip, christmas-summer-camping-australia-2026-27.
