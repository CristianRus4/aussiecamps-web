## 1. Route
- Only one realistic route: Flinders Highway (A6) Townsville to Cloncurry, then Barkly Highway (A2) Cloncurry to Mount Isa. OSRM direct Townsville to Mount Isa 905 km, 11h06. RACQ (fetched): "The Overlanders Way from Townsville to Mount Isa is approximately 905km." Best time "April through to October" (RACQ). Wikipedia (Flinders Highway, Queensland): 775 km Townsville to Cloncurry, sealed throughout (last section completed November 1976), A6, formerly National Route 78, also known as Overlanders Way; "From Cloncurry, the Barkly Highway (National Route A2) runs west to Mount Isa". Wikipedia (Overlanders Way): State Strategic Touring Route, Townsville to Tennant Creek via Flinders and Barkly Highways.
- Legs (OSRM, town centre to town centre, sum 909 km): Townsville to Charters Towers 136 km 1h41; Charters Towers to Hughenden 250 km 3h06; Hughenden to Julia Creek via Richmond 264 km 3h07; Julia Creek to Cloncurry 138 km 1h43; Cloncurry to Mount Isa 121 km 1h34. Also Townsville to Hughenden 384 km 4h44; Townsville to Cloncurry 785 km 9h33. (A waypoint in Richmond makes OSRM detour: do not quote Richmond legs from OSRM.)
- Tourism NT (northernterritory.com, fetched) itinerary: Townsville to Charters Towers 138 km; Charters Towers to Hughenden 252; Hughenden to Richmond 115; Richmond to Julia Creek 149; Julia Creek to Mount Isa 257.
- Wikipedia highway table (km from Townsville): Charters Towers 122 (prose says 133), Torrens Creek 284, Hughenden 371, Julia Creek 637, Cloncurry 759. Pentland about 240 km in prose (not in table). Tables inconsistent with OSRM by 10 to 15 km: do not mix; use OSRM for legs and say so.

## 2. Camps (council and parks)
- Julia Creek free RV site (McKinlay Shire Council Visitor Information page, fetched): "designated free camping area" on the water's edge, 1.3 km on the eastern side of town; for self-contained RVs, "Fully self contained (portable showers are not acceptable)", black water tank or toilet cassette; permits free, valid 96 hours; from the onsite camp hosts during peak season (April to September), from the Julia Creek Visitor Information Centre October to March; picnic tables, bush cook camps, bins; no open fires, no dumping of black or grey water, pets restrained. Outback Queensland listing agrees (Punchbowl Road, "Does not cater for people with access needs").
- Hughenden council RV park (Visit Hughenden, fetched): "designed for fully self-contained travellers with a stay of up to 7 nights"; register and display a permit, self-registration onsite; page states no charge (neither confirms nor denies). Allen Terry Caravan Park, Resolution Street: amenities, laundry, fire pit, a dump point, vehicle wash-down bay. Torrens Creek: Exchange Hotel powered and unpowered sites with hot water and bathrooms (Visit Hughenden); Outback Queensland listing for the Oasis Exchange Hotel lists a petrol station (listing undated, over 2 years old: confirm).
- Cloncurry, Mary Kathleen Memorial Park, Chinaman Creek Dam: no council page on camping, fees or dump point found: NOT CONFIRMED. RACQ: "Self-contained caravanners can free camp" at Mary Kathleen; "a 4WD is required to reach the mine site" (RACQ, not an authority).
- Data campgrounds (corridor.py 15 km): Alligator Creek camping area, Mount Elliot AU11350 (9.2 km off, 25 km along; toilets, flush toilets, drinking water, camper trailer sites); Alligator Falls AU11282; Cockatoo Creek AU11268; Mount Elliot remote bush camp AU11263. None near Charters Towers onward. No fees checked.
- Dump point (data): Cloncurry/Mary Kathleen Memorial Park AU16348, free, drinking water, toilets, accessible toilet, accessible parking, sharps disposal; no washout tap listed. Rest areas: none within 15 km.

## 3. Porcupine Gorge National Park
- Queensland Parks (search extract): "Camping is available at the Pyramid camping area. All bookings for camp sites must be made online prior to visiting the park, and well in advance during peak season (April to October). Camping permits are required and fees apply." "Access to Porcupine Gorge National Park (including the lookout and camping area) is fully sealed. From Hughenden, follow the Kennedy Developmental Road north for 60km to reach the turn-off to the Gorge lookout. The turn-off to the Pyramid camping area, day-use area and walking track is a further 11.7km north along the Kennedy Developmental Road." Fee amounts not found.
- Visit Hughenden: Pyramid suits tents, caravans, camper trailers, motorhomes; composting toilets, picnic tables; campfires only in steel fire rings, bring your own firewood, generators not permitted; "pets are not allowed as this campground is in the National Park."

## 4. Roads, floods, fuel
- QLDTraffic (fetched): run by Department of Transport and Main Roads; alert categories hazards, crashes, congestion, flooding, special events, roadworks; future roadworks. Drive North West QLD (regional tourism site, fetched): current conditions at the Queensland Government traffic site or by calling 131 940 (13 19 40); "Road conditions vary and it's important for you to stay informed"; summer travel (November to April) can be affected when "rain and storms can impede travel plans"; "Water crossings are not recommended and should not be attempted if you are uncertain of your vehicle's capabilities"; "Long distances and stretches of unchanging landscape can make a driver very tired", "Avoid driving at night as much as possible"; on major highways fuel stops are "rarely more than 200km apart", "no fuel" signs mean no fuel; road trains: when one approaches to overtake "move as far to the left as possible and stop if necessary", allow plenty of room, they "may move side to side".
- McKinlay Shire Council road report, Flinders Highway, current as at 4:30pm on 11 March 2026 (PDF, text extracted with pdftotext): Julia Creek to Richmond (14D) CLOSED due to flooding on Boundary Creek; Julia Creek to Cloncurry (14E) CLOSED due to flooding at Eastern Creek Bridge, 300 mm over the bridge. Search extract: by 14 March the Julia Creek to Cloncurry section OPEN with some surface water, Richmond section still closed at Boundary Creek.
- Queensland Government statement 15 February 2019 (statements.qld.gov.au/statements/86621, fetched): Flinders Highway closed between Richmond and Julia Creek; detour via the Landsborough Highway, Winton and Hughenden; "large sections of the Flinders Highway between Richmond to Julia Creek are still inundated and inaccessible."
- Fuel: no operator or Queensland Government list found. Road Trip Nomads blog claims no fuel Cloncurry to Julia Creek: not an authority, left out. Wikipedia table shows no town between Julia Creek (637) and Cloncurry (759) and between Charters Towers and Torrens Creek except Pentland.
- Mobile coverage: no authority source.
