## 1. Route options
1. **Newell Highway (A39) via Shepparton and Tocumwal, then Gore Highway (A39) to Toowoomba**
   - OSRM: 1,675 km, 21 h 49 min (Melbourne, Shepparton, Tocumwal, Narrandera, West Wyalong, Parkes, Dubbo, Coonabarabran, Narrabri, Moree, Goondiwindi, Millmerran, Toowoomba, Brisbane). This is the shortest of the three by OSRM.
   - Official-style figures (Wikipedia, secondary): Newell Highway is 1,058 km from Tocumwal (Murray River; becomes the Goulburn Valley Highway in Victoria) to Boggabilla and Goondiwindi, longest highway in NSW, route A39. Gore Highway is 197 km Goondiwindi to Charlton, A39, also the Melbourne to Brisbane National Highway link.
   - Road type (Wikipedia): apart from short four-lane urban sections, single carriageway, two-lane, with many overtaking lanes. Speed: most rural sections 110 km/h; Tooraweenah to Coonabarabran 100 km/h. Gore Highway: 100 km/h Athol to Millmerran, 110 km/h Millmerran to Goondiwindi. Toowoomba Bypass completed September 2019 (Gore Highway rerouted between Athol and Charlton).
   - OSRM legs: Melbourne-Shepparton 178 km 2h20; Shepparton-Tocumwal 82 km 1h15; Tocumwal-Narrandera 166 km 2h07; Narrandera-West Wyalong 135 km 1h35; West Wyalong-Parkes 140 km 1h50; Parkes-Dubbo 119 km 1h29; Dubbo-Coonabarabran 145 km 1h55; Coonabarabran-Narrabri 120 km 1h28; Narrabri-Moree 100 km 1h13; Moree-Goondiwindi 124 km 1h29; Goondiwindi-Millmerran 147 km 1h54; Millmerran-Toowoomba 94 km 1h23; Toowoomba-Brisbane 125 km 1h46.
2. **Hume Highway to Wagga Wagga, then Sturt Highway to Narrandera, then the Newell as above**
   - OSRM: 1,786 km, 23 h 01 min (Melbourne, Wangaratta, Wagga Wagga, Narrandera, then same as option 1). About 111 km and 1 h 12 min longer than option 1 by OSRM. Legs: Melbourne-Wangaratta 240 km 3h02; Wangaratta-Wagga 199 km 2h35; Wagga-Narrandera 97 km 1h17.
   - Real option if you want the Hume's dual carriageway and Albury-Wodonga or Wagga stops (see sydney-to-melbourne-drive). Sturt Highway road name for Wagga-Narrandera is not source-checked: write "via Narrandera".
3. **Hume Highway to Sydney, then Pacific Highway to Brisbane**
   - OSRM: 1,784 km, 21 h 32 min (Melbourne, Albury, Goulburn, Prestons, Hornsby, Grafton, Brisbane). Legs: Melbourne-Albury 316 km 3h51; Albury-Goulburn 357 km 4h07; Goulburn-Prestons 157 km 1h53; Prestons-Hornsby 58 km 0h49; Hornsby-Grafton 582 km 6h59; Grafton-Brisbane 315 km 3h50.
   - Pacific Highway completed as dual carriageway Hexham to the Queensland border 17 December 2020 (federal investment listing, search extract). Sydney tolls (see section 4) apply on that approach. About 109 km longer than option 1 by OSRM but with fuel, parks and towns every 100 km or less.
   - OSRM times here are shorter than the Newell for the same kms because more of the route is dual carriageway.

## 2. Legs and overnight stops (option 1, Newell)
Three-day split (OSRM): Day 1 Melbourne to Narrandera about 426 km (178+82+166); Day 2 Narrandera to Narrabri about 659 km, too long: split at Dubbo (Narrandera-Dubbo about 394 km: 135+140+119) and Dubbo-Narrabri 265 km (145+120); Day 3 Narrabri to Brisbane about 590 km (100+124+147+94+125). Four days is realistic: Melbourne-Narrandera 426 km; Narrandera-Dubbo 394 km; Dubbo-Moree 365 km (145+120+100); Moree-Brisbane 490 km (124+147+94+125). Tell readers these are driving distances without stops.
Commercial parks are not in the AussieCamps campground data here; names come from listings and a 2015 Newell Highway visitor guide (newellhighway.org.au, via search extract): "phone to confirm they still operate".
- **Narrandera (about 426 km)**
  - Data: Narrandera Visitors Centre rest area AU09338 (Toilets, Picnic tables, Barbecues, Heavy vehicle area, Accessible toilet, Playground). Sandy Beach campground, Cudgel AU00314 (7.6 km off line, Toilets, Boat ramp). Rest area is for a break, not a place to sleep.
- **Dubbo (about 820 km along the OSRM line)**
  - Dubbo Midstate Caravan Park, 21 Bourke Street (Newell Highway visitor guide, 2015, search extract; powered and unpowered sites and cabins). Data: Dubbo Tourist Information Centre rest area AU09413 (Toilets, Picnic tables, Barbecues, Heavy vehicle area, Accessible toilet, Playground).
  - Parkes: no powered caravan park confirmed in Parkes itself; River Meadows Caravan Park is in Forbes at the corner of the Newell Highway and River Road (listing).
- **Narrabri**: Narrabri Big Sky Caravan Park, 11-35 Tibbereena Street (visitor guide listing).
- **Moree**: no commercial park named in the search; Moree Showgrounds offer powered and unpowered camping with toilets, showers and a dump point, powered $30 a night per a roadtrip guide (undated: do not quote the price).
- **Goondiwindi**: BIG4 Goondiwindi Holiday Park, 36 Old Cunningham Highway (listing); showgrounds 3 km north of town, more than 40 sites (guide).
- **Corridor data** (all Murray River campgrounds near Tocumwal/Cobram, no facilities listed in most cases): Smithers Beach campground, Tocumwal AU00323 (3.4 km off line); Woperana campgrounds AU00391 (Phone reception); Quicks Beach campground, Cobram AU00298 (Toilets, Flush toilets).
- **Rest areas on the Newell in data** (all list Toilets, Picnic tables, Barbecues, Heavy vehicle area, Accessible toilet): McCann Park, Bland NSW AU10437 (Playground; the route's West Wyalong area); Marsden Rest Area Southbound, Bland NSW AU10028 (Shelter). Victoria: Grass Tree, Mangalore AU06306; Coach Road, Avenel AU06432; Calder Woodburn, Arcadia AU06594 (no Accessible toilet listed). Queensland: Millmerran rest area AU07022 (Toilets, Drinking water, Shelter, Picnic tables, Heavy vehicle area, Accessible toilet).
- **Spring Creek Rest Area** (north of Narrabri on the Newell): opened September 2025 as the region's largest heavy vehicle rest area; short and long-term heavy vehicle parking for up to 18 vehicles, separate parking for light vehicles and for light vehicles with caravans or trailers, two flushable toilets with disabled access, picnic shelters and bins (federal minister and Transport for NSW releases, search extract). Rest areas elsewhere on the Newell are not all signed for overnight stops; a government statement said outside Narrabri drivers faced "lengthy detours for rest areas with toilets" (before Spring Creek opened).
- Overnight rules: Queensland up to 20 hours in 24 unless signed; NSW advice conflicts; Victoria no statewide limit, councils can ban (see can-i article). Treat all rest areas as break stops.

## 4. Road conditions, closures, tolls
- Live sources: Live Traffic NSW (https://www.livetraffic.com, phone 132 701 in Transport for NSW closure reports), QLDTraffic (https://qldtraffic.qld.gov.au; alert types include flooding; shows truck restrictions and rest areas), VicTraffic and VicEmergency (URLs did not load: confirm before linking).
- Flood closures (news and Wikipedia, search extracts): Wikipedia says sections of the Newell are subject to periodic flooding from the rivers it crosses, "particularly north of Moree, at Dubbo, and south of Narrandera". Examples: a major flood warning for the Gwydir River near Moree (2025 event) with the Newell closed in both directions between Moree and the Queensland border; April 2024: Newell closed between Boggabilla and Moree, with a diversion via the Carnarvon Highway and the Barwon Highway to Goondiwindi (Carnarvon Highway also closed in the 2021 floods). No current closure found for 21 October 2026.
- Fire: one undated news result reports a bushfire closure between Narrabri and Coonabarabran with detours via Gunnedah. Historical only.
- Gore Highway flooding: no source found; do not say.
- Heavy vehicles (Wikipedia, secondary): heavy vehicles make up 26% to 52% of Newell traffic depending on location; near Grong Grong about 32% of roughly 1,900 daily vehicles; in the north a truck passes on average every 60 seconds around the clock. Link driving-with-road-trains-australia for passing etiquette (road trains are not confirmed on the Newell: do not say).
- Roadworks (search extract): the Narrabri to Moree heavy duty pavement upgrade ($261.17 million, more than 20 km new pavement, five new overtaking lanes) was reported complete in September 2025. Tycannah Rest Area upgrade ($12 million) reported complete (federal release title). No current roadworks list found.
- Tolls, Melbourne: Linkt Visitor's Pass (page text): "good to go for up to 30 days on any toll road in Melbourne"; "CityLink trips made up to 5 days ago, and EastLink trips made up to 3 days ago"; $3.50 online or $5.00 by phone plus a video matching fee per trip. Whether your exit from Melbourne uses a toll road depends on where you start: NOT CONFIRMED.
- Tolls, Brisbane: Linkt Road Pass "can cover trips made up to 5 days ago on all toll roads in Queensland"; Queensland Government guidance (search extract) says 3 days. Arriving from Toowoomba, which Brisbane toll roads you meet: NOT CONFIRMED.
- Tolls, Sydney (option 3 only): NSW Government and Service NSW: 72 hours to pay or create an account; eMU Casual Pass for up to 30 days. M5 and M7 are tolled and meet the Hume at Prestons (Wikipedia).

## 5. State border rules
- **Victoria and NSW (Murray River at Tocumwal or Albury):** Agriculture Victoria (search extract; page returned 403): Greater Sunraysia Pest Free Area regulation ended July 2024, so no restrictions on carrying Queensland fruit fly host produce into or out of Sunraysia; Mediterranean fruit fly restrictions apply statewide. Safe wording: "we found no fruit fly checkpoint on these crossings; check Agriculture Victoria (136 186) before carrying produce."
- **NSW zones on the Newell (Interstate Quarantine site, undated):** Rice Biosecurity Zone covers most of the Murrumbidgee and Murray River regions: "Do not bring rice plants or unprocessed grain such as paddy rice or brown rice into the zone." Phylloxera Infested Zone covers areas around Albury-Corowa: "Do not take soil, grapevine plants, mulch or garden organics out of these areas." Whole of NSW is a Potato Biosecurity Zone (not travellers' food).
- **NSW and Queensland at Goondiwindi/Boggabilla:**
  - Queensland: "The whole of the state is a biosecurity zone for bananas, grape plants, mangoes and sugarcane. Do not take any plants, soil or items that have been in contact with these plants into Queensland." (Interstate Quarantine site, undated.)
  - Fire ants: the NSW DPIRD extract describes the infested area as about 1,111,800 ha of south east Queensland and parts of northern NSW, with roadside checks (Operation VICTA) in northern NSW. Whether Goondiwindi or Toowoomba sit inside the zone, and how the Brisbane end is treated: NOT CONFIRMED. The Brisbane approach from Toowoomba may enter the fire ant biosecurity zone; direct the reader to the Biosecurity Queensland map and 13 25 23, and to the NSW DPIRD page. Carriers named by DPIRD include soil, mulch, hay, pallets, packaging, green waste, compost and manure, heavy vehicles, equipment. Report suspect ants: 13 22 68.
  - There is no confirmed fixed quarantine station at Goondiwindi.
- Time zone: NSW and Victoria on daylight saving at the travel dates; Queensland is not; the clock changes 1 hour at the Queensland border (NOT CONFIRMED by a fetched source).
