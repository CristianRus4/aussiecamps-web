## 1. Route options
- A. Calder Freeway/Calder Highway (A79) via Bendigo, Charlton, Wycheproof, Sea Lake and Ouyen. OSRM direct 542 km, 6h46 (waypoints Melbourne, Charlton, Mildura). Per leg: Melbourne to Bendigo 151 km 1h57; Bendigo to Charlton 106 km 1h30; Charlton to Wycheproof 30 km 0h25; Wycheproof to Sea Lake 77 km 0h57; Sea Lake to Ouyen 89 km 1h03; Ouyen to Mildura 101 km 1h16 (total 555 km 7h11 with the Bendigo waypoint). Wikipedia (Calder Highway): total 560.9 km from Curlwaa NSW to Airport West; A79 NSW/Victoria border to Ravenswood, M79 (Calder Freeway) Ravenswood to Niddrie; "Between Red Cliffs and Wycheproof, the limit is 110 km/h"; Melbourne to Bendigo Calder Freeway four-lane dual carriageway (Keilor to Kyneton completed 2003, Harcourt North to Elphinstone 2009); Mallee: "Elsewhere in the Mallee, the highway is a two-lane single carriageway", including the stretch between Mildura and Ouyen; Mildura to Irymple four-lane dual. Distances from Mildura end (Wikipedia table): Ouyen 123.9 km, Sea Lake 209.1, Wycheproof 288.0, Charlton 318.3, Bendigo 423.6. Calder Alternative Highway (A790) Marong to Ravenswood is a bypass west of Bendigo.
- B. Western Freeway to Ballarat, then Sunraysia Highway (B220) via St Arnaud and Donald to Ouyen, then Calder Highway to Mildura. OSRM waypoints Melbourne, Ballarat, St Arnaud, Donald, Ouyen, Mildura: 557 km, 7h24 (Ballarat 114 km; to St Arnaud 133; to Donald 38; to Ouyen 171; to Mildura 101). About 15 km and 38 min longer than A. Wikipedia (Sunraysia Highway): 330.2 km from Ouyen (Calder Highway) to Mitchell Park on Ballarat's edge; B220; towns Speed, Lascelles, Woomelang, Birchip, Donald, St Arnaud, Avoca, Lexton; it "does not" reach Sea Lake or Mildura (Sea Lake via Birchip-Sea Lake Road). Real alternative, not a shortcut.
- C. Horsham (Western Highway, Henty Highway): OSRM via Ballarat, Horsham, Mildura 604 km, 7h52. Left out (longer, no source describing it as a Mildura route).
- D. Via Swan Hill: OSRM via Bendigo, Swan Hill, Mildura 558 km, 7h17 (Bendigo to Swan Hill 187 km; Swan Hill to Mildura 220). Left out (no source; the Murray Valley Highway not researched).
- Mildura (Wikipedia): 35,652 people at the 2021 census; largest settlement in Sunraysia; on the Murray River; January mean daily maximum 33.3 C, record high 48.6 C; busiest regional airport in Victoria; 545 km straight-line from Melbourne.

## 2. Daily legs
- Day 1 Melbourne to Bendigo 151 km 1h57 (or on to Charlton 257 km 3h27, OSRM). Day 2 Bendigo to Mildura 404 km, 5h14 (OSRM total 555 km 7h11 minus the first leg). The direct 542 km route skips the Bendigo centre: going via Bendigo adds about 13 km. A single day is 6h46 of driving.
- Camping data (AussieCamps, corridor.py 15 km): only Hattah-Kulkyne National Park: Lake Hattah Camping Area AU03888 (+5.9 km off route, 488 km along), Lake Mournpall Camping Area AU03889 (+6.5 km, 495 km along), "Camping in Hattah-Kulkyne National Park" AU03887. Data lists no campground in Bendigo, Charlton, Wycheproof, Sea Lake, Ouyen or Mildura within 15 km: coverage gap, not proof. Name no caravan park.
- Parks Victoria (www.parks.vic.gov.au Hattah-Kulkyne page, fetched): campgrounds Firemans Bend, Jinkers Bend, Ki Bend, Lake Hattah, Lake Mournpall. Lake Hattah and Lake Mournpall pages: "Fees apply" (rate not on the page), "Dogs are not allowed", also no horses, no generators, no firearms (Lake Hattah page); booking through the Parks Victoria booking site; call 13 1963. Closure notice on the page: Lake Hattah and Lake Mournpall campgrounds closed 26 and 27 October 2026 for a goat conservation program (date passes before publish: do not use). Victorian Murray Floodplain Restoration Project works to December 2026 with "increased heavy vehicle movement and temporary track closures" (northern section). Raak Track and Goosefoot Track closed due to fire impacts. Check the page before going.
- Wikipedia (Hattah-Kulkyne): about 48,000 ha, Hattah Lakes Ramsar listed 15 December 1982, largest lake Lake Hattah, most lakes and streams dry out in the dry season; more than 200 bird species.
- Lake Tyrrell (Wikipedia): 6 km north of Sea Lake, lookout 7 to 8 km from the township, Victoria's largest salt lake, about 20,860 ha, shallow salt-crusted depression; no camping information on the page. A 2021 Premier release: new viewing platform and car park with toilets at the southern end; Sea Lake Visitor Information Centre. Third-party travel pages say camping at the lake is not permitted: NOT CONFIRMED officially, left out.

## 5. Fruit fly (changed in July 2024: the rule most pages still get wrong)
- Agriculture Victoria, "Regulatory changes in the Greater Sunraysia region" (403 to scripts; search extract, quoted twice): "A Greater Sunraysia Pest Free Area (GSPFA) was in place from 2006 through to 2024"; "regulation of the GSPFA lapsed and ceased to exist in July 2024"; "Signs and quarantine bins are being removed during 2025"; "There are no longer restrictions around bringing QFF host produce into or out of the Sunraysia area. However, the requirements for the movement of fruit to interstate QFF-sensitive markets have not changed and consignments will need to meet each state's importation requirements. Restrictions in movement of Mediterranean fruit fly host produce, which apply statewide, do not change."
- North West Farmer, 12 July 2024 (fetched): Agriculture Victoria statement; QFF "established throughout the Victorian and New South Wales portions of Greater Sunraysia"; "Signs and quarantine bins will be removed in the coming months"; the Lindsay Point Order (restricted area in north-west Victoria for QFF and Medfly) "remains in place to assist South Australia in retaining pest free area status in the Riverland."
- Agriculture Victoria Plant Quarantine Manual Condition 53, Queensland fruit fly Lindsay Point Control Area (403; search extract): affected plants and plant products cannot leave the control area without a permit from an inspector. Location of the control area not confirmed here: Lindsay Point is in the far north-west, the page does not say where the highway meets it.
- Visit Mildura fruit fly page returns 404 now. Older pages describing roadside bins are out of date.
- Interstate Quarantine (fetched): no Sunraysia or Victorian fruit fly zone listed; South Australia section: "zero tolerance approach", Riverland needs a plant health certificate for fresh, unprocessed fruit and fruiting vegetables, "Quarantine stations and fruit disposal bins are strategically placed throughout South Australia." A South Australia entry from Mildura (Renmark, Riverland) is the border that still matters: link interstate-biosecurity-road-trip and melbourne-to-adelaide-coastal-route. PIRSA pages not opened: no SA rule quoted.
