## 1. Route options
- A. Inland: Adelaide, South Eastern Freeway (M1) to Murray Bridge area, Princes Highway to Tailem Bend, Dukes Highway (A8) via Coonalpyn, Tintinara, Keith, Bordertown to the border, Western Highway (A8, M8 at Buangor) via Kaniva, Nhill, Dimboola/Horsham, Stawell, Ararat, Beaufort, Ballarat, Western Freeway to Melbourne.
  - OSRM direct: 726 km, 9h09. OSRM with waypoints in each town (Murray Bridge, Tailem Bend, Keith, Bordertown, Kaniva, Nhill, Horsham, Stawell, Ararat, Ballarat): 739 km, 9h43. Use "about 730 km and 9 h 10 min to 9 h 45 min" or quote both.
  - Legs (OSRM, waypoint route): Adelaide to Murray Bridge 76 km 1h02; to Tailem Bend 24 km 0h22; to Keith 130 km 1h32; to Bordertown 46 km 0h35; to Kaniva 44 km 0h35; to Nhill 39 km 0h31; to Horsham 75 km 0h59; to Stawell 67 km 0h53; to Ararat 31 km 0h28; to Ballarat 94 km 1h13; to Melbourne 114 km 1h27.
  - Adelaide to Horsham (sum) 434 km, 5h36. Adelaide to Nhill 359 km. Horsham to Melbourne about 306 km.
  - Dukes Highway (Wikipedia): 190 km, Tailem Bend to the border, route A8, "mostly a single carriageway of one lane each way", 36 overtaking lanes. Towns: Tailem Bend, Coomandook, Coonalpyn, Tintinara, Keith, Cannawigara, Bordertown, Wolseley.
  - Western Highway Victoria (Wikipedia): 419 km, Derrimut to the SA border at Serviceton. About 258 km single carriageway and 161 km dual (Western Freeway). Single carriageway with overtaking lanes from the border to just west of Buangor, through Nhill, Horsham, Stawell, Ararat. Dual from Buangor to Derrimut. Ballarat to Buangor duplicated in stages 2013, 2015, 2016. Distances from Melbourne (Wikipedia exit table): Ballarat about 114, Beaufort 143, Buangor 161, Ararat 185, Stawell 215, Horsham 278, Dimboola 318, Nhill 356, Kaniva 394, border 419. Wikipedia: second-busiest national highway for freight; at Anthony's Cutting more than 4,000 freight vehicles daily out of more than 29,000.
  - South Eastern Freeway (Wikipedia): 73.1 km, Adelaide to Long Flat near Murray Bridge, Heysen Tunnels, vehicles with five or more axles must keep left and 60 km/h between Crafers Interchange and the old tollhouse. Wikipedia does not say whether tolled; not described as tolled.
- B. Coastal: Princes Highway via Tailem Bend, Mount Gambier, Warrnambool, Colac, Geelong. OSRM 874 km, 11h20 (waypoints Tailem Bend, Mount Gambier, Warrnambool, Colac, Geelong). Via the Great Ocean Road (Warrnambool, Port Campbell, Apollo Bay, Torquay): OSRM 971 km, 13h10. Existing guide melbourne-to-adelaide-coastal-route covers the coast; link it. Legs OSRM: Adelaide to Tailem Bend 96 km, to Mount Gambier 339 km (4h03), to Warrnambool 183 km (2h26).
- Time zone: australia.gov.au (search extract): South Australia ACST UTC+9:30, ACDT UTC+10:30 with DST from first Sunday October to first Sunday April; Victoria AEST/AEDT UTC+10/+11. Both change on the same Sundays (4 Oct 2026, 4 Apr 2027), so Victoria is 30 minutes ahead of South Australia all year (derived).

## 5. Border rules
- Direction Adelaide to Melbourne: Agriculture Victoria (Mediterranean fruit fly page, search extract): people travelling to Victoria from South Australia and Western Australia are asked not to bring fruit or fruiting vegetables, to keep medfly out; Victoria is free from medfly. A request, not a stated ban. Plant Quarantine Manual covers commercial entry only.
- Interstate Quarantine (page opened, 200): Victoria: no fruit fly zones listed; phylloxera control areas, potato Plant Protection Districts, Toolangi; "Signs on major roads indicate when you are entering a Phylloxera Exclusion Zone or leaving a Phylloxera Infested Zone". South Australia: fruit zone only the Riverland (not on this route) and Kangaroo Island rules; "Quarantine stations and fruit disposal bins are strategically placed throughout South Australia. Mobile quarantine stations can operate anywhere and at anytime within South Australia."
- Greater Sunraysia regulation lapsed July 2024 (Agriculture Victoria, used on the finished Melbourne to Canberra page).
- Travelling the other way (Melbourne to Adelaide): PIRSA fruitfly.sa.gov.au (search extract): for travellers from Victoria fresh fruit is not permitted; processed, dried, preserved, cooked, frozen or canned fruit and vegetables are allowed; fresh produce at risk from fruit fly has to be eaten or thrown away before reaching SA or the Riverland; quarantine bins at road entry points; fines apply (PIRSA releases quote $417 and $420; amount NOT CONFIRMED, leave out). A receipt does not allow produce at risk from fruit fly.
- Red imported fire ants: Queensland, not this route.
