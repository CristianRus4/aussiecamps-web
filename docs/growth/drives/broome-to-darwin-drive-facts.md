# Broome to Darwin drive: facts (slug broome-to-darwin-drive)

Researched 2026-10-21. OSRM = "OSRM routing on OpenStreetMap data, computed 21 October 2026" (car, no stops, no caravan, centre to centre). Say "about" on every distance. IMPORTANT correction to the brief: the WA quarantine checkpoint is NOT at Kununurra town on the Great Northern Highway. It is on the Victoria Highway at the WA/NT border and it checks traffic ENTERING WA. A driver going Broome to Darwin crosses it going the other way and is not stopped (section 5).

## 1. Route options

1. **Great Northern Highway to Kununurra, Victoria Highway to Katherine, Stuart Highway to Darwin (main route, the only sealed through route)**
   - OSRM: 1,874 km, 20 h 54 min (Broome, Fitzroy Crossing, Halls Creek, Kununurra, Timber Creek, Katherine, Darwin). Brief figure "about 1,900 km": OSRM is about 1,870 to 1,880 km, so say "about 1,880 km". No road authority total was found.
   - OSRM legs: Broome-Fitzroy Crossing 398 km 4h38; Fitzroy-Halls Creek 290 km 3h25; Halls Creek-Kununurra 358 km 4h12; Kununurra-Timber Creek 228 km 2h22; Timber Creek-Katherine 285 km 2h50; Katherine-Darwin 314 km 3h25.
   - Main Roads WA 2015 rest area guide (Port Hedland to Kununurra map) gives: Fitzroy Crossing 939 km and Halls Creek 1,228 km and Kununurra 1,587 km from Port Hedland, so Fitzroy to Halls Creek is 289 km and Halls Creek to Kununurra is 359 km (matches OSRM within 1 km). Guide dated March 2015.
   - Visit Kununurra: from Broome the Great Northern Highway is followed east, 22 km past Willare Roadhouse then 816 km to the intersection with the Victoria Highway; Kununurra is 46 km along the Victoria Highway turning right, or turn left for Wyndham (55 km). So Kununurra is a 46 km detour off the Great Northern Highway line, and the Victoria Highway to Katherine starts at that junction (tourism body page, text as read).
   - Wikipedia (secondary): Victoria Highway 555 to 557 km long, about 470 km of it in the NT; western junction with the Great Northern Highway at km 0, WA/NT border at 87.6 km, Timber Creek about km 300, Katherine at the eastern end (km 557), "fully reconstructed and sealed to a good standard by the early 1990s".
   - Wikipedia: Victoria River crossing near Timber Creek is flood-prone in the wet season (November to April), floodwater "can cut the link for days or weeks"; a replacement bridge 7 m higher was completed in May 2008 and "floods of roughly one-in-twenty-year size will still overtop it".
2. **Gibb River Road (Derby to Kununurra), unsealed, 4WD** - Main Roads WA's guide has a Derby to Kununurra (via Gibb River Road) map (map 07). No distance or time was taken. Link: gibb-river-road-camping. Not a Broome to Darwin shortcut for caravans.
3. **Stuart Highway south route via Alice Springs** is not a real option for this trip. Link the sibling guide darwin-to-alice-springs-drive for Darwin onwards.

Note on Sandfire: Sandfire Roadhouse is on the Perth to Broome side (south of Broome, OSRM Broome-Sandfire 322 km). It is NOT on Broome to Darwin. Do not use it as a fuel stop here. Link perth-to-broome-road-trip for that coast.

## 2. Legs and overnight stops
Suggested split (OSRM times, no stops): Day 1 Broome to Fitzroy Crossing (about 398 km, 4h38), Day 2 Fitzroy Crossing to Halls Creek (290 km, 3h25), Day 3 Halls Creek to Kununurra (358 km, 4h12), Day 4 Kununurra to Katherine via Timber Creek (about 513 km, 5h12), Day 5 Katherine to Darwin (314 km, 3h25). Fitzroy to Halls Creek and Halls Creek to Kununurra can also be combined as a long single day (648 km, about 7h37).
- **Fitzroy Crossing, Halls Creek**: roadhouse and town services; no park confirmed on an operator page: name none. Main Roads WA marks rest areas at Ngumban Cliff (1,036 km from Port Hedland), Mary Pool (1,119), Little Panton (1,274), Leycesters, Spring Creek, Dunham, Cockburn (1,541) on its 2015 map.
- **Doon Doon and Warmun (Turkey Creek)**: roadhouses with camping between Halls Creek and Kununurra (search extracts: Doon Doon Roadhouse beside a campground; Warmun about 160 km north of Halls Creek). Halls Creek-Warmun distance: about 160 km (search extract, two sources agree 160 to 162 km). Fuel availability: see section 3.
- **Kununurra**: Hidden Valley Kununurra Caravan Park, next to Mirima National Park (Summerstar Tourist Parks operator page, as search extract; Summerstar bought and renamed it in mid-2025). Other parks (Kununurra Lakeside Tourist Resort, Town Caravan Park) appear on anycamp listings only: do not name without checking. Kununurra to Lake Argyle: about 70 to 78 km (sources differ: 69 km, 75 km, 78 km on the visitor centre map); the turn-off is about 35 km east of Kununurra and 7 km before the border checkpoint; Lake Argyle Resort and Caravan Park is at the end of a sealed road (35 km off the highway). All tourism sources, not official.
- **Timber Creek (NT)**: Timber Creek Hotel and Caravan Park and WIRIB Tourism Park (both listed with powered sites, cabins: tourism listing, search extract). Under 300 people. Timber Creek is about 228 km from Kununurra by OSRM.
- **Victoria River Roadhouse (NT)**: the only real stop between Timber Creek and Katherine (tourism listing); about 194 km west of Katherine (tourism listing; OSRM Victoria River Roadhouse to Katherine 195 km). Dump point in data: Victoria River Roadhouse, Gregory NT, AU15427 (Free; toilets, showers).
- **Katherine**: dump points in data below. Nitmiluk (Katherine Gorge) entrance is about 30 km from Katherine (tourism sources agree). Entry needs an NT Parks Pass (NT residents exempt) and camping is booked and paid in advance; prices in listings are dated: do not quote.
- **Darwin**: Katherine to Darwin 314 km. Link darwin-kakadu-litchfield-loop and darwin-to-alice-springs-drive.
- **Campgrounds in AussieCamps data (Gregory National Park area, NT)**: Big Horse Creek Campground AU20805 (Camping fee, 0.3 km off route, about 1,265 km along); Sullivan Creek Campground AU20819 (Camping fee, 0.1 km off, about 1,384 km); Gumil Campground AU20814 (Camping fee, 5 km off, about 1,394 km). No facilities listed in the data. These are national park campgrounds, not caravan parks.
- **Rest areas in AussieCamps data (breaks, not beds)**: Cockburn, Wyndham-East Kimberley WA AU08337 (Toilets, Shelter, Accessible toilet, Dump point); Saddle Creek NT AU06775, Mathison Creek NT AU06773, Bridge Creek NT AU06759 (all Free; Toilets, Drinking water, Shelter, Picnic tables, Barbecues).
- Rules: WA, up to 24 consecutive hours only where signed; Main Roads WA says its 24-hour rest areas "are not intended for camping". NT policy: no more than 24 hours at any one rest area, and the NT lists which highway stops allow 24-hour overnight camping (AussieCamps can-i guide, sourced to NT Government). Link: can-i-stay-overnight-at-a-rest-area-in-australia, camping-rules-western-australia, camping-rules-northern-territory.

## 3. Fuel and services
- Search extract, Australia's North West fuel guide (tourism body, may be out of date): fuel outlets at Broome, Roebuck Roadhouse, Willare Bridge, Fitzroy Crossing, Halls Creek, Warmun, Wyndham and Kununurra. "The greatest distance between fuel outlets on the Great Northern Highway is 295 kilometres between Fitzroy Crossing and Halls Creek." Main Roads WA 2015 map gives Fitzroy to Halls Creek as 289 km, OSRM 290 km. Use: "about 290 km, the longest gap on the Broome to Kununurra stretch (Australia's North West)".
- Fitzroy Crossing to Halls Creek: 289 km (Main Roads WA 2015 guide). Halls Creek to Kununurra: 359 km (same guide) with Warmun (about 160 km from Halls Creek) and Doon Doon (about 106 to 110 km from Kununurra by tourism sources, which disagree) listed as roadhouses; so the Halls Creek to Warmun stage is about 160 km. Whether Doon Doon sells fuel today is NOT CONFIRMED (a fuel guide lists it as a key stop, no operator page opened).
- Broome to Willare Bridge Roadhouse and Roebuck: the Willare Roadhouse is about 22 km before the junction used in the Visit Kununurra directions; the Broome to Willare distance is NOT CONFIRMED.
- Kununurra to Timber Creek: 228 km (OSRM). Timber Creek to Victoria River Roadhouse: 91 km (OSRM). Kununurra to Victoria River Roadhouse is about 319 km by OSRM. Fuel at Timber Creek and Victoria River Roadhouse is NOT CONFIRMED on an operator page (tourism listing calls Victoria River Roadhouse "the main fuel, food and drink stop"). Say "plan fuel at Kununurra and again at Timber Creek or Victoria River Roadhouse".
- Main Roads WA: "Extra fuel if travelling long distances"; "Enough food and water to last for 2 to 3 days past your intended travel time". Link road-trip-fuel-planning-australia.

## 4. Road conditions and seasonal closures
- WA live source: Main Roads WA Travel Map (https://travelmap.mainroads.wa.gov.au/, returned 200), phone 138 138. Main Roads: "Use the Travel Map to plan your journey and to check for any road closures which may affect your travels."
- NT live source: Road Report NT (https://roadreport.nt.gov.au/, returned 200; the page is a script app and gave no text). Visit Kununurra recommends checking the WA Travel Map and the NT Road Report for current road conditions.
- Wet season: Main Roads WA says "The wet season in the north of WA generally runs from November to April". Cyclone season 1 November to 30 April (search extract of a 2018 BoM outlook).
- Fitzroy River: the original bridge was badly damaged in early 2023 and demolished; the new Fitzroy River Bridge opened to traffic in late 2023 and "road users can no longer cross the river using the low-level crossing" (search extracts of Main Roads WA flood updates). Say that the bridge reopened the crossing; do not say it can never flood.
- Victoria River (NT) crossing: Wikipedia says the crossing can cut the link in the wet season. Search extracts of news reports from earlier wet seasons record the Victoria Highway closed by flooding and a load limit after reopening (not official, historical). Current closures: NOT CONFIRMED; point to Road Report NT.
- Purnululu (Bungle Bungles): the Spring Creek Track is 53 km off the Great Northern Highway, 4WD high clearance only, dry season only (April 1 to December 31 per a search extract; another guide says wet closure December to March; DBCA's old campground brochure says campgrounds open April to December). Current dates: NOT CONFIRMED. Link gibb-river-road-camping for the Kimberley 4WD.
- Road trains, wildlife: Main Roads WA says "Only overtake when you have a long straight stretch of road ahead which is completely clear" and "Do not cut in front of them"; "Keep an eye out for animal warning signs". Main Roads animal alert (2015 guide): many major rural highways are unfenced; be alert "particularly dusk and dawn". Link driving-with-road-trains-australia.
- NT speed limits (search extract of the NT Government speed limits page; the page returned 403 to script): "the default speed limit is 110km/h unless a speed limit sign states otherwise." The extract lists the Barkly, Stuart, Victoria and Arnhem Highways as having "some sections signposted at 130 km/h". NT Traffic Regulations 1999, reg 77A (AustLII extract): 130 km/h applies only where signs say so. The NT open-speed (no limit) trial ended and was replaced by 130 km/h signs on the Stuart Highway (older news, not the Victoria Highway). Do NOT say the Victoria Highway has an open speed limit; say "some signposted sections allow 130 km/h; obey the signs". Exact Victoria Highway 130 km/h sections: NOT CONFIRMED.
- Wikipedia (secondary): WA/NT border at about km 88 of the Victoria Highway; Ord River Dam access road branches off just west of the border.

## 5. State border rules (WA to NT)
- Checkpoint: DPIRD border checkpoints page (https://dpird.wa.gov.au/about-us/contact-us/wa-border-checkpoints): "WA/NT Road Checkpoint", location "WA/NT Border, Victoria Hwy, Lake Argyle WA", "Operates: 24/7 year-round", phone 08 9168 7354 (page shows only the 2026 copyright line; the page has no entry named Kununurra). The separate "Kununurra Quarantine Stockyards" at Drovers Road is for livestock inspections (Mon to Fri 7 am to 3 pm), not for travellers.
- Direction: Visit Kununurra biosecurity page and the Visit Kununurra getting here page both say "There is NO BORDER CHECKPOINT when leaving Western Australia to enter the Northern Territory." So Broome to Darwin is not stopped. For the reverse direction (Darwin to Broome) the checkpoint applies: "As you enter Western Australia from the Northern Territory, you'll pass through a 24-hour Road Border Checkpoint." Restricted risk items "may need to be surrendered in quarantine bins or handed directly to an inspector"; "significant penalties apply for failing to declare" (Visit Kununurra, tourism body).
- What is banned entering WA (DPIRD "What can I bring into WA", page undated, 2026 copyright): "Generally, no fresh fruit and vegetables are allowed, except for the items listed below", provided they are "free of soil, pests, disease and do not have green growth or sprouting". Honey is prohibited unless certified (DPIRD search extract). Bees, hive products and used equipment prohibited except by permit. Plants and bulbs need prior approval. Quarantine WA phone (08) 9334 1800. Full wording of the permitted list was not opened: the writer should link the DPIRD page, not copy a list. Amnesty bin wording at the checkpoint: NOT CONFIRMED (Visit Kununurra says "quarantine bin", DPIRD page does not use the word amnesty).
- Ord River Irrigation Area (Kununurra): a 2013 DPIRD-linked news extract says fruit and some vegetables are barred from the Ord from 1 April to 30 November each year; the Shire of Carnarvon page says Medfly is present everywhere in WA except the Ord River Irrigation Area. Current rule and dates: NOT CONFIRMED. Tell travellers not to carry produce into Kununurra and check DPIRD.
- Entering the NT from WA: no NT checkpoint or NT rule was researched: do not state one. Link interstate-biosecurity-road-trip.

## 7. Time change, parks and other facts
- Time zone: WA is on AWST, NT is on ACST. Secondary sources (tz database mailing list, timeanddate-style references in search results) give ACST as UTC+9:30 and AWST as UTC+8, a 1.5 hour difference, and place the WA/NT border on 129 degrees E. No official government page was reachable (australia.gov.au redirected). Say "the Northern Territory is 1.5 hours ahead of WA (AWST, ACST)" but mark it as general knowledge in the page. Neither WA nor the NT uses daylight saving: NOT CONFIRMED by a fetched source. A 1996 mailing list post said Kununurra had informally switched to NT time; unverified, leave out. Practical wording: "put your clock forward 1.5 hours when you cross the border" is NOT CONFIRMED for a specific spot; say "at the WA/NT border".
- Boab Prison Tree: Derby, about 7 km from Derby (Tourism WA; Visit Broome says 6 to 7 km south of Derby). Several sources say the "prison" story is doubtful (Atlas Obscura, Wikivoyage). It is NOT on the main route: Derby is a detour off the Great Northern Highway (distance from the highway NOT CONFIRMED). A different boab, Boab Tree rest area (Main Roads WA, 783 km from Port Hedland, between Broome and Fitzroy Crossing), is on the highway. Photo of a giant boab at a rest area between Derby and Fitzroy Crossing exists on Commons (section 8).
- Purnululu, Gibb River Road: see section 4. Katherine Gorge (Nitmiluk): about 30 km from Katherine (section 2).
- National park fees: NT Parks Pass for Nitmiluk (see section 2); camping fees for the Gregory National Park campgrounds in the data ("Camping fee"). Amounts: NOT CONFIRMED.
- Lake Argyle: see section 2 (distances from tourism sources).
- Related guides: perth-to-broome-road-trip, gibb-river-road-camping, wet-season-camping-top-end-northern-wa, driving-with-road-trains-australia, darwin-to-alice-springs-drive (sibling), darwin-kakadu-litchfield-loop, interstate-biosecurity-road-trip, road-trip-fuel-planning-australia.
