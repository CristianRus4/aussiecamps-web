# Adelaide to Port Lincoln drive: facts (slug adelaide-to-port-lincoln-drive)

Researched 2026-10-21. OSRM = "OSRM routing on OpenStreetMap data, computed 21 October 2026" (car, no stops, centre to centre). Whole route is in South Australia: no state border. Note: the road out of Adelaide is Port Wakefield Road/Highway then the Augusta Highway (A1), not the Princes Highway (Wikipedia: Augusta Highway "was at one time referred to as the Princes Highway, although it is not continuous with the Princes Highway in the state's southeast").

## 1. Route
- OSRM Adelaide to Port Lincoln direct: 650 km, 7 h 44 min (Port Wakefield Highway, Augusta Highway, Eyre Highway about 25 km, Lincoln Highway). With waypoints in the town centres of Port Wakefield, Port Augusta, Whyalla, Cowell, Arno Bay and Tumby Bay: 673 km, 8 h 16 min. Legs: Adelaide-Port Wakefield 100 km 1 h 15; Port Wakefield-Port Augusta 209 km 2 h 28; Port Augusta-Whyalla 77 km 56 min; Whyalla-Cowell 107 km 1 h 17; Cowell-Arno Bay 46 km 35 min; Arno Bay-Tumby Bay 65 km 48 min; Tumby Bay-Port Lincoln 70 km 53 min. Adelaide-Port Augusta 309 km 3 h 43.
- Wikipedia Augusta Highway: 208.1 km, A1, Port Wakefield to Port Augusta West (Eyre and Stuart Highway junction); mainly single carriageway with occasional overtaking lanes; Port Wakefield to Lochiel section duplicated, about 29 km dual carriageway largely completed by March 2025; Augusta Highway Freight Highway Upgrade Program, a 10-year program from 2023, includes overtaking lanes. Towns: Port Germein, Port Pirie, Crystal Brook, Snowtown, Port Wakefield.
- Wikipedia Lincoln Highway: 315 km, B100, from Eyre Highway junction at Lincoln Gap to Flinders Highway at Port Lincoln; sealed, one lane each way, few overtaking lanes, many straight stretches, mostly 110 km/h; table km from Lincoln Gap: Whyalla 47, Middleback Range 72, Cowell 161, Arno Bay 198 (Birdseye Highway B91 to Cleve and Lock), Tumby Bay 269, Port Lincoln 315. Other settlements: Port Neill, Louth Bay, North Shields. Gulf visible only in glimpses.
- Wikipedia: Tumby Bay "269 km" is the Bratten Way junction toward Cummins (Tumby Bay town is off the highway: OSRM Arno Bay-Tumby Bay-Port Lincoln is 135 km versus highway 117 km).

## 5. National parks (parks.sa.gov.au, fetched 2026-10-21)
- Lincoln National Park: "$14.40 per vehicle", pay before entering; camping and booking online or in person via booking agent; accommodation from $23.10 a night; "Dogs are not permitted in this park"; entrance 13 km south-west of Port Lincoln via Proper Bay Road; "There is no water available in the park and you must bring your own"; Memory Cove needs a booking and key, fires and generators not permitted there; caravans/camper vans suitable at Surfleet, Taylors Landing, September Beach; 4WD-only sites; closes on Catastrophic Fire Danger days and may close on Extreme days (CFS 1800 362 361); alert: western (Sleaford) end of Sleaford Wanna 4WD track blocked by sand dune.
- Coffin Bay National Park: "Vehicle entry: $14.40 per vehicle"; accommodation from $23.10 per night; camping fees paid before arrival; "Dogs are not permitted in this park"; "located 50km west of Port Lincoln"; Yangie Bay campground (2WD) suits tents, camper trailers, camper vans and caravans; other named campgrounds 4WD only; "Do not tow vans along the Gunyah Beach track or beyond Yangie because of the difficult terrain"; check tide times, avoid beaches at high tide; whale carcass alert on Almonta Beach.

## 6. Fruit fly (PIRSA; pages 403 to script; search extracts only)
- PIRSA outbreak page updated 12 Oct 2026 (search extract): Elizabeth East Queensland fruit fly outbreak end date 12 December 2026 (northern Adelaide); Riverland outbreaks end date 3 January 2027; 1.5 km outbreak area and 15 km suspension area around detections. Restrictions on moving fruit and vegetables grown within the red outbreak area (search). 24-Hour Fruit Fly Hotline 1300 666 010 (search extract). Fines up to $20,000 for a truck driver and up to $100,000 for a company, per a PIRSA brochure (search extract; not used).
- Riverland is not on this route. Whether the Elizabeth East zones touch the route out of Adelaide: NOT CONFIRMED (check PIRSA map). No Eyre Peninsula zone found.
