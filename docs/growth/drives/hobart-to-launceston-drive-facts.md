# Hobart to Launceston drive: facts (slug hobart-to-launceston-drive)

Researched 2026-10-21. OSRM = "OSRM routing on OpenStreetMap data, computed 21 October 2026" (car, no stops, centre to centre). Whole route is in Tasmania. Say "about" on every distance.

## 1. Route options
1. **Midland Highway (A1 / National Highway 1) via Brighton, Kempton, Oatlands, Ross, Campbell Town, Epping Forest, Perth**
   - Wikipedia: 181 km between Hobart and Launceston; mostly two lanes, one each way, with overtaking options and at-grade intersections, "small portions of grade-separated dual carriageway" at both city ends. Called the Midlands Highway; labelled "The Heritage Highway" in the 2000s.
   - OSRM centre to centre: 201 km, 2 h 41 min. Legs: Hobart-Kempton 49 km 42 min; Kempton-Oatlands 35 km 26 min; Oatlands-Ross 37 km 28 min; Ross-Campbell Town 12 km 11 min; Campbell Town-Epping Forest 23 km 18 min; Epping Forest-Launceston 46 km 34 min.
2. **Via Swansea and Lake Leake Highway (B34)**: Tasman Highway to Swansea, then Lake Leake Highway to Campbell Town, then Midland Highway. OSRM: 272 km, 3 h 38 min (Hobart-Swansea 134 km 1 h 50; Swansea-Campbell Town 69 km 54 min; Campbell Town-Launceston 69 km 53 min). Wikipedia (Midland Highway article): Lake Leake Highway meets the Midland Highway near Campbell Town. Road conditions on Lake Leake Highway: NOT CONFIRMED.
3. **East coast via Swansea, Bicheno, St Marys, Esk Highway (A4)**: OSRM 353 km, 4 h 53 min via Swansea, Bicheno, St Marys, Fingal, Conara. Wikipedia: Esk Highway A4 82 km, joins Midland Highway at Conara Junction just north of Campbell Town, passes Avoca, Fingal and St Marys, climbs curvy 6 km St Marys Pass, ends at Tasman Highway near Falmouth. Not a direct route; a scenic detour.
- Hobart to Launceston default: Midland Highway.

## 2. Overnight / stops (no AussieCamps campground within 12 km of route)
- Corridor (12 km): campgrounds 0; dump point: Brighton Showground, Pontville (AU19116, 29 km from Hobart, free, toilets, accessible toilet, sharps disposal). Rest areas: St Peters Pass, Oatlands (AU06034, 92 km, toilets, picnic tables, barbecues); Campbell Town Weighbridge rest area (AU06029, 138 km, heavy vehicle area); Conara (AU06037, 145 km, picnic tables, barbecues). Two Near Kingston rest areas (AU05986, AU06065) are 7 to 8 km off at the Hobart end: not used.
- Kempton (Southern Midlands Council, southernmidlands.tas.gov.au/stopover-dump-points): Kempton Roadside Stopover near Station Park, off Main Street: free with donation suggested, 48 hours max, self-contained vehicles only. Dump point Old Hunting Ground Road behind Victoria Hall, 24 hours, free, water, cassette toilets only, hard for large motorhomes to reverse into.
- Oatlands: Oatlands Lakeside Stopover off High Street on the Esplanade, northern end, Lake Dulverton foreshore: free, donation suggested, 72 hours max; picnic table, fresh water, fire pot. RV dump point Williams Street at Wellington Street junction: public, 24 hours, free, water, "size ok for greater than 20 metres".
- Ross: Ross Caravan Park listed on Northern Midlands Council "Caravan Parks and Camping Grounds 2023/2024" fee schedule: "Fees set by Lessee", info@rosscaravanpark.com.au or 0437 425 832. Address 1 Esplanade (search extract, directories). Facilities/rates NOT CONFIRMED beyond that.
- Campbell Town (Northern Midlands Council, nmc.tas.gov.au/community/facilities/rv-friendly-towns): free 48-hour area for self-contained vehicles in Blackburn Park Reserve on the south-eastern side of the Red Bridge, beside the Elizabeth River; permit needed (free up to 48 hours; online form 24/7 or call (03) 6397 7303 in business hours); display permit number; showering, washing, toilet use, cooking and sleeping must be inside the vehicle; no liquid waste on the ground, near trees or into the river, fine up to $205 per unit under the Litter Act 2007; "This is not a booking system you cannot reserve a place." Council page lists no dump point for Campbell Town. Spirit of Tasmania listing (runs to 6 Oct 2027): RV Dump Point at King Street Oval, Campbell Town, free, potable and rinse water, "Big Rig friendly", hosted by Northern Midlands Council.
- Lake Leake campground fees on 2023/24 council schedule: left out (old).

## 4. Road conditions
- State Growth Midland Highway 10 Year Action Plan page (transport.tas.gov.au) returned 403 to scripts; used search extracts and news. Search extract of Tasmanian Government release 5 September 2026 (premier.tas.gov.au, 403): final stage Campbell Town North to Conara, 10 km: environmental approvals received; contract expected in the coming weeks; work to start late 2026 and run until late 2028; road widening, turning improvements, sealed shoulders, overtaking lanes in both directions, flexible safety barriers; $605 million joint commitment. A 2024 release said about 153 km plan, 10 km remaining.
- Search extract of Australian Government ministerial release: safety upgrades at the Midland Highway and Lake Leake Road intersection in Campbell Town to start Monday 21 September 2026, 7am to 6pm Monday to Friday, lane closures and reduced speed limit during working hours, completion expected by end of year weather permitting. Whether it started on time: NOT CONFIRMED.
- Pulse Tasmania (news, 26 May 2024): 110 km/h limits returned between Conara and Epping Forest after upgrade. Current limits: NOT CONFIRMED; say obey signs.
- RSAC (rsac.tas.gov.au/visitingdrivers/new-to-tasmania and international-tourists): default limits 50 km/h urban, 100 km/h rural, 80 km/h gravel unless signposted; "Slow down and LOOK-OUT for animals on the road, especially at dawn and dusk"; "wildlife and roadkill, especially at night and early morning"; "Travelling 150 km may take 2 hours"; "Plan no more than 300 km per day and take frequent breaks"; "Always drive on the LEFT"; "pull over in a safe place to allow others to pass from behind"; "SLOW DOWN on winding, gravel or icy roads in bad weather"; "black ice and moss on the road, especially in shady areas"; "Fog, snow and ice can also be experienced in some areas"; "Always check the weather forecast before setting out".
- Ice or snow on the Midland Highway per Department of State Growth: NOT CONFIRMED. Tasmania Police (2018 release, Midlands and Central Highlands, roads not named): avoid non-essential travel in snow affected areas; 4WD and snow chains; police do not do recoveries; check Tasmania Police Community Alerts (police.tas.gov.au/community-alerts) for closures.

## 5. Biosecurity (Biosecurity Tasmania food page, nre.tas.gov.au)
- Not permitted: "Fresh fruit and vegetables including potatoes, fresh herbs, garlic, ginger, onion." Permitted: "Cooked, baked, hard frozen, canned, preserved, commercially dried, freeze dried, home dehydrated after cooking." Fresh and processed plant foods must be declared on arrival and inspected (page). Page has no fines text and no separate mainland rule. Contact 1300 368 550. Spirit of Tasmania arrivals: dispose of food onboard or at the Biosecurity Inspection point.
- The rule applies on arrival; no checks between Hobart and Launceston found: NOT CONFIRMED either way, say only what the page says.
