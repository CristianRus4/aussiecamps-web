## 1. Route options

**A. Inland: Hume Highway to Tarcutta/Wagga, Sturt Highway via Narrandera, Hay, Balranald, Mildura, Renmark, Gawler**
- OSRM: 1,457 km, 17h58 (waypoints Goulburn, Gundagai, Wagga, Narrandera, Hay, Balranald, Mildura, Renmark, Waikerie). [1]
- Sturt Highway alone: 947 km, Tarcutta (Hume Highway junction) to Gawler SA; about 596 km NSW, 117 km Vic, 234 km SA (Wikipedia, derived from km markers). [2]
- Wikipedia calls it "the shortest, highest-standard route between Sydney and Adelaide". Most of it is single carriageway; four or six lanes near Gawler. [2] (Wikipedia, not a road authority.)
- Towns: Goulburn, Gundagai, Wagga Wagga, Narrandera, Darlington Point, Hay, Balranald, Euston, Mildura/Buronga, Renmark, Berri, Waikerie, Gawler. [1][2]
- Road type: Hume Highway is 110 km/h and dual carriageway for its full length (Wikipedia: duplication completed 2013). [3]

**B. Via Melbourne: Hume Highway to Melbourne, then Melbourne to Adelaide**
- OSRM: Sydney to Melbourne 899 km, 11h12 (via Goulburn, Wagga, Albury); Melbourne to Adelaide 726 km, 9h14. Total 1,625 km, 20h26. [1]
- Hume Highway Sydney to Melbourne: 840 km (Wikipedia). [3] OSRM is 59 km longer because it runs city centre to city centre.
- Melbourne to Adelaide coastal option is covered in melbourne-to-adelaide-coastal-route.

**C. Barrier Highway: Great Western/Mitchell Highways to Dubbo, Nyngan, Cobar, Wilcannia, Broken Hill, Yunta, Burra**
- OSRM: 1,797 km, 23h40. DO NOT USE AS A HEADLINE FIGURE: OSRM's Nyngan to Cobar leg is 204 km, but Rita's Outback Guide gives 132 km and Wikipedia's chainage gives about 130 km. OSRM looks inflated by roughly 70 km on that leg. [1][4][5]
- Barrier Highway alone: 1,012 km, Nyngan to Giles Corner SA (Wikipedia, infobox cites Google Maps). Chainage west to east: Giles Corner 0, Burra 71.6, Yunta 227, SA/NSW border 377, Broken Hill about 424, Wilcannia about 620 to 641, Cobar 882, Nyngan 1,011. [5]
- Official-style leg figures (Rita's Outback Guide): Nyngan to Cobar 132 km, Cobar to Wilcannia 261 km, Wilcannia to Broken Hill 198 km, Broken Hill to Yunta 195 km, Yunta to Burra 159 km. [4]
- Wikipedia: "The area traversed by the highway is remote and very sparsely settled." Fully sealed in October 1972. [5]
- Sydney to Dubbo OSRM 398 km, 5h57; Dubbo to Nyngan 165 km, 2h02. [1]

## 2. Legs and overnight stops (route A, OSRM figures; no stops)
- Day 1: Sydney to Wagga Wagga, 457 km, 5h33 (194 to Goulburn, 181 to Gundagai, 82 to Wagga). [1]
- Day 2: Wagga Wagga to Hay, 308 km, 3h54 (106 to Narrandera, 202 on to Hay). [1]
- Day 3: Hay to Mildura/Buronga, 292 km, 3h31 (132 to Balranald, 160 to Mildura). [1]
- Day 4: Mildura to Adelaide, 401 km, 4h56 (141 to Renmark, 79 to Waikerie, 181 to Adelaide). [1]

**Wagga Wagga**
- Caravan park: BIG4 Wagga Wagga Holiday Park (listing on big4.com.au, page returned 200; no operator detail checked). [6]
- Wagga Beach Caravan Park: a 2019 local-history note said the lease would end in 2019; current status NOT CONFIRMED.
- Town: Riverina city on the Murrumbidgee; Riverside Wagga Beach is a council precinct with playground, barbecues and river access (council page, search extract). [7]

**Narrandera (break or overnight)**
- Rest area (break): Narrandera Visitors Centre, AU09338: toilets, picnic tables, barbecues, heavy vehicle area, accessible toilet, playground. Data lists it as a rest area; it is not a place to sleep unless signed (see overnight rules guide).
- Campground (data): Sandy Beach campground, Cudgel, AU00314 (toilets, flush toilets, non-flush toilets, boat ramp, phone reception; no fee listed).

**Hay**
- Hay Caravan Park, Sturt Highway, Hay NSW 2711, ph 02 6993 1415, haycp.com.au (Hay Shire Council directory; site returned 200). Grassed drive-through powered and unpowered sites, ensuite sites and cabins; about 1 km east of the Sturt/Cobb Highway junction (search extract). [8]
- Also on the council directory: Hay Plains Holiday Park (BIG4), 4 Nailor Street; Hay Showground Caravan and Camping Ground, 73 Showground Road (search extract). [8]
- Campground (data): Wooloondool campground, Hay, AU00387 (boat ramp, phone reception).
- Town: Riverina town on the Murrumbidgee, where the Sturt and Cobb Highways meet.

**Balranald (break or overnight)**
- Rest area (break): Balranald Lions Park, AU09270: toilets, shelter, picnic tables, barbecues, heavy vehicle area, playground.

**Mildura / Buronga (Vic/NSW border)**
- Discovery Parks Mildura, Buronga Riverside, 71A Caravan Park Road, Buronga NSW (operator page returned 200). Cabins, powered and unpowered sites, dump point, pool, camp kitchen. Search extract: it reopened after 2022 flood recovery, bookings open from December 2024; total campfire ban noted during fire danger season. [9]

**Renmark (SA Riverland)**
- Discovery Parks Renmark Riverfront, 1 Patey Drive, Renmark (operator page returned 200). Search extract: nearly 1 km of Murray frontage, powered sites, cabins, no dogs. [10]
- Campgrounds (data) in the Riverland: Loch Luna AU04420, Kaiser Strip AU04419, Moorook AU04421 (no facilities listed).

**Route C overnight towns** (Dubbo, Cobar, Broken Hill)
- Cobar Caravan Park, 101 Marshall Street, Cobar NSW 2835: listed on Cobar Shire Council's directory (page 200). Search extract: over 100 powered sites, two amenities blocks, camp kitchen, cabins. Council and tourism pages show different phone numbers (02 6836 5888 and 02 6836 2425); do not print a number. [11]
- Broken Hill Tourist Park (G'Day Parks listing, page 200): cabins, powered, ensuite and drive-through sites, dump point, pool. [12]
- Rest areas (data): Lithgow Endeavor Park AU10032 (toilets, picnic tables, barbecues, heavy vehicle area, accessible toilet, playground); Dubbo Tourist Information Centre AU09413 (same list); MacCullochs Rest Area Westbound, Central Darling, AU07284 (toilets, picnic tables, barbecues, heavy vehicle area, accessible toilet, playground).
- Campgrounds (data) near the route: Lane Cove caravan park AU00221 (Sydney end, park entry fee); Wheeny Creek AU00004; The Diggings, Capertee AU00341; Woolshed Flat AU00390. Data has none between Dubbo and Burra.

**Overnight rest area rules**: NSW sources conflict (Service NSW and Transport for NSW say not for camping; a July 2026 planning practice note says it may be permitted unless a no camping sign is up). SA and Vic have no statewide limit found. See can-i-stay-overnight-at-a-rest-area-in-australia. Treat every rest area above as a break unless the sign says otherwise.

## 3. Fuel and services
- Route C, Cobar to Wilcannia (261 km, Rita's): Emmdale Roadhouse is described as "the only place to get fuel" on that stretch. Position not stated there; a cycling blog puts it about 160 km past Cobar and about 100 km before Wilcannia (blog, not official). A 2019 Isuzu truck-stop guide gave hours 7 am to 8 pm daily. [4][13] Hours are old: ring ahead.
- Route C, Wilcannia to Broken Hill (198 km): Little Topar Roadhouse is "the only place to offer fuel, drinks and food" (Rita's). Wikipedia says "The roadhouse has closed its doors January 28" (year not stated; a reference URL suggests January 2024) and a Facebook page still showed 24/7 fuel. Status NOT CONFIRMED. [4][14]
- Route C elsewhere (Rita's): Nyngan and Cobar have fuel; Hermidale, 46 km east of Nyngan, has fuel; Cockburn, 50 km past Broken Hill, has fuel at Border Gate Truck Stop; Yunta has "two fuel service stations" (page undated); fuel is not available in every small locality near Peterborough. [4]
- Route A: longest town-to-town legs in OSRM are Narrandera to Hay 202 km and Balranald to Mildura 160 km. Which of Darlington Point, Carrathool, Oxley or Euston sell fuel is NOT CONFIRMED.
- NSW National Parks (outback safety): food, water and fuel supplies can be scarce in the outback; Mutawintji National Park, 75 km from Broken Hill via the Barrier Highway, has no fuel or food (search extract). [15]
- General fuel planning: road-trip-fuel-planning-australia.

## 4. Road conditions and seasonal closures
- NSW: Live Traffic NSW, livetraffic.com (returned 200). Balranald Shire's road report (28 August 2026) lists local roads only and sends drivers to livetraffic.com. [16]
- SA: DIT Outback Roads closures page, dit.sa.gov.au/OutbackRoads/outback-road-closures, phone 1300 361 033 (search extract; the page returned 403 to scripts). Closed areas are restricted to residents, landowners, accredited media and relief services; the page says not to try to travel through affected areas. [17]
- Vic: VicTraffic (URL not opened).
- Flooding: a Riverina story said the Sturt Highway west of Hay closed to light vehicles when the Murrumbidgee spilled onto the road. It is undated; NOT CONFIRMED as current. [18]
- Heat: NSW National Parks says Outback NSW is a region of extreme temperatures, reaching up to 50 C in some places, and that autumn and spring are the best times for long drives; stay with your vehicle if it breaks down; service the vehicle before the journey (search extract). [15] Transport for NSW warns drivers in heatwaves to allow extra time because high temperatures often cause more breakdowns (news report). [19]
- Tolls leaving Sydney: M5 and M7 are tolled (Wikipedia). Linkt: without an account you can buy a pass within 5 days of your first trip on Sydney or Queensland toll roads; a Linkt account or Sydney Pass covers all toll roads in Australia. Unpaid trips bring a notice plus admin fee to the registered owner. [3][20]
- Speed: Hume Highway 110 km/h full length (Wikipedia). [3] Road trains: driving-with-road-trains-australia.
- Announced roadworks: not researched beyond SA DIT notices for Manoora/Saddleworth (Barrier Highway, 2025 notices) and a May 2026 DIT notice for Barrier Highway rest areas and culvert upgrades; contents not opened. NOT CONFIRMED.

## 5. State border rules
**South Australia (all routes entering SA)**
- PIRSA fruit fly site: "There are permanent restrictions on bringing host produce into South Australia and the Riverland Pest Free Area, as well as restrictions on moving fruit and vegetables within declared fruit fly outbreak areas." (fruitfly.sa.gov.au homepage, Wayback copy 16 Oct 2026.) [21]
- PIRSA, 1 April 2026: "Quarantine bins are available at key entry points into South Australia and the Riverland. If travellers are carrying any restricted items, they should either eat them beforehand or dispose of them in one of these bins." "We will also have random roadblocks in place, with quarantine officers checking vehicles for fresh fruit and vegetables. Travellers found carrying restricted items may face a $420 fine." Restricted produce can move if "canned, cooked, dried, frozen, grated, or pureed". [22]
- Fines increased from 1 July 2026 "as part of the annual CPI revi[ew]" (PIRSA news headline text, truncated). The new amount was not found; do not print $420 as current. [21]
- Interstate Quarantine site: for the Riverland, "Do not take fresh, unprocessed fruit or fruiting vegetables"; zero tolerance, fines at the Yamba Quarantine Station or roadblocks. Page undated, copyright 2026. [23]
- Search extract: bins at Bede Road near Yamba and Old Wentworth Road at the border. Which bins serve the Barrier Highway entry (near Cockburn) NOT CONFIRMED.
- Outbreaks: PIRSA 1 April 2026 listed Riverland end date 25 November 2026 (as of 30 March 2026); a PIRSA detections page of 15 to 17 June 2026 (search extract) listed 23 December 2026 and said no detections in 22 of 58 outbreak areas over 12 weeks. 12 October 2026 news item: Pick and Collect trial in high-risk Riverland areas. Current end date and boundaries NOT CONFIRMED; send readers to the PIRSA outbreak map and Fruit Fly Hotline 1300 666 010. [21][22][24]
- A January 2026 PIRSA page listed "Lindsay Point (Victoria)" among Riverland outbreaks. [25]
- Restrictions apply in outbreak areas "regardless of their source" (PIRSA homepage text). [21]

**Victoria**
- Agriculture Victoria (search extract): the Greater Sunraysia Pest Free Area regulation lapsed in July 2024; restrictions on moving host produce into or out of Sunraysia were removed; Mediterranean fruit fly restrictions still apply statewide; two control orders in north-west Victoria remain to help SA keep Riverland status. The page returned 403. [26]
- Interstate Quarantine site lists no fruit fly zone for Victoria or NSW. [23]

**NSW**: a NSW DPI fact sheet (search extract, undated) lists a Control Zone for the NSW portion of the Greater Sunraysia Pest Free Area; whether it is in force NOT CONFIRMED.

## 7. Other facts a driver needs
- Daylight saving began Sunday 4 October 2026 at 2am AEST in NSW, Vic, SA, Tas, ACT and ends Sunday 4 April 2027 at 3am AEDT (NSW Government). [27] Wentworth Council gives the same 4 October date and the same states. [28]
- Broken Hill is in NSW but uses Australian Central time (UTC+9:30 standard, UTC+10:30 summer), so it is half an hour behind Sydney now; it follows South Australia's daylight saving (Wikipedia, timeanddate-style summaries, search extract). [29] Which town west of Cobar changes clock: NOT CONFIRMED; only say Broken Hill.
- Route A: Victoria (Mildura) is on eastern time (AEDT, UTC+11 in summer) and South Australia on central time (ACDT, UTC+10:30), so clocks go back 30 minutes crossing from Victoria into South Australia near Renmark while daylight saving runs. This is arithmetic from the two zones (the Broken Hill source above gives the central offsets), not a quoted source.
- Fees: no national park fee on the main route confirmed. Mutawintji National Park (side trip from Broken Hill): no fuel or food on site. [15]
