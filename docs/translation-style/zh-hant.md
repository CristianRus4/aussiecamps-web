# Traditional Chinese (zh-hant) style guide: AussieCamps and KiwiCamping

This guide applies to every zh-hant unit on both sites (`lib/translations/zh-hant.json`). Both repos hold an identical copy. Engine locale id: `zh-Hant` (Taiwan). Every translator, subagent and reviewer follows it.

## 1. Reader and market

- **Taiwan first.** Taiwan is a large working-holiday (打工度假) source for both Australia and New Zealand, and Taiwanese road-trippers rent campervans there every summer. Write Taiwan Traditional Chinese with Taiwan vocabulary (資訊, 網路, 軟體, 影片, 品質, 預設, 搜尋, 儲存, 設定).
- **Hong Kong readers too.** They read Taiwan-style Traditional Chinese without trouble. Do not mix in Cantonese or HK-only words (山火, 迴旋處, 行山, 雪櫃, 燒烤 for BBQ, 悉尼, 珀斯, 新西蘭).
- **Never character-convert Simplified Chinese.** Zero Simplified-only characters, and no mainland vocabulary (信息, 網絡, 軟件, 視頻, 質量, 默認, 屏幕, 房車, 自駕游, 野營, 服務區, 越野車, 生物安保).
- **Who reads us:** working-holiday makers aged 18 to 35 (on a budget, often buying or renting a cheap campervan, collecting 二簽/三簽 days), couples and families on a 2 to 4 week campervan road trip, and hikers heading for DOC huts and Great Walks.

## 2. Voice

Write like an experienced Taiwanese travel writer who has driven these roads (過來人), writing for a friend who is about to go. Calm, practical, specific.

- **Address the reader as 你**, never 您. Taiwanese travel blogs and Tourism Australia's own Traditional Chinese body copy use 你; 您 reads like a bank or a government form. Use 大家 sparingly, only where a group is meant. (The engine has no zh register setting; this guide sets it.)
- **Short to medium sentences.** One idea per sentence. Split long English sentences. Chinese runs on commas easily; stop with 。 when the thought ends.
- **Lead with what the reader does.** 「出發前先查好…」「記得…」「建議…」「千萬別…」「最好…」「其實…」 are natural travel-writer moves. Use them, but not in every sentence.
- **Be direct about rules and risks** without sounding frightened or preachy: 「被抓到會罰款」 not 「將會面臨罰款之處分」.
- **No hype.** No 最強, 必備神器, 史上最, 超級, 一定要下載 unless the English says something that strong. No emoji, no 「XD」, no exclamation marks unless the English has one.
- **Translate meaning, not word order.** Within a paragraph you may reorder, split or merge sentences, but keep every fact, number, condition and caveat.
- **Keep the structure.** Same number of sections, paragraphs, tips, FAQ items, sources and price rows as English. The site's completeness gate counts them.

**English in parentheses.** Taiwanese travel writers routinely add the English after a local term the first time, because travellers need it for signs, booking sites and maps: 「有電營位（powered site）」「國際駕照（IDP）」. Do this **once per page, at the first mention in the body** (not in titles), for the terms marked ✱ below and for place names a reader will need to find on a sign. Never repeat the parenthesis in every paragraph.

## 5. Conventions

- **Punctuation:** full-width ，。、；：？！（） in Chinese text. 「」 for quotes and UI names, 『』 inside 「」. 、 for list items. Never ASCII , . : ; ( ) inside Chinese sentences. Use 「～」 or 「至」 for ranges in prose (「11 月至隔年 4 月」), never a dash.
- **No dashes at all.** No em dash (U+2014), no doubled Chinese 破折號, no en dash (U+2013) for ranges. Rewrite with ，、： or 至/到. The content audit rejects em dashes.
- **Spacing:** one half-width space between Chinese and Latin letters or Arabic numerals (「用 AussieCamps 規劃」「超過 74,000 個地點」「DOC 營地」); no space next to full-width punctuation (「（DOC）」「AUD）」).
- **Numbers:** Arabic numerals, comma thousands separator as in English (74,000, 6,500). Write source words as numerals where natural (fifty metres → 50 公尺). 萬 only where the English is approximate prose, never for headline counts.
- **Counts and claims:** keep 74,000+ / 6,500+ / 4,000+ / 2,000+ exactly: 「74,000 多個地點」「超過 6,500 個地點」.
- **Currency:** amounts stay in AUD / NZD exactly as the English writes them. Keep the $ figure and add 澳幣 / 紐幣 after it where the currency is not already clear: 「每晚 $30 澳幣」「$80 至 $130 澳幣」「一個人 $159 紐幣」 [TW-blog Christy: 「一個人 $159 紐幣」]. Keep A$ / NZ$ / AUD / NZD tokens when the English uses them (「每公升 A$2.08」「AUD」). Never convert to 新台幣. Never 澳元/紐元 in body copy (official-sounding; bloggers say 澳幣/紐幣).
- **Units:** 公里, 公尺, 公升, 公斤, 攝氏 / °C, 公里/小時 or 時速 100 公里. Labels in the tools may keep the unit symbol: 「距離（km）」「油耗（L/100 km）」.
- **Dates and times:** 2026 年 8 月 12 日; 11 月到隔年 4 月; 晚上 10 點到早上 7 點; 上午 10 點前退營. Weekdays 週一…週日.
- **Titles and H1:** lead with the term Taiwanese search: 澳洲露營 / 澳洲自駕 / 紐西蘭露營車 / 紐西蘭自由露營 / 昆士蘭露營規定 / DOC 山屋. About 30 characters or fewer. Exception to the no-English-in-titles rule: where Taiwanese travellers search the English word itself (Dump Point / Dump Station, Holiday Park), the title may carry it once next to the Chinese term: 「澳洲 Dump Point 排污站：…」. Use 「：」 between the main term and the angle. No 【】 or ｜ stacks (blog style, but too noisy for this site).
- **Meta descriptions:** 60 to 80 Chinese characters, one or two sentences, no keyword stuffing.
- **Headings:** short noun phrases or imperative phrases; no full stop.
- **Tips (short bullets):** 6 to 15 characters, no full stop, imperative or noun phrase: 「先訂好營位」「河口鱷出沒，遠離水邊」.
- **FAQ:** natural spoken questions as Taiwanese would type them: 「在昆士蘭的國家公園露營需要許可證嗎？」 Answers start with the answer: 「需要。…」.
- **Source labels:** keep the publisher's English name, add a short Chinese description: 「Numbeo：雪梨飲料價格」.
- **Never changes:** AussieCamps, KiwiCamping, App Store, iPhone, iOS, iCloud, Apple, MapKit, AUD, NZD, DOC, URLs, {placeholders}, HTML markup such as <a data-guide="slug">…</a> (translate only the link text), brand names (BookEasy, BookIt, Woolworths, Coles, BP, Z, Jucy, Top 10 Holiday Parks, Numbeo).

## 6. Facts that must survive

- **No offline map, in any wording.** The apps are not offline maps. Place details and guides work offline; map tiles, routing, weather and photos need signal. Allowed: 「沒有訊號也能查看地點資訊」「地點資料內建在 App 裡，離線也能打開」. Banned: 離線地圖, 離線導航, 「地圖離線也能用」. Where the English gives general advice to "download offline maps" for navigation, write 「出發前先把整段路線的地圖下載到手機」 and 「開飛航模式測試下載好的地圖能不能用」.
- **The Pass boundary.** Do not promise free access to anything the English does not call free.
- **Testimonials (ui.review*, reviewsEyebrow, reviewsTitle) are never translated** for zh-hant.
- **No added superlatives, counts or claims.** 「最好的露營 App」 only where the English says "best".

## 8. Before and after

zh-hant is a new locale, so these contrast a literal machine-style rendering of the current English with the natural version.

1. Source: "Queensland protected-area camping usually starts with a permit. Book before arrival and carry the permit details beyond phone coverage."
   - Literal: 昆士蘭保護區露營通常以許可證開始。在到達之前進行預訂，並在手機覆蓋範圍之外攜帶許可證詳細資訊。
   - Natural: 在昆士蘭的保護區露營，第一步通常是申請許可證。抵達前就要訂好，許可證資料也要另外存一份，開到沒訊號的地方才拿得出來。
2. Source: "Do not rely on the absence of a no-camping sign. Confirm the land manager or council rule when the purpose of the area is unclear."
   - Literal: 不要依賴禁止露營標誌的缺席。當該區域的目的不清楚時，請確認土地管理者或議會的規則。
   - Natural: 沒看到「禁止露營」的牌子，不代表就能過夜。搞不清楚這塊地是做什麼用的，先查清楚土地管理單位或地方政府的規定。
3. Source: "74,000+ places across Australia, bundled offline so they still open with no signal."
   - Literal: 澳大利亞各地 74,000+ 個地點，離線捆綁，因此在沒有信號時仍然打開。
   - Natural: 澳洲各地 74,000 多個地點資料都內建在 App 裡，沒有訊號也照樣打得開。

## 9. Checklist before a unit is final

1. Would a Taiwanese reader notice this was translated? If yes, rewrite from the meaning.
2. Glossary terms used; same term for the same thing across the page and both sites.
3. Every number, $ amount, unit, date, brand, link and markup tag from the English is present.
4. Same count of sections, paragraphs, tips, FAQ items, sources and price rows.
5. Full-width punctuation, 「」 quotes, half-width space between Chinese and Latin or digits, no dashes.
6. No Simplified characters, no mainland or HK-only vocabulary, no 您.
7. No offline-map wording; no claims the English does not make.
